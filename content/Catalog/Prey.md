## Highlights

Neuromods, fabricators, recyclers and the GLOO cannon.

## The setting

*Prey* (2017): on the space station Talos I, the TranStar corporation studies the **Typhon**, a shapeshifting alien species, and uses their biology to make **Neuromods**: injections that rewrite the brain to grant skills instantly. Then the Typhon get loose.

## Signature tech

- **Neuromods:** instant skills (and, with Typhon mods, alien powers). Removing one erases the memories since.
- **Fabricators** and **recyclers:** break anything into base materials and print from blueprints.
- **GLOO cannon:** fires fast-hardening foam for climbing, sealing and freezing enemies.
- **Looking Glass** screens and **Operators** (drone assistants).

## Materials

Typhon-based neuromods need Typhon: **hazardous**. Human-skill neuromods are fine.

## Risks

Typhon mods turn people into something else. Typhon mimics are, very specifically, [[Mimics]].
