## Highlights

Digital life, Digivices, and the Digital World.

## The setting

*Digimon*: Digital Monsters live in a Digital World built from the data of our networks. Human partners ("DigiDestined", "Tamers") carry Digivices that let their Digimon digivolve into stronger forms in a crisis.

## Signature tech

- **Digivices:** partner links, digivolution triggers, and gates to the Digital World.
- **Digi-eggs and Digi-Cores:** digital life, hatched from data.
- **Crests and D-Arks:** tools that focus a partner bond into power.
- **Digital gates** between the real and digital worlds.

## Materials

Data and computing power. The Digital World itself needs somewhere to exist: **Gate salvage** could anchor one.

## Risks

Creating new, self-aware life. Digimon grow, and some grow into gods.
