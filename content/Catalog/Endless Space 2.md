## Highlights

Dust: the remains of the Endless, a substance that can do anything.

## The setting

*Endless Space 2*: galactic empires expand across a galaxy littered with ruins of the **Endless**, a precursor race who turned themselves into Dust. Dust is currency, power source and magic.

## Signature tech

- **Dust:** a precursor substance that powers anything and bends physics.
- **Faction tech:** Vodyani ark-ships, Riftborn temporal beings, Unfallen tree-vines, Cravers' hive industry.
- **Warp and wormhole travel.**

## Materials

Dust: **Shard Dungeon elements** or **Endbringer tissue** as the closest analogues.

## Risks
