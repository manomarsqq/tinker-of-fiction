## Highlights

Automated production lines, logistics robots and rocket silos.

## The setting

*Factorio*: an engineer crash-lands on an alien planet and builds an ever-growing automated factory to launch a rocket. The locals (the Biters) object to the pollution.

## Signature tech

- **Assemblers and inserters:** automatic crafting.
- **Belts, trains and logistics robots.**
- **Mining drills, furnaces, oil refineries.**
- **Blueprints:** a design replicated by robots.
- **Rocket silos.**

## Materials

Conventional, at scale.

## Risks

Mass production. Pollution.
