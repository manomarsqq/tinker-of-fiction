## Highlights

Transforming Valkyrie fighters, fold drives, and the power of song.

## The setting

*Macross*: after an alien ship crashes on Earth, humanity reverse-engineers **Overtechnology** and builds the SDF-1 Macross. They fight the Zentradi, a giant warrior race, and win with the power of culture and song.

## Signature tech

- **Valkyries (VFs):** variable fighters that transform between fighter, GERWALK and battroid modes.
- **Fold drives:** faster-than-light travel.
- **Macross-class ships** with transforming hulls and main guns.
- **Micronisation:** shrinking and growing people.
- **Fold quartz.**

## Materials

Overtechnology: **Gate salvage**. Fold quartz: **Labyrinthos ore**.

## Risks

Micronisation is power-granting of a kind.
