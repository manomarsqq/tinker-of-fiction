## Highlights

Spy gadgets disguised as compacts, lipsticks and hair dryers.

## The setting

*Totally Spies!*: Sam, Clover and Alex, three Beverly Hills teenagers, secretly work for WOOHP (World Organization of Human Protection). Their boss, Jerry, sends them after villains with gadgets disguised as fashion accessories.

## Signature tech

- **Compowders:** communicators and scanners disguised as makeup compacts.
- **Gadgets:** laser lipstick, hair-dryer vacuums, jetpack backpacks, grappling belts, freezing sprays.
- **WOOHP transport:** being "WOOHPed" out of anywhere, usually through a hole in the floor.
- **WOOHP labs** and vehicles.
