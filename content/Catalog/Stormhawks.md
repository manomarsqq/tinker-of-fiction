## Highlights

Sky vehicles, crystals, and the terras of Atmos.

## The setting

*Storm Hawks*: in Atmos, a world of floating landmasses ("terras") above the Wastelands, a squadron of teenage Sky Knights fight the Cyclonian Empire. Everything runs on **crystals**.

## Signature tech

- **Skimmers** and **heli-scooters:** personal sky vehicles that transform.
- **The Condor:** a carrier airship.
- **Crystals:** each with a different power (fire, wind, lightning, gravity).
- **Crystal-powered weapons.**

## Materials

Crystals: **Labyrinthos ore** or **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

Some crystals are dangerous (Cyclonian storm crystals).
