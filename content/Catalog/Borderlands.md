## Highlights

Digistruct, New-U respawn stations, and a billion guns from rival manufacturers.

## The setting

*Borderlands*: corporate frontier planets (Pandora, Promethea) where manufacturers sell guns to anyone and the Eridians left alien relics behind.

## Signature tech

- **Digistruct:** turn data into matter, and matter into data.
- **New-U stations:** respawn, for a fee.
- **Manufacturer guns:** Hyperion (smart), Atlas (tracking), Jakobs (old-West), Vladof (fast), Dahl (burst), Maliwan (elemental), Tediore (throw-to-reload).
- **Eridian tech** and **Eridium**.

## Materials

**Eridium**: **Endbringer tissue** or **Labyrinthos ore**.

## Risks

Eridium.
