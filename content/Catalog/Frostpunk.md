## Highlights

Steam, heat, and the generator that keeps a city alive at the end of the world.

## The setting

*Frostpunk*: a volcanic winter freezes the 19th-century world. Survivors build a city around a colossal coal-fired **generator** in a crater, and the captain must choose which laws keep them alive: child labour, extended shifts, faith or order.

## Signature tech

- **The Generator:** a city-heating steam engine with overdrive.
- **Steam hubs** and heaters that extend the heat radius.
- **Automatons:** steam-powered walking labourers that work in any cold.
- **Steam cores:** irreplaceable power units.
- **Airships and scout sledges.**

## Materials

Coal, steel and steam: conventional. Steam cores are the rare part; **Labyrinthos ore** could stand in.

## Risks

Low tech-wise. The lesson of Frostpunk is what people do to each other to survive.
