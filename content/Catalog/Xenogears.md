## Highlights

Gears, nanomachines, Solaris, and the Zohar.

## The setting

*Xenogears*: on a world ruled from the hidden floating city **Solaris**, people fight in giant mechs called **Gears**. Fei Fong Wong discovers the truth: humanity was made by the **Zohar**, and the Emperor's nanomachines (the Emeralda colony) and an ancient bio-weapon (Deus) are waiting.

## Signature tech

- **Gears:** humanoid mechs, and Omnigears.
- **Nanomachines:** the Emeralda colony, a living nanomachine being.
- **Solaris's biotech:** the limiter, Soylent System.
- **The Zohar:** an infinite energy source.
