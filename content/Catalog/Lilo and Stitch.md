## Highlights

Jumba's genetic experiments: 626 of them, each built for one kind of chaos.

## The setting

*Lilo & Stitch*: Dr Jumba Jookiba, an "evil genius" of the United Galactic Federation, creates Experiment 626 (Stitch), a nearly indestructible creature designed to destroy. Stitch crash-lands in Hawaii and becomes part of Lilo's family. The series follows the other 625 experiments.

## Signature tech

- **Genetic experiments:** each designed with a specific power (Sparky: electricity; Reuben: sandwiches; Angel: hypnotic song; Leroy: an evil clone army).
- **Experiment pods:** dehydrated experiments activated by water.
- **Plasma blasters** and Federation ships.
- **Containment cells.**

## Materials

Alien genetics: **Gate salvage** or megafauna tissue.

## Risks

Creating life designed for destruction. Leroy can mass-produce.
