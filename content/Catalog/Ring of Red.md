## Highlights

Armoured Fighting Walkers: crewed walking tanks in an alternate post-war Japan.

## The setting

*Ring of Red*: in an alternate 1960s, Japan was split into a Soviet-backed North and a US-backed South after World War II. The war goes on with **Armoured Fighting Walkers (AFWs)**, crewed by a pilot and infantry teams.

## Signature tech

- **AFWs:** diesel-driven walking tanks with cannons, needing crew to load and aim.
- **Infantry support teams** that change what the walker can do.
- **Special ammunition** (armour-piercing, incendiary).
