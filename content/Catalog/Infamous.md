## The setting

*inFAMOUS*: a Ray Sphere detonates in Empire City and activates Cole MacGrath as a Conduit, gaining electrical powers. The man behind it, Kessler, is Cole from a future where the Beast destroyed everything.

## Signature tech

- **The Ray Sphere:** activates Conduits, killing everyone nearby to do it.
- **Blast Shards:** fragments that boost Conduit power.
- **Ray Field Inhibitor.**
- **Conduit genes.**

## Materials
