## Highlights

Compound V: the chemical that makes supes, and the corporation that sells them.

## The setting

*The Boys* (comic and series): **Vought International** makes superheroes with **Compound V**, injected into infants, and markets them as heroes who were born with gifts. The Seven are its flagship team; Homelander is its god. The Boys, a CIA-backed team of damaged people, fight them with blackmail, violence, and a dose of V of their own.

## Signature tech

- **Compound V:** a serum that grants random powers. Most reliable when given to infants; adults usually die or get something ugly.
- **Temp V:** a temporary version (series).
- **V52 / V1:** refined versions for adults and targeted powers.
- **Supe-killing tech:** Novichok, Soldier Boy's radiation, the virus that kills supes.
- **Vought's marketing machine.**

## Materials

Chemistry. The original formula came from Vought's 1940s research; the refined versions need modern biochemistry and **Lost Island flora** for exotic compounds.

## Risks

Power-granting on an industrial scale. Random outcomes. Deaths. And Vought.

## On Earth Bet

**In the world:** [[Vought International]] exists and Compound V is its secret. The MC's power could explain, reproduce, or expose it. The single biggest secret-detonator on the Catalog. *(proposed hook)*

Leet has read the book. He's one of maybe a dozen people on Earth who knows Compound V exists, and the only one outside Vought who could make it.

## Uses for Leet

- **Compound V**: allowed, very carefully. Making supes puts him in competition with Vought, Cauldron and All For One at once.
- **Supe-killing tech**: the counter to [[Homelander]].
- **Knowledge**: exposing Vought would detonate the world's biggest secret.
