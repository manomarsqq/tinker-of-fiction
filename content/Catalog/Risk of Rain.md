## Highlights

Items and artefacts that stack into godhood, on a planet that gets harder every minute.

## The setting

*Risk of Rain*: survivors of the UES Contact Light crash on Petrichor V, a planet that grows more dangerous the longer they stay. They survive by finding **items**, each with a small effect, and stacking them until they can fight gods.

## Signature tech

- **Items:** Soldier's Syringe (attack speed), Hopoo Feathers (extra jumps), Ukulele (chain lightning), Bundle of Fireworks, 57 Leaf Clover, and hundreds more. They stack.
- **Artefacts:** world-rule modifiers.
- **Lunar items:** powerful, with drawbacks.
- **Void items:** corrupted versions.
- **Teleporters.**

## Materials

Lunar and Void items need exotic matter: **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

Stacking breaks physics. Lunar items all carry a cost.
