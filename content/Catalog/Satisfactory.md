## Highlights

Automation, FICSIT, and building a factory across a whole alien planet.

## The setting

*Satisfactory*: as an employee of FICSIT Inc., you land on an alien planet and build a sprawling automated factory for Project Assembly, a mysterious corporate project.

## Signature tech

- **Constructors, assemblers, manufacturers** and conveyors.
- **Nuclear and fuel power plants.**
- **Hypertubes, trains and drones.**
- **Space Elevator.**
- **Somersloops and Mercer Spheres:** alien artefacts.

## Materials

Conventional, at scale. Alien artefacts need **Gate salvage**.

## Risks

Mass production. FICSIT.
