## Highlights

Machines, Cauldrons, terraforming AI, and the Focus.

## The setting

*Horizon Zero Dawn*: a thousand years after the Faro Plague (a self-replicating war-machine swarm that consumed the biosphere), tribes live among animal-shaped machines. The machines are part of **GAIA**, the AI that rebuilt life on Earth.

## Signature tech

- **Machines:** Watchers, Striders, Thunderjaws, Stormbirds. Built in underground **Cauldrons**.
- **The Focus:** an augmented-reality earpiece that scans everything.
- **Overriding:** hacking machines to fight for you.
- **GAIA and her subordinate functions** (HEPHAESTUS, APOLLO, HADES).
- **Zero Dawn:** the terraforming project that reseeds a dead world.

## Materials

Machine parts and blaze: conventional to advanced.

## Risks

The Faro swarm: self-replicating machines that eat biomass. Hazardous.
