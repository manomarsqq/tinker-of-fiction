## Highlights

Arcanotech, D-engines, Engels, and everything the Mythos can do to a mind.

## The setting

*CthulhuTech*: in 2085, humanity (with the alien Nazzadi) fights the Migou, fungi from Yuggoth, while the cults of the Old Ones wait to wake their gods. The New Earth Government's science runs on **arcanotechnology**, built with Mythos physics.

## Signature tech

- **Arcanotechnology:** machines powered by Mythos formulas.
- **D-engines:** dimensional reactors.
- **Mecha:** Engels (bio-organic, grown) and conventional mechs.
- **Tagers:** humans bonded with Mythos symbionts.

## Materials
