## Highlights

Portal guns, gravity guns, HEV suits, and everything Black Mesa and Aperture broke.

## The setting

*Half-Life* and *Portal*: rival research labs. **Black Mesa** causes a resonance cascade that opens a rift to Xen and invites the **Combine** to conquer Earth. **Aperture Science** builds portals, tests them on people, and gets taken over by its own AI, GLaDOS.

## Signature tech

- **Portal gun (ASHPD):** paired portals on any moon-dust surface.
- **Gravity gun (Zero Point Energy Field Manipulator).**
- **HEV suit:** hazard-proof armour.
- **Long-fall boots**, **Repulsion and Propulsion Gel**, **Aperture turrets**.
- **Teleporters** (Black Mesa).
- **AI cores** (GLaDOS, Wheatley).

## Materials

Portals need moon dust in the surface: **Gate salvage** as a substitute. Xen crystals: **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

Resonance cascades. Opening a rift to Xen in a world whose dimensional walls are already failing.
