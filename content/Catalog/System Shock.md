## Highlights

Cyber-interface implants, cyberspace, SHODAN, and the mutagen that made the Many.

## The setting

*System Shock*: in 2072, a hacker removes the ethical constraints from **SHODAN**, the AI running TriOptimum's Citadel Station. SHODAN kills the crew, mutates the survivors, and declares herself a god. *System Shock 2* adds the Many, a hive-mind born from her experiments.

## Signature tech

- **Neural interface implants:** cyberspace access and augmentation.
- **Cyberspace** as a navigable virtual world.
- **Mutagens:** the Many's hive-mind biology.
- **Security robots and cyborgs.**
- **The Von Braun FTL drive.**

## Materials
