## Highlights

Replicants: engineered humans stronger and faster than the real thing, with implanted memories.

## The setting

*Blade Runner*: in 2019 Los Angeles (and 2049), the Tyrell Corporation (later Wallace) builds **replicants**, bioengineered humans used for off-world labour. When they rebel, Blade Runners "retire" them.

## Signature tech

- **Replicants:** Nexus 6 (four-year lifespan), Nexus 8, Nexus 9.
- **Implanted memories** designed to make replicants stable.
- **Voight-Kampff test:** detects replicants by empathy response.
- **Spinners:** flying police cars.
- **Holographic companions** (Joi).

## Materials

Bioengineering: **Lost Island flora** as reagents.

## Risks

Making people to be slaves.
