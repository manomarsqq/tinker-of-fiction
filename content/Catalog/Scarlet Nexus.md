## Highlights

Psionics, the brain-link (SAS), and the red strings that connect minds.

## The setting

*Scarlet Nexus*: in New Himuka, psionically gifted people join the Other Suppression Force to fight the **Others**, monsters that fall from the sky and eat brains. Squads share powers through the **Struggle Arms System (SAS)**, a brain-link.

## Signature tech

- **SAS (Struggle Arms System):** links squad members' brains so they can borrow each other's powers.
- **Psionic enhancement** through drugs and implants.
- **Brain-computer interfaces** everywhere in daily life.
- **The Extinction Circle** (the Red Strings).

## Materials

Psionic drugs: **Lost Island flora**. Others' tissue: **Endbringer tissue** as an analogue.

## Risks

Power-sharing drugs are addictive. The Others were human once.
