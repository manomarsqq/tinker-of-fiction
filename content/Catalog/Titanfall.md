## Highlights

## The setting

*Titanfall*: on the Frontier, the Militia fights the IMC corporation with **Titans** (giant mechs) and **Pilots**, elite soldiers with jump kits for wall-running. *Titanfall 2* follows rifleman Jack Cooper and the Titan BT-7274.

## Signature tech

- **Titans:** Vanguard (BT-7274), Ion, Scorch, Northstar, Ronin, Tone, Legion. Dropped from orbit.
- **Jump kits:** double jumps and wall-running.
- **Pilot tactical kits:** cloak, grapple, stim, phase shift, pulse blade.
- **Neural links** between Pilot and Titan.
- **The Fold Weapon** (hazardous).
