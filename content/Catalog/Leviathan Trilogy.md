## Highlights

Darwinist fabricated beasts versus Clanker walkers, in an alternate World War I.

## The setting

*Leviathan* by Scott Westerfeld: in 1914, the **Darwinist** powers (Britain, France, Russia) fabricate living creatures as machines (including the Leviathan, a whale-airship), while the **Clanker** powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary) build diesel walkers.

## Signature tech

- **Fabricated beasts:** the Leviathan airship, message lizards, Huxley ascenders, strafing hawks.
- **Clanker walkers:** Stormwalkers, Cyklops, Dreadnought walkers.
- **Tesla cannon.**
