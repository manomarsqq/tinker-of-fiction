## Highlights

Gem technology: warp pads, injectors, gem ships, and bubbling.

## The setting

*Steven Universe*: the Crystal Gems (magical alien beings of light, projected from gemstones) defend Earth from the Gem Homeworld, which once tried to strip the planet for new Gems. Steven, half-human and half-Gem, inherits his mother's gem and her war.

## Signature tech

- **Warp pads:** instant teleport network.
- **Injectors:** create Gems by draining a planet's life.
- **Gem ships**, including the hand ship.
- **Bubbling:** storing objects and poofed Gems in bubbles.
- **Gem weapons** summoned from the gem.

## Materials

Gem tech needs **light** and exotic materials: **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

Injectors drain planets. Corrupted Gems.
