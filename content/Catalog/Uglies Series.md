## Highlights

Pretty surgery, hoverboards, and the Specials.

## The setting

*Uglies* (Scott Westerfeld): in a future city, everyone gets **pretty surgery** at sixteen, and with it a hidden brain lesion that makes them docile. **Specials** are surgically enhanced enforcers. Tally Youngblood runs away to the Smoke to escape it.

## Signature tech

- **Hoverboards:** magnetic lift over metal grids, with crash bracelets.
- **Pretty surgery:** total cosmetic reconstruction.
- **Special surgery:** enhanced strength, speed and reflexes (and cruelty).
- **Interface rings** and **skintennas**.

## Materials

Hoverboards need a metal grid, or lift fans. Surgery is advanced medicine.

## Risks

The brain lesions. Special surgery changes personality.
