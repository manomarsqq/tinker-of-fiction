## Highlights

## The setting

*Wolfenstein: The New Order*: the Nazis won World War II by stealing the technology of the **Da'at Yichud**, a secret Jewish society of scientists whose inventions were centuries ahead. B.J. Blazkowicz fights their regime in an alternate 1960.

## Signature tech

- **Da'at Yichud tech:** concrete-and-steel manufacturing, super-battery, the Laserkraftwerk.
- **Nazi robotics:** Panzerhunds, Supersoldaten, walkers.
- **Moon base** and space program.
- **Laserkraftwerk:** a laser cutter weapon.
