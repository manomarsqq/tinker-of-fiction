## Highlights

MJOLNIR power armour, slipspace FTL, Covenant plasma, and Forerunner hard light.

## The setting

*Halo*: in the 26th century, humanity's UNSC fights the **Covenant**, a theocratic alliance of alien species that believes humans must be destroyed. Both are playing in the ruins of the **Forerunners**, who built the Halo rings to starve the parasitic **Flood** by killing all sentient life in the galaxy.

## Signature tech

- **UNSC:** MJOLNIR power armour with energy shields; Spartan-II augmentation; MA5 rifles; Warthogs, Pelicans; MAC cannons; slipspace drives; smart AIs like Cortana.
- **Covenant:** plasma weapons, energy swords, needlers, Ghosts and Banshees, gravity lifts, personal shields, plasma bombardment from orbit.
- **Forerunners:** hard light (solid light constructs), slipspace mastery, Sentinels, Promethean constructs, and the Halo array itself.

## Materials

UNSC tech is conventional to advanced. Covenant tech needs exotic power sources: **Endbringer tissue**. Forerunner tech needs precursor matter: **Shard Dungeon elements** or **Gate salvage**.

## Risks

The Flood. The Halo array. Smart AIs go rampant after seven years.
