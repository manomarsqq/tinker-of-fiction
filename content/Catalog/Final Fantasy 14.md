## The setting

*Final Fantasy XIV*: on Hydaelyn, the ancient Allagan Empire built towers and dragons of metal; the modern Garlean Empire fights with magitek. Aether is the energy of life, and primals are gods summoned from belief.

## Signature tech

- **Allagan tech:** Crystal Tower, Omega, bio-engineered chimeras.
- **Garlean magitek:** ceruleum engines, magitek armour.
- **Airships** and **Aetherytes** (teleport network).
- **Aether manipulation.**

## Materials

Ceruleum and aether crystals: **Labyrinthos ore**, **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks
