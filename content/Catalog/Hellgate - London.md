## Highlights

Templar and Cabalist tech against a demon invasion of London.

## The setting

*Hellgate: London*: in 2038, demons invade London through a Hellgate. The **Templars** fight with blessed weapons and armour, the **Cabalists** turn demonic magic against itself, and the **Hunters** use technology.

## Signature tech

- **Templar arms and armour:** blessed swords and shields.
- **Cabalist focus items:** demon-channelling tools.
- **Hunter tech:** guns, drones and engineering.
- **Underground stations** as fortified bases.

## Materials

Blessed items need faith, and demonic ones need demons. **Hazardous.**
