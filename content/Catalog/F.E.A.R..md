## Highlights

Replica soldiers, telepathic commanders, and Armacham's psychic projects.

## The setting

*F.E.A.R.*: Armacham Technology Corporation runs Project Origin and Project Perseus: breeding psychic commanders from Alma Wade, and cloning Replica soldiers they can control telepathically. Alma is not happy about it.

## Signature tech

- **Replica soldiers:** vat-grown clones slaved to a telepathic commander.
- **Reflex enhancement:** the Point Man's slow-motion reflexes.
- **Powered armour** (REV6, EPA).
- **Particle weapons** that strip flesh from bone.

## Materials

Cloning vats and bio-labs: conventional, with **Lost Island flora** for the exotic reagents.

## Risks

The psychic side (Alma) is a haunting that levels cities. Hazardous.
