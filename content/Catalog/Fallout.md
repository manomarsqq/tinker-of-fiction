## Highlights

Power armour, FEV, energy weapons, Pip-Boys, and retro-futurist everything.

## The setting

*Fallout*: an America that never left the 1950s aesthetic fights a war with China over resources and ends in nuclear fire. Survivors crawl out of Vaults. The **Forced Evolutionary Virus** makes super mutants.

## Signature tech

- **Power armour:** T-45 through X-01.
- **Pip-Boy:** a wrist computer.
- **Fusion cores**, micro fusion cells.
- **Laser and plasma weapons.**
- **FEV:** mutagenic power-granting virus.
- **Vaults.**
- **Alien tech** (Zetans).

## Materials
