## Highlights

Tiberium, superweapons, Tesla coils and chronosphere teleportation.

## The setting

*Command & Conquer*: in the *Tiberian* series, GDI and the Brotherhood of Nod fight over **Tiberium**, an alien crystal that spreads, mutates life and powers everything. In *Red Alert*, a time-travel accident changes history, and the Allies and Soviets fight with chronospheres and Tesla coils.

## Signature tech

- **Tiberium** harvesting and refining.
- **Ion cannon:** orbital strike.
- **Obelisk of Light:** laser defence.
- **Chronosphere:** teleportation.
- **Tesla coils** and **Iron Curtain.**
- **Mammoth tanks.**
