## Highlights

Nanotech, mining tools, rail drivers and GeoMod destruction.

## The setting

*Red Faction*: on Mars, miners working for the Ultor Corporation rebel against brutal conditions and a nanotech plague. *Guerrilla* (a later game) follows the Red Faction's war against the Earth Defense Force with sledgehammers, explosives and destructible buildings.

## Signature tech

- **Nano forge:** a nanotech fabricator that builds weapons from salvage.
- **Rail driver:** shoots through walls, with a scope that sees through them.
- **Arc welder**, **Singularity bomb**, **thermobaric rockets.**
- **Sledgehammers** and **remote charges.**
- **Walkers** and mining vehicles.

## Materials

Salvage: conventional. The nano forge needs **Labyrinthos ore** for exotic nanites.

## Risks

Ultor's nanotech plague.
