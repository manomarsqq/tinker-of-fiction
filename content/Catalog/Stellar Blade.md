## Highlights

Exospines, Beta and Burst skills, and the Naytiba.

## The setting

*Stellar Blade*: Eve, a soldier of the 7th Airborne Squad, descends from the orbiting Colony to a ruined Earth to fight the **Naytiba**, the monsters that drove humanity off-world. With Adam and Lily, she reaches Xion, the last city, and learns what the Naytiba really are.

## Signature tech

- **Exospines:** body modules that add abilities.
- **Beta and Burst skills:** energy-fuelled combat techniques.
- **Nano-suits** with swappable designs.
- **Drones** for scouting and ranged fire.
- **Hyperstream:** the energy of the Naytiba.

## Materials

Naytiba cores: **Endbringer tissue**, Harbinger remains after 15 May.

## Risks

The Naytiba were human. So is what powers the tech.
