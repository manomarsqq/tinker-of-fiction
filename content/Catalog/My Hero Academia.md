## Highlights

Support items, Quirk science and bio-engineering, from UA's support course to the Nomu labs.

## The setting

*My Hero Academia*: 80% of the population has Quirks. Heroes are licensed professionals, and support companies build their gear. All For One and Dr Garaki build Nomu from harvested Quirks.

## Signature tech

- **Support items:** Mei Hatsume's babies, hero costumes, gauntlets, capture tape.
- **Quirk science:** Quirk-erasing bullets (Trigger and the Quirk-destroying drug), Quirk analysis.
- **Nomu:** engineered bodies loaded with multiple Quirks.
- **Trigger:** a Quirk-boosting drug.

## Materials

Mostly conventional; Nomu need **Endbringer tissue** or worse.

## Risks

Nomu. Quirk-destroying drugs. Power-granting.
