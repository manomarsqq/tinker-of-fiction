## Highlights

Minds, post-scarcity, displacement, and General Systems Vehicles.

## The setting

*The Culture* (Iain M. Banks): a post-scarcity interstellar civilisation run by superintelligent AIs called **Minds**, who live in ships and habitats and care for trillions of humans and drones. Special Circumstances handles the dirty work with other civilisations.

## Signature tech

- **Minds:** AIs vastly beyond human, running ships and Orbitals.
- **General Systems Vehicles (GSVs):** ships the size of cities that build other ships.
- **Displacement:** instant short-range teleportation.
- **Knife missiles**, **effectors** and **drones.**
- **Neural lace:** a mesh that links and backs up the brain.
- **Gland implants:** drug glands for any mental state.
- **Orbitals:** ringworld habitats.

## Materials

The Culture's tech is far-future physics: **Shard Dungeon elements**, **Gate salvage**. A tier-5 catalog.
