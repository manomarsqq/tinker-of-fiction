## Highlights

Slug blasters, elemental slugs that transform when fired, and mecha-beasts.

## The setting

*Slugterra*: beneath the surface lies Slugterra, a world of 99 caverns. Eli Shane, the new Shane gunslinger, and his gang fire **slugs** (small elemental creatures) from blasters. At high velocity, a slug transforms into a powerful "velocimorph" form. Dr Blakk corrupts slugs into **ghouls** with Dark Water.

## Signature tech

- **Slug blasters:** fire slugs at speed to transform them.
- **Mecha-beasts:** robotic mounts that transform into vehicles.
- **Fusion shots:** two slugs combined into one attack.
- **Ghoul-slugs:** corrupted by Dark Water.

## Materials

Slugs are the power source: **Lost Island** or **Labyrinthos** creatures as substitutes.

## Risks

Dark Water corruption.
