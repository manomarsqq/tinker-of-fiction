## Highlights

Cyberware, rigger drones and vehicles, weapons and armour, and the Matrix.

## The setting

*Shadowrun*: in 2050s megacorp cyberpunk, magic has returned. Elves, dwarves, orks and trolls walk among humans, and shadowrunners do deniable jobs for corps.

## Signature tech

- **Cyberware and bioware:** wired reflexes, cybereyes, datajacks.
- **Rigging:** jacking into drones and vehicles.
- **The Matrix:** virtual-reality network. Deckers hack through it.
- **Weapons and armour**, smartlinks.

## Materials

Conventional to advanced.

## Risks

Essence loss: too much cyberware erodes the soul (and magic).
