## Highlights

The Transistor: a sword that absorbs people and wields their functions.

## The setting

*Transistor*: in the city of Cloudbank, the singer Red loses her voice to an assassination attempt and gains the **Transistor**, a talking sword holding the mind of the man who took the blade for her. The Camerata, who wanted to reshape the city, unleashed the **Process**, which is erasing Cloudbank.

## Signature tech

- **The Transistor:** absorbs a person's mind and "function" when they die near it.
- **Functions:** Crash(), Breach(), Bounce(), Jaunt(), Spark(), Load(), Void(), Help(): combinable into custom abilities.
- **Turn():** a planning mode that freezes time while Red plans her moves.
- **The Process:** automated white machines that erase and rebuild.
