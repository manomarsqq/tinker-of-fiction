## Highlights

BattleMechs, fusion engines, myomer muscle and Kearny-Fuchida jump drives.

## The setting

*BattleTech*: the 31st century. Great Houses of a fractured Inner Sphere fight endless wars over worlds with BattleMechs: ten-metre walking tanks piloted through neurohelmets. Technology has regressed and recovered, and a 'Mech is a family heirloom.

## Signature tech

- **BattleMechs:** from 20-ton scouts to 100-ton assault 'Mechs, powered by **fusion engines**, moved by **myomer** (artificial muscle that contracts under current).
- **Neurohelmets:** the pilot's sense of balance drives the 'Mech.
- **Weapons:** PPCs, autocannons, LRM/SRM racks, lasers, Gauss rifles.
- **Kearny-Fuchida drive:** instant interstellar jumps between charged points.
- **DropShips and JumpShips.**

## Materials

Fusion engines and myomer are within reach with Earth materials. The KF drive needs exotic physics: **Gate salvage**.

## Risks

Low, apart from building a walking tank in a Boardwalk basement.
