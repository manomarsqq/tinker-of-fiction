## Highlights

Capitol tech: hovercraft, force fields, arena control, and muttations.

## The setting

*The Hunger Games*: the Capitol of Panem rules twelve districts and forces them to send children to fight to the death in televised Games, controlled by Gamemakers who can change the arena at will.

## Signature tech

- **Arena control:** force fields, weather, fire, terrain changes.
- **Muttations:** genetically engineered creatures (tracker jackers, jabberjays, mockingjays).
- **Hovercraft** and **trackers.**
- **Capitol medicine:** rapid healing, cosmetic surgery.
- **Pods** in the Capitol (Mockingjay).

## Materials

Mostly conventional; muttations need genetic engineering.

## Risks
