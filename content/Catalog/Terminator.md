## Highlights

## The setting

*Terminator*: in the future, **Skynet**, a military AI, launches a nuclear war and fights humanity with machines. It sends Terminators back in time to kill John Connor before he can lead the resistance.

## Signature tech

- **T-800:** a coltan endoskeleton covered in living tissue.
- **T-1000:** mimetic polyalloy (liquid metal).
- **Time displacement equipment.**
- **Plasma rifles**, **HK drones**.
- **Neural net CPUs.**

## Materials

Coltan alloys and mimetic polyalloy: **Labyrinthos ore**. Time displacement: **Gate salvage**.
