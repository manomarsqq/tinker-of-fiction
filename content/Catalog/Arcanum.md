## Highlights

Steam engines versus magic, in a world where the two actively interfere with each other.

## The setting

*Arcanum: Of Steamworks and Magick Obscura*: an industrial revolution in a fantasy world. Magick and technology are opposed; the more of one you have, the worse the other works around you.

## Signature tech

- **Steam engines, trains, zeppelins.**
- **Firearms and explosives.**
- **Mechanical constructs** and schematics that combine.

## Materials

Mundane, mostly: iron, steam, coal. The magick side needs a Powers purchase.

## Risks

Tech and magick don't mix. Buying both is a problem.
