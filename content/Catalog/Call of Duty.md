## The setting

*Call of Duty*: military shooters from WWII through near-future wars. The *Advanced Warfare*, *Black Ops III* and *Infinite Warfare* entries add exosuits, cybernetics and space combat.

## Signature tech

- **Exosuits** (Advanced Warfare): boost jumps, dodges, shields, cloaking.
- **Direct Neural Interface** (Black Ops III): cyber-core abilities, drone control.
- **Killstreaks:** UAVs, sentries, airstrikes, attack helicopters.
- **Modern weapons**, thermal optics, body armour.
- **Grappling hooks** and wingsuits.
