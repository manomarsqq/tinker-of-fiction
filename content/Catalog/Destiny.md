## Highlights

Golden Age tech, Ghosts, and the Light.

## The setting

*Destiny*: the Traveler, a vast white sphere, gave humanity a Golden Age of miracles and then fell silent when the Darkness came. Guardians, raised from death by Ghosts, wield the Light to defend the Last City.

## Signature tech

- **Ghosts:** small drones that resurrect and assist Guardians.
- **Golden Age tech:** Warminds (Rasputin), terraforming, advanced ships.
- **Exos:** humans in robotic bodies.
- **Weapons** with exotic properties.

## Materials

Golden Age tech needs Traveler-touched materials: **Endbringer tissue**, **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

The Light and the Darkness are cosmic forces. A "Traveler" on Earth Bet is an uncomfortable echo of Scion.
