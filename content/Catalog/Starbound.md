## Highlights

Ships, matter manipulators, and a universe of procedurally generated planets.

## The setting

*Starbound*: after Earth is destroyed by the Ruin, a Protector escapes in a starship and explores a procedurally generated universe of planets, species (Apex, Avian, Floran, Glitch, Hylotl, Novakid) and ancient artefacts.

## Signature tech

- **Matter manipulator:** mines, places and moves blocks.
- **Starship** with FTL, S.A.I.L. AI and teleporter.
- **Tech consoles:** dash, double jump, morph ball.
- **Pixel printers** and 3D printers.
- **Species tech:** Apex miniknog labs, Glitch castles, Avian temples.

## Materials
