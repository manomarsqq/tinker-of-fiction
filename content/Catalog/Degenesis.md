## Highlights

Post-apocalyptic survival tech and the Primer: an alien biology remaking Europe and Africa.

## The setting

*Degenesis*: centuries after asteroids hit Earth, the survivors rebuild in Europe ("Borca") and Africa ("Purgare"). The asteroids brought the **Primer**, a spore-based alien biology that mutates life and people. Cults (Spitalians, Chroniclers, Anubians, Jehammedans) each carry a fragment of the old world.

## Signature tech

- **Spitalian medicine:** field surgery and anti-spore treatments.
- **Chronicler tech:** salvaged pre-apocalypse data systems.
- **Burn:** a drug refined from spore fields.
- **Primer biology:** Psychonauts, whose altered brains touch the Primer.

## Materials

Spores: **Lost Island flora**, or (after 2012) **Nemesis spore deposits**.

## Risks

The Primer is an invasive biology.
