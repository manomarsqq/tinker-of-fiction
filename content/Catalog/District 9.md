## Highlights

Prawn exosuits, DNA-locked weapons, and the fluid that turns humans into Prawns.

## The setting

*District 9*: a vast alien ship stalls over Johannesburg. Its crew, insectoid refugees nicknamed "Prawns", are penned into a slum. Their powerful weapons only work with Prawn DNA, so the corporation MNU wants a human who can use them.

## Signature tech

- **Prawn exosuit:** a heavily armed, mind-linked mech.
- **Prawn weapons:** lightning guns, gravity guns, plasma rifles; DNA-locked.
- **The fluid:** fuel for the mothership, and a mutagen that turns humans into Prawns.
- **The command module and mothership.**
