## The setting

*Trails of Cold Steel*: in the Erebonian Empire, orbal technology (powered by **septium** crystals and **orbments**) drives industrialisation. Class VII, a group of students, becomes entangled with ancient **Divine Knights**: giant armoured beings bonded to chosen pilots.

## Signature tech

- **ARCUS units:** orbments for arts and links between allies.
- **Orbal tech:** trains, airships, orbal guns.
- **Panzer Soldats:** mechs.
- **Divine Knights:** Valimar and the others.

## Materials

Septium: **Labyrinthos ore**. Divine Knights are **Gate-salvage**-grade.
