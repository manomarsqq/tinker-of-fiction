## The setting

*Ben 10*: a kid finds the Omnitrix, a watch that transforms him into alien species. Built by Azmuth, a Galvan: the smartest species in the galaxy.

## Signature tech

- **The Omnitrix:** DNA sampling and transformation into any catalogued species.
- **Galvan tech:** Galvanic Mechamorphs, time devices, the Codon Stream.
- **Plumber gear:** badges, blasters, prison pods.
- **Proto-tool**, hoverboards.
