## Highlights

Franxx: bio-mechs piloted by a boy-girl pair, fighting Klaxosaurs.

## The setting

*Darling in the Franxx*: in a far future where adults live in comfort, children are raised to pilot Franxx in pairs, the "Stamen" and "Pistil", to defend mobile fortress cities from giant Klaxosaurs.

## Signature tech

- **Franxx:** humanoid bio-mechs with a pilot pair; one controls, one channels.
- **Magma energy:** fuel mined from the earth, fought over.
- **Plantations:** mobile fortress cities.

## Materials

Klaxosaur-derived biology: **Endbringer tissue**, and Harbinger remains after 15 May.

## Risks

Pilot bonding is intimate and psychologically intense. Built from monster parts.
