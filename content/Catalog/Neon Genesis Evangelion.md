## Highlights

Evangelions, AT fields, S² engines and the MAGI.

## The setting

*Neon Genesis Evangelion*: after the Second Impact, NERV pilots teenage children in **Evangelions** (cloned giants in armour) against the **Angels**. The real plan is the Human Instrumentality Project.

## Signature tech

- **Evangelions:** clone giants restrained by armour.
- **AT fields:** absolute barriers.
- **S² engines:** perpetual power.
- **The MAGI:** three supercomputers with a human mind each.
- **Entry plugs** and LCL.

## Materials

Evangelions are grown from Angel tissue: **Endbringer tissue** or Harbinger remains.

## Risks
