## The setting

*Final Space*: Gary Goodspeed, serving a prison sentence alone on a ship, befriends Mooncake, a tiny green creature that can destroy planets. They're hunted by the Lord Commander, who wants Mooncake's power to open the breach into **Final Space**, a dimension where cosmic Titans are imprisoned.

## Signature tech

- **The Crimson Light / Galaxy One:** ships with AI companions (H.U.E.).
- **Robot companions:** KVN, a relentlessly cheerful and annoying bot.
- **Breach technology:** openings into Final Space.
- **Infinity Guard** weapons and ships.

## Materials

## Risks
