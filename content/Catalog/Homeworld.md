## Highlights

Hyperspace, motherships, resource harvesting and fleet-scale construction.

## The setting

*Homeworld*: the Kushan, on the desert planet Kharak, discover a buried hyperspace core that proves they came from elsewhere. They build a colossal **Mothership** to return to their homeworld, Hiigara, and fight the Taiidan Empire all the way.

## Signature tech

- **The Mothership:** a mobile shipyard and home for a people.
- **Hyperspace cores**, and the Bentusi's advanced tech.
- **Resource collectors** and refineries.
- **Fleet construction:** fighters, corvettes, frigates, destroyers, carriers.
- **Salvage corvettes** that steal enemy ships.

## Materials

Hyperspace cores: **Gate salvage** or **Shard Dungeon elements**.
