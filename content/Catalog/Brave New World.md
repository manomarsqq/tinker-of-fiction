## Highlights

## The setting

*Brave New World* (Aldous Huxley): the World State manufactures people in **Hatcheries**, conditions them from birth into castes (Alpha to Epsilon), and keeps everyone happy with sex, consumerism and **soma**. Stability above everything.

## Signature tech

- **Hatcheries:** Bokanovsky's Process, splitting one egg into up to 96 identical twins.
- **Hypnopaedia:** conditioning through sleep-teaching.
- **Neo-Pavlovian conditioning:** shaping likes and fears.
- **Soma:** a perfect drug with no hangover.
- **Feelies:** full-sensory films.

## Materials
