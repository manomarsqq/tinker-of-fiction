## Highlights

Everything, grimdark: the Imperium, the Tau, the Eldar, the Necrons, and the Warp behind them all.

## The setting

*Warhammer 40,000*: in the 41st millennium, the Imperium of Man holds a million worlds together with faith, fear and a corpse-god on a golden throne. Every alien race and the Chaos gods of the **Warp** want it dead. There is only war.

## Signature tech

- **Imperium:** bolters, power armour, Space Marine gene-seed, Titans, plasma, lasguns, the Astronomican.
- **Tau:** battlesuits, pulse weapons, drones, the Greater Good.
- **Eldar:** wraithbone, psychic tech, spirit stones, webway gates.
- **Necrons:** living metal, gauss weapons, phase-out, tomb worlds.
- **The Warp:** FTL through a hell dimension, with Gellar fields.

## Materials

Everything exotic. Wraithbone and living metal: **Shard Dungeon elements**. Warp tech: **hazardous**.

## Risks

The Warp. Chaos. Gene-seed is power-granting and brutal.
