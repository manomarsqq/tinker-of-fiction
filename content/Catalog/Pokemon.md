## Highlights

Poké Balls, matter-energy conversion, PC storage, and Porygon.

## The setting

*Pokémon*: people and Pokémon live alongside each other. Trainers catch Pokémon in **Poké Balls**, which convert living creatures into energy and back, and store extras in a networked **PC** system. Silph Co. and Devon build the tech.

## Signature tech

- **Poké Balls:** matter-energy conversion of living beings, with variants (Great, Ultra, Master).
- **PC storage:** networked storage for converted Pokémon.
- **Pokédex:** a scanner and database.
- **Porygon:** a digital Pokémon, made entirely by humans.
- **Healing machines** at Pokémon Centers.
- **Silph Scope**, **Master Ball**.

## Materials

Apricorns for handmade balls: **Lost Island flora**.

## Risks

Storing living beings as data. The Master Ball captures anything.
