## Highlights

Wanzers: realistic, modular walking tanks in near-future wars.

## The setting

*Front Mission*: in the 21st century, supranational blocs (the O.C.U., U.S.N., DHZ) fight proxy wars with **Wanzers** (from the German *Wanderung Panzer*, walking tanks). It's grounded, political and grim.

## Signature tech

- **Wanzers:** modular walkers with swappable arms, legs, body and backpack.
- **Neural interfaces** for pilots (and the notorious bio-processors, made from human brains).
- **Support drones and ECM.**
