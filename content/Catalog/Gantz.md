## Highlights

The Gantz suits, X-guns, teleportation, and the black sphere that runs the game.

## The setting

*Gantz*: people who die are "reborn" in a room with a black sphere that forces them to hunt aliens in a points-based game. Reach 100 points and you can go free or revive someone.

## Signature tech

- **Gantz suits:** strength, durability, and jumping far beyond human.
- **X-guns and Y-guns:** delayed-kill and capture weapons.
- **Gantz swords** with extending blades.
- **Teleportation** and body reconstruction.
- **The sphere:** reconstructs the dead from data.

## Materials

The sphere's tech is alien and far beyond Earth. **Gate salvage**.

## Risks

Gantz is a death game with a points system. Very close to home for a Tinker who earns points from conflict.
