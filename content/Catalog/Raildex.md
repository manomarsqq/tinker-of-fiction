## Highlights

Esper development, AIM fields, drones and Academy City's science.

## The setting

*A Certain Magical Index / A Certain Scientific Railgun*: in **Academy City**, 80% of the population are students, and the city's **Power Curriculum Program** develops them into espers through drugs, hypnosis and electrodes. Magic exists on the other side of the world.

## Signature tech

- **Esper development:** turning normal people into espers, Level 0 to Level 5.
- **AIM fields** and the AIM Diffusion Field.
- **Sisters:** clones of Misaka with a network mind.
- **Drones, Five Over, Hardened Space tech.**
- **Tree Diagram:** a supercomputer that predicts everything.

## Materials

Mostly conventional to advanced.

## Risks

Esper development destroys the minds of the unlucky. Power-granting on an industrial scale.
