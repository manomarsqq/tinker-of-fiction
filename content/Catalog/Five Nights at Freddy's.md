## Highlights

Animatronics, Remnant, and haunted engineering.

## The setting

*Five Nights at Freddy's*: Fazbear Entertainment's pizzeria animatronics are possessed by the souls of children murdered by William Afton. Afton's research into **Remnant** (soul matter that binds to metal) keeps them, and him, walking.

## Signature tech

- **Animatronics:** singing mascots with free-roam modes.
- **Springlock suits:** costumes that double as animatronics, and trap their wearers.
- **Remnant:** soul-infused material that binds to machines.
- **Security systems:** cameras, doors, audio lures.

## Materials

Animatronics are conventional. Remnant is **hazardous**.

## Risks

Possession, and binding souls to machines.
