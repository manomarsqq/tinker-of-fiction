## Highlights

The Nanosuit: armour, cloak, strength and speed in one suit that bonds to its wearer.

## The setting

*Crysis*: in 2020, US special forces in prototype Nanosuits fight North Korean forces on an island where the **Ceph**, an ancient squid-like alien species, is waking up. The later games move to a ruined New York.

## Signature tech

- **Nanosuit:** four modes: Armour (absorbs damage), Cloak (optical camouflage), Strength (jumps, throws, punches through walls), Speed (sprinting). Nanosuit 2 adds tactical visor and upgrade modules.
- **Ceph tech:** exosuits, cold weapons, the Alpha Ceph's hive mind.
- **Weapon attachments** swapped on the fly.

## Materials

The suit is nanotech plus Ceph-derived components: **Gate salvage** and **Labyrinthos ore** for the exotic parts.

## Risks

Nanosuit 2 bonds with its wearer at a cellular level and slowly rewrites them. Handle as power-granting tech.
