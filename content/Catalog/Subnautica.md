## Highlights

Fabricators, submersibles, underwater bases, and the Precursors' science.

## The setting

*Subnautica*: a crew member crash-lands on the ocean planet 4546B and survives by building underwater. The Precursors left behind facilities studying the Kharaa bacterium.

## Signature tech

- **Fabricators**, **habitat builders** and **scanners** (scan a thing, learn to build it).
- **Seamoth, Prawn Suit, Cyclops** submersibles.
- **Stasis rifle**, **propulsion cannon**.
- **Precursor tech:** teleporters, the disease research facility, ion cubes.

## Materials

Mostly conventional (Earth-like minerals). Precursor tech: **Gate salvage**.

## Risks

Kharaa. Leviathan-class predators.
