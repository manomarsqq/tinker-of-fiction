## Highlights

Mechs, printing, and NHPs: non-human persons born from paracausal computation.

## The setting

*Lancer*: in a far-future galaxy under the post-scarcity Union, mech pilots (Lancers) fight with frames licensed from manufacturers. **NHPs**, AIs grown from paracausal computation, are shackled and cycled to keep them sane.

## Signature tech

- **Mech frames:** GMS standard, IPS-Northstar (logistics, mobility), Smith-Shimano Corporo (luxury tech), Horus (paracausal pirate code), Harrison Armory (heavy weapons, the Purview).
- **Printing:** mechs and gear printed from licences.
- **NHPs:** paracausal AIs.
- **Blinkspace** travel.

## Materials

Printers need feedstock. Horus and NHP tech need **paracausal** input: **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

NHPs that go cascading. Horus.
