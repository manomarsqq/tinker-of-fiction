## Highlights

Steampunk airships, crew-based combat, and engineering under fire.

## The setting

*Guns of Icarus*: after the Great Sky War, the world's surface is ravaged and the skies belong to airship crews: a pilot, gunners, and engineers who keep the ship from burning while it's being shot at. Factions fight over what's left.

## Signature tech

- **Airships:** Galleons, Goldfish, Pyramidions, Squids, Junkers, Spires, Mobulas. Balloons, engines and hull armour.
- **Ship weapons:** gatlings, flak, harpoons, mines, lumberjacks, hwachas.
- **Engineering tools:** spanners, mallets, pipe wrenches, fire extinguishers, chemical sprays.
