## Highlights

Super Earth's arsenal: stratagems, orbital strikes, exosuits, and FTL-capable destroyers.

## The setting

*Helldivers 2*: Super Earth, a satirical managed democracy, sends Helldivers to spread liberty across the galaxy against the Terminids, the Automatons and the Illuminate. Helldivers die a lot, and are replaced.

## Signature tech

- **Stratagems:** call-down supply drops, turrets, exosuits and orbital/eagle strikes, keyed by code.
- **Super Destroyers** in orbit.
- **Exosuits** (Patriot, Emancipator).
- **Hellpods:** orbital drop pods.
- **Weapons:** Liberators, Breakers, Railguns, Quasar Cannons, Autocannons.

## Materials

Mostly conventional. Hellbombs and destroyers need more: **Gate salvage** for FTL.

## Risks

Friendly fire.
