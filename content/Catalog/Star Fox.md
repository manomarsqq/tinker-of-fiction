## Highlights

Arwings, G-Diffusers, Landmasters and the Great Fox.

## The setting

*Star Fox*: Fox McCloud leads the Star Fox mercenary team (Falco, Peppy, Slippy) to defend the Lylat System from the mad scientist Andross, flying Arwing starfighters from the carrier Great Fox.

## Signature tech

- **Arwings:** starfighters with **G-Diffusers** (anti-gravity systems that make barrel rolls and space flight possible) and shield reflection.
- **Landmaster:** a hovering tank.
- **Blue-Marine:** a submarine.
- **Great Fox:** a mothership with ROB 64, an AI pilot.
- **Smart bombs** and charged lasers.

## Materials

G-Diffusers need anti-gravity physics: **Gate salvage** or **Shard Dungeon elements**.
