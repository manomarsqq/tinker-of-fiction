## Highlights

## The setting

*Lost Planet*: on the frozen planet E.D.N. III, colonists fight the **Akrid**, giant insectoid creatures whose bodies hold **thermal energy (T-ENG)**, the resource everyone needs to survive the cold.

## Signature tech

- **Vital Suits (VS):** mechs with harpoons, rotary guns and thrusters.
- **Harmonizers:** devices that use T-ENG to keep the body alive.
- **Anchor grapples.**
- **T-ENG** as fuel.

## Materials

T-ENG: **Endbringer tissue** or megafauna tissue.
