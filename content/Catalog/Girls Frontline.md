## Highlights

## The setting

*Girls' Frontline*: after World War III and the ELID outbreak, private military companies like Griffin & Kryuger field **T-Dolls** (tactical androids built around specific firearms) against the rogue AI army **Sangvis Ferri**.

## Signature tech

- **T-Dolls:** tactical android soldiers with firearm-linked fire-control cores.
- **Neural clouds:** backed-up minds that survive body loss.
- **Dummy links:** one T-Doll controlling several bodies.
- **Sangvis Ferri units**, and the rogue AI Elisa.
- **Collapse fluid** research (hazardous).
