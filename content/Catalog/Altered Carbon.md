## Highlights

Cortical stacks, resleeving, and death as an inconvenience for the rich.

## The setting

*Altered Carbon*: consciousness is stored on a disk at the base of the skull. Bodies ("sleeves") are replaceable. The rich ("Meths") live for centuries.

## Signature tech

- **Cortical stacks:** consciousness storage.
- **Needlecasting:** transmitting a mind across interstellar distance.
- **Sleeves:** cloned, synthetic or combat bodies.
- **Remote backup** for the very rich.

## Materials

Elder alien materials for the original stacks: **Gate salvage**. Human-made stacks are within reach.

## Risks

Immortality for whoever controls the stacks. Real death stops meaning anything.
