## Highlights

Mechanoids, archotech, and medicine for any disaster.

## The setting

*RimWorld*: colonists crash-land on a rim world with raiders, disease, animals and madness. In the background, the **archotechs** (superintelligent AIs) are gods, and their technology is priceless.

## Signature tech

- **Mechanoids:** centipedes, lancers, scythers.
- **Archotech:** archotech limbs, eyes, and the persona core (a superintelligence for a ship).
- **Luciferium:** heals everything and is fatally addictive.
- **Healer mech serum** and **resurrector mech serum.**
- **Glitterworld medicine** and bionics.

## Materials
