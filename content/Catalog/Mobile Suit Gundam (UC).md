## Highlights

Mobile suits, Minovsky physics, space colonies and Newtypes.

## The setting

*Mobile Suit Gundam* (Universal Century): humanity lives in space colonies at the Lagrange points. The Principality of Zeon declares independence from the Earth Federation, and the One Year War is fought with **mobile suits**, made practical by **Minovsky particles** that jam radar.

## Signature tech

- **Mobile suits:** the RX-78-2 Gundam, Zakus, and generations after.
- **Minovsky physics:** Minovsky particles, reactors, beam weapons and I-fields.
- **Space colonies** (O'Neill cylinders) and colony drops.
- **Psycommu and funnels** for Newtypes.
- **Beam sabres and rifles.**

## Materials

Minovsky reactors need helium-3 or equivalents: **Gate salvage** for the exotic parts. Luna Titanium / Gundarium: **Labyrinthos ore**.

## Risks

Colony drops. The whole setting is about war crimes.
