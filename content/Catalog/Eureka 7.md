## Highlights

## The setting

*Eureka Seven*: on a planet covered by the Scub Coral, trapar particles in the air let people "lift" (surf the sky) on ref-boards and pilot LFO mechs. The Scub Coral turns out to be alive.

## Signature tech

- **Ref-boards:** sky-surfing boards.
- **LFOs (Light Finding Operations):** mechs that ride trapar waves.
- **Compac Drives:** control interfaces that bond with pilots.
- **The Gekko-Go:** a flying ship.
