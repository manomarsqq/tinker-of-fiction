## Highlights

Originium: a crystal that powers civilisation, fuels Arts, and infects the people who touch it.

## The setting

*Arknights*: Terra's civilisation runs on Originium, and Originium causes Oripathy, a fatal infection. The infected are persecuted; Rhodes Island treats them.

## Signature tech

- **Originium engines** and nomadic mobile cities.
- **Arts units:** focusing devices for magic.
- **Oripathy medicine.**

## Materials

Originium: **Labyrinthos ore** or **Gate salvage**, with great care.

## Risks

Oripathy. Anything Originium-based could infect.
