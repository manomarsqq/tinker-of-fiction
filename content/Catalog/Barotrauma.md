## Highlights

Submarines on Europa's ice ocean, crewed by people who have to keep everything running.

## The setting

*Barotrauma*: in the 2100s, humans live on Jupiter's moon Europa in the ocean beneath the ice. Crews pilot submarines between outposts, fighting flooding, fires, sabotage, monsters and each other.

## Signature tech

- **Submarines:** reactors, sonar, ballast, engines, hull repair.
- **Diving suits** and exosuits.
- **Fabricators** and **deconstructors.**
- **Coilguns and railguns** mounted on subs.
- **Genetic materials** that give crew abilities.

## Materials

Mostly conventional. Some chemistry for fuel rods.

## Risks

Low tech-wise. Europa's monsters (Molochs, Endworms) are the danger.
