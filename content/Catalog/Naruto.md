## Highlights

Seals, puppets, and the tools of the shinobi world.

## The setting

*Naruto*: in a world of hidden ninja villages, shinobi use **chakra** for jutsu. The technology side is sealing (fuinjutsu), puppetry, and ninja tools.

## Signature tech

- **Seals (fuinjutsu):** storage scrolls, barrier seals, explosive tags, the Flying Thunder God formula.
- **Puppets:** Sasori's human puppets and Kankuro's weaponised ones.
- **Ninja tools:** kunai, shuriken, smoke bombs, soldier pills.
- **Chakra-conducting metals.**

## Materials

Chakra-conducting metals: **Labyrinthos ore**. Seals need **Chakra** (under Powers).

## Risks

Human puppetry is a war crime. Some seals trap souls.
