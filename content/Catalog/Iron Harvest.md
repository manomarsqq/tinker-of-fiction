## Highlights

Dieselpunk mechs in an alternate 1920s Europe.

## The setting

*Iron Harvest* (from Jakub Różalski's *1920+* art): an alternate history where the Great War was fought with walking war machines, and Europe is still full of their wrecks. Polania, Saxony and Rusviet stand on the edge of a second war, pushed by a secret conspiracy.

## Signature tech

- **Mechs:** from nimble two-legged scouts to towering multi-legged dreadnoughts.
- **Dieselpunk weapons:** flamethrowers, heavy machine guns, artillery.
- **Tesla's inventions** (in-setting): electrical warfare and lightning towers.
