## The setting

*Futurama*: the year 3000. Planet Express, a delivery company run by the senile genius Professor Farnsworth, whose inventions solve every problem and cause two more.

## Signature tech

- **The Planet Express ship:** a dark-matter engine that moves the universe around the ship.
- **Bender:** a bending robot with a beer-powered fuel cell.
- **The Smelloscope, the What-If Machine, the Finglonger, the Death Clock.**
- **Suicide booths, head jars, tubes.**
- **Time machines** that only go forward.
