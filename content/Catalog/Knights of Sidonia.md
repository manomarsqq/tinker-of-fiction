## Highlights

## The setting

*Knights of Sidonia*: a thousand years after the Gauna (shape-shifting aliens) destroyed Earth, the seedship **Sidonia** flees across space. Pilots fight Gauna in mechs called Gardes, using the only thing that kills them: Kabizashi.

## Signature tech

- **Gardes:** mechs with Higgs particle drives.
- **Kabizashi:** a rare material that can pierce a Gauna's core.
- **Heavy mass cannons.**
- **Photosynthesis-capable humans:** engineered to live on light.
- **Cloning** and Placenta tissue.

## Materials

Kabizashi is the key material: **Endbringer tissue** as a substitute. Higgs drives need **Gate salvage**.

## Risks

Gauna and Placenta tissue are shape-shifting and aggressive.
