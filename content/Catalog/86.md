## Highlights

Autonomous war drones, spider-legged mechs, and the neural links that let pilots share senses in combat.

## The setting

*86 -Eighty Six-*: the Republic of San Magnolia fights the Giadian Empire's autonomous drone army, the **Legion**, by sending its persecuted minority (the "86") to die in walking tanks while claiming the war is fought by unmanned drones.

## Signature tech

- **Legion drones:** self-directing combat units, from scouts to railgun platforms. Advanced units absorb the brain structures of the dead to keep thinking.
- **Juggernauts and Reginleifs:** four-legged mechs. The Juggernaut is a death trap; the Reginleif is fast and lethal.
- **Para-RAID:** a neural link that shares hearing (and more) between a handler and her pilots.
- **Morpho-class railgun:** a long-range artillery Legion.

## Materials

Mostly conventional. The Legion's liquid micromachines are the exotic part: **Labyrinthos ore** could stand in.

## Risks

Legion that learn from brains. Anything self-replicating and self-directing is one bad day from a Machine Army.

## On Earth Bet

The Legion (autonomous drones that absorb brains) share a *name* with the Endbringer Legions. Nothing else, yet. Europe already has a **Machine Army**. A second one would be very bad.

## Uses for Leet

- **Para-RAID** for Uber and Leet: shared senses in the field, perfect for a duo.
- **Reginleif-style walkers** for mobility.
- Drone swarms, carefully leashed.
