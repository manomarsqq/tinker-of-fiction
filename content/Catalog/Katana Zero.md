## Highlights

## The setting

*Katana Zero*: in a neo-noir city, a samurai assassin (the Dragon's rival, Subject Zero) uses **Chronos**, a military drug that lets him see possible futures and "replay" fights until he gets them right. The drug is breaking his mind.

## Signature tech

- **Chronos:** a precognitive drug. Users experience planning as a rewind.
- **Katanas** that deflect bullets in the user's hands.
- **Military cloning and conditioning** programs.

## Materials

Pharmaceutical; exotic reagents from **Lost Island flora**.

## Risks

Addiction, madness and withdrawal. Power-granting.
