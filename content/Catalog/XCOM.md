## Highlights

## The setting

*XCOM*: an international organisation fights alien invasion by capturing and reverse-engineering alien technology. *XCOM 2* is the resistance after the aliens win.

## Signature tech

- **XCOM technology:** laser and plasma weapons, armour, skyrangers, interceptors.
- **Alien technology:** UFO power sources, elerium, alien alloys.
- **Gene mods** and **MEC troopers.**
- **Psionics:** the Psi Lab and Gift.

## Materials
