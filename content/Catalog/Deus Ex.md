## Highlights

Mechanical augmentation, nanotech augmentation, robotics and weapons.

## The setting

*Deus Ex*: augmented humans and the conspiracies around them. *Human Revolution* and *Mankind Divided* focus on mechanical augs; the original game focuses on nano-augs and the Illuminati.

## Signature tech

- **Mechanical augs:** Typhoon explosive system, Icarus landing, cloaking, social enhancer, hacking capture, arm blades.
- **Nano-augs:** nanites that rewrite the body.
- **Neuropozyne:** anti-rejection drug.
- **Robots and turrets.**

## Materials

Conventional for mechanical augs. Nanotech needs more.

## Risks

Augmentation dependency, and backdoors.
