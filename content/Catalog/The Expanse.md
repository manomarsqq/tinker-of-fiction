## Highlights

The Epstein drive, the protomolecule, and the hard-SF tech of a colonised solar system.

## The setting

*The Expanse*: humanity has settled the solar system. Earth, Mars and the Belt are on the edge of war when the **protomolecule** (an alien technology that hijacks life to build things) is found, and it builds a ring gate to a thousand other worlds.

## Signature tech

- **Epstein drive:** an efficient fusion drive that made the solar system reachable.
- **Point defence cannons (PDCs)** and **railguns.**
- **Martian power armour** (Goliath).
- **Juice:** drugs for surviving high-g burns.
- **The protomolecule** and the **Ring Gates**.

## Materials

The Epstein drive is achievable with fusion tech. The protomolecule is **hazardous**.
