## Highlights

Transhumanism: backups, swappable bodies ("morphs"), nanofabrication and farcasting.

## The setting

*Eclipse Phase*: after the Fall, when rogue AIs (the TITANs) nearly destroyed humanity, survivors are scattered across the solar system. Minds are software, bodies are morphs, and death is an inconvenience if you have a backup.

## Signature tech

- **Cortical stacks** and **backups.**
- **Morphs:** biomorphs, synthmorphs, pods.
- **Nanofabricators ("makers"):** build anything from raw feedstock.
- **Farcasting:** transmitting minds by radio.
- **Muse:** personal AI assistants.

## Materials

Nanofabrication feedstock: **Labyrinthos ore** for exotics.

## Risks

The TITANs. The **Exsurgent virus**, a basilisk hack that rewrites minds. Hazardous.
