## Highlights

## The setting

*Cyberpunk 2077*: Night City in 2077 is run by megacorps like Arasaka and Militech. Mercenaries install chrome, jack into the Net, and chase legends. V ends up with the digital ghost of Johnny Silverhand in their head.

## Signature tech

- **Cyberware:** Sandevistan (time slowing), Mantis blades, Gorilla Arms, Monowire, Projectile Launch System, Kerenzikov, Kiroshi optics.
- **Netrunning:** quickhacks through the Net (short circuit, cyberpsychosis, suicide).
- **Smart weapons** with guided bullets.
- **The Relic:** a biochip that stores a personality engram.
- **Braindances:** recorded experiences.
