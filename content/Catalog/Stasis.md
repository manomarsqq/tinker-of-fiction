## Highlights

Cryostasis, the Groomlake's genetic experiments, and the horrors of the Cayne Corporation.

## The setting

*STASIS*: in 2155, John Maracheck wakes from cryosleep aboard the **Groomlake**, a derelict research vessel orbiting Neptune, and searches for his wife and daughter. The Cayne Corporation used the ship for genetic experiments: cloning, hybridisation and the "Lazarus" program to cheat death.

## Signature tech

- **Cryostasis pods:** long-term suspended animation.
- **Cloning vats** and **hybridisation labs.**
- **Neural storage** of the dead.
- **Biomechanical creatures** from the experiments.
- **PDA logs** everywhere: the Groomlake is a haunted archive.

## Materials
