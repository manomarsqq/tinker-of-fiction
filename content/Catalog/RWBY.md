## The setting

*RWBY*: on Remnant, Huntsmen and Huntresses fight the Creatures of Grimm with **Aura** (the soul's shield), **Semblances** (personal powers) and **mechashift weapons** that transform between forms. **Dust** crystals are the world's energy source.

## Signature tech

- **Mechashift weapons:** Crescent Rose (scythe-sniper), Ember Celica, Myrtenaster.
- **Dust:** elemental crystals for fire, ice, lightning, gravity.
- **Atlesian tech:** airships, Paladins, androids (Penny).
- **Aura unlocking.**
