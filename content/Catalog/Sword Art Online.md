## The setting

*Sword Art Online*: Akihiko Kayaba's **NerveGear** traps 10,000 players in a death game. Later arcs explore the Seed, the Underworld Project and **fluctlights** (quantum souls).

## Signature tech

- **NerveGear / AmuSphere:** full-dive VR headsets.
- **The Seed:** a VR-world engine.
- **Soul Translator (STL):** reading and copying fluctlights.
- **Bottom-up AI** (Alice).
