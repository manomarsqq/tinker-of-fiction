## Highlights

Assembly, refineries, jump drives, gravity generators, and building ships block by block.

## The setting

*Space Engineers*: a sandbox of engineering in space and on planets. Engineers mine asteroids, refine ore, assemble components, and build ships and stations block by block, with physics that makes bad designs fall apart.

## Signature tech

- **Assemblers** and **refineries.**
- **Jump drives:** short-range FTL.
- **Gravity generators** and **artificial mass.**
- **Programmable blocks** for automation.
- **Welders and grinders:** build and deconstruct anything.
- **Ion, atmospheric and hydrogen thrusters.**
