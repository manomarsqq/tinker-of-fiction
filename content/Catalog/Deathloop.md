## Highlights

Time loops, the anomaly that sustains them, and Slabs that grant powers.

## The setting

*Deathloop*: the island of Blackreef is trapped in a single day that resets at midnight, sustained by a mysterious anomaly. The Visionaries who run it party forever. Colt wakes up and tries to break the loop.

## Signature tech

- **The loop:** a whole island reset each night.
- **Slabs:** artefacts granting powers like Shift (teleport), Aether (invisibility), Karnesis (telekinesis), Nexus (linking enemies), Havoc.
- **Residuum:** a substance that preserves items across loops.

## Materials

Anomalous: **Gate salvage** or **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

Time manipulation. Slabs are power-granting.
