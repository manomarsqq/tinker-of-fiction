## Highlights

Third Energy, and a time-rift experiment that pulled dinosaurs into the present.

## The setting

*Dino Crisis*: on Ibis Island, Dr Kirk's **Third Energy** research (a clean energy source) tears open a rift in time, and dinosaurs flood the facility. Special forces operative Regina has to get out alive.

## Signature tech

- **Third Energy:** a power source drawn from a rift in space-time.
- **Time-rift generators.**
- **Anaesthetic weapons** and dinosaur-grade firearms.
- **Recovery and stasis systems** for large animals.

## Materials

Third Energy needs exotic physics: **Gate salvage** or **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

Opening rifts in time in a world whose dimensional walls are already failing.
