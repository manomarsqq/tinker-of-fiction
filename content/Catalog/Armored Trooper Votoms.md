## Highlights

Armored Troopers: small, cheap, disposable mechs. The infantry of mechs.

## The setting

*Armored Trooper VOTOMS*: after a hundred-year war between the Gilgamesk and Balarant, Chirico Cuvie, a veteran AT pilot, is betrayed by his own side and hunted across the galaxy. VOTOMS is the grittiest real-robot anime: mechs are disposable and so are pilots.

## Signature tech

- **Armored Troopers (ATs):** four-metre mechs like the Scopedog, with roller-dash feet and turn-pick spikes.
- **Polymer Ringer Solution (PR liquid):** the fluid that drives AT muscles; it degrades and must be changed.
- **Mission disks:** programmed combat routines.
- **Heavy machine guns, solid shooters, missile pods.**
