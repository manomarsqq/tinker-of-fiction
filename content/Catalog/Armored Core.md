## Highlights

Fully customisable mechs built from interchangeable parts, for mercenary work.

## The setting

*Armored Core*: in a future of corporate wars, mercenary pilots called Ravens fight in **Armored Cores**, modular mechs they rebuild for every job. The series spans underground cities, Kojima-particle wars, and the Coral of Rubicon.

## Signature tech

- **Armored Cores:** swappable legs (biped, reverse-joint, quad, tank), cores, arms, heads, generators, boosters and weapons.
- **Overed Boost** and **Assault Armor.**
- **Kojima particles:** an energy source with lethal contamination.
- **Coral** (Armored Core VI): an energy substance that burns.
- **Mass-produced MTs.**

## Materials

Mostly conventional; Kojima particles and Coral need **Endbringer tissue** or similar.

## Risks

Kojima contamination.
