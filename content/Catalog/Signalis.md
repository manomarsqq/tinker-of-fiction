## Highlights

Replikas: android workers with implanted human memories, and an FTL-capable totalitarian state.

## The setting

*Signalis*: in a totalitarian interstellar Eusan Nation, **Replikas** (androids with imprinted human personalities) serve alongside humans. On a derelict mining colony, a Replika searches for her lost partner as the other Replikas succumb to a corruption spreading through them.

## Signature tech

- **Replikas:** androids imprinted with a human "persona" (STAR, KLBR, LSTR, FKLR models).
- **Persona imprinting.**
- **Protektor** interrogation.
- **FTL penrose ships.**

## Materials

Replika production is advanced but achievable. The corruption is **hazardous**.

## Risks
