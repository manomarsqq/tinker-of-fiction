## Highlights

Salvage and cutting tools for tearing apart spaceships piece by piece.

## The setting

*Hardspace: Shipbreaker*: you're a salvage worker for the Lynx Corporation, in debt from day one, cutting decommissioned spaceships apart in orbit and sending the pieces to barge, furnace or processor.

## Signature tech

- **Cutter:** a precision tool for severing joints and cutting hull.
- **Grapple:** tether and pull pieces in zero-G.
- **Demo charges.**
- **Thrusters** and **EVA suits.**
- **Scanning tools** that identify every component.
