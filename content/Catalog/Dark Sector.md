## Highlights

## The setting

*Dark Sector*: a covert operative in the fictional Soviet state of Lasria is infected with the Technocyte virus, which mutates people into monsters. His arm mutates into a weapon instead, and grows a three-bladed throwing weapon called the Glaive.

## Signature tech

- **Technocyte virus:** mutagenic; usually monstrous, occasionally empowering.
- **The Glaive:** a three-bladed returning weapon that can be charged with fire, ice or electricity and steered mid-flight.
- **Shielding and cloaking** from the infected arm.

## Materials

Viral: **Endbringer tissue** as a biological base.

## Risks

Infection. Power-granting by mutation.
