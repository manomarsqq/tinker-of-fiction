## Highlights

Steam balls, flying steam castles and Victorian super-engineering.

## The setting

*Steamboy*: in Victorian Britain, a boy inventor, Ray Steam, receives the **steam ball**, a device containing high-pressure steam, and is caught between his father's steam castle and the O'Hara Foundation.

## Signature tech

- **The steam ball:** a compact, enormous power source.
- **The Steam Tower / Castle:** a flying fortress.
- **Steam-powered troopers and jet packs.**

## Materials

Pure-mineral water at impossible pressure: **Labyrinthos ore** for containment.

## Risks

The steam ball is a bomb if it ruptures.
