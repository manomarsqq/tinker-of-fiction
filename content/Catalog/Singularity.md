## The setting

*Singularity*: on Katorga-12, a Soviet island, research into **Element 99 (E99)** led to a catastrophe in 1955. An American pilot uses the **TMD** to age and restore objects and people across time.

## Signature tech

- **TMD (Time Manipulation Device):** ages or reverts objects and people; creates time bubbles.
- **E99:** an element with time-bending properties.
- **The Singularity Bomb.**
