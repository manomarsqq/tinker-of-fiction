## Highlights

Engineering tools as weapons, RIG suits, stasis, kinesis, and the Markers.

## The setting

*Dead Space*: Isaac Clarke, an engineer, survives necromorph outbreaks triggered by the **Markers**, alien signal-emitting monoliths that drive people mad and turn the dead into monsters.

## Signature tech

- **Plasma cutter, line gun, ripper, pulse rifle, contact beam:** mining tools used as weapons.
- **RIG suits:** engineering suits with health displays and vacuum sealing.
- **Stasis module:** slows objects in time.
- **Kinesis module:** telekinetic grabbing and throwing.
- **The Markers.**

## Materials
