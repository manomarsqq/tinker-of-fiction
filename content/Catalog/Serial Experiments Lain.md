## Highlights

The Wired, Psyche processors, and the boundary between the network and the world.

## The setting

*Serial Experiments Lain*: Lain Iwakura, a withdrawn schoolgirl, gets a new Navi (computer) and is drawn into the **Wired**, a global network that's starting to leak into reality. The Knights, a hacker group, and Masami Eiri, who wants to be the Wired's god, are in the middle of it.

## Signature tech

- **Navis:** personal computers that connect to the Wired.
- **Psyche processor:** a chip that links the brain to the Wired.
- **Protocol 7:** a network protocol that erases the line between online and offline.
- **KIDS:** a psychic amplification project.

## Materials

Conventional computing; Protocol 7 needs something stranger. **Shard Dungeon elements**.

## Risks

Protocol 7 dissolves the boundary between minds and the network.
