## Highlights

BOR parasites, Blood Codes and immortal Revenants.

## The setting

*Code Vein*: after a catastrophe, Revenants (the dead revived by BOR parasites) survive in a ruined city. They're immortal and strong, but they need blood, and without it they become Lost: mindless monsters.

## Signature tech

- **BOR parasites:** revive the dead and grant Revenant powers.
- **Blood Codes:** swappable sets of abilities, drawn from other Revenants' memories.
- **Blood Veils:** living weapons worn on the body that drain enemies.
- **Blood Beads:** synthetic blood to hold off the hunger.

## Materials

Parasites and blood science: **Endbringer tissue** or **Lost Island flora**.

## Risks

Power-granting, with vampiric hunger and a very ugly failure state.
