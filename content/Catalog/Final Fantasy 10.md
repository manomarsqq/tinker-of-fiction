## The setting

*Final Fantasy X*: Spira's people live under Yevon's teaching that machina are forbidden, while Sin (a colossal monster) destroys anyone who uses them. Summoners call Aeons from the dreams of the dead Fayth.

- **Spheres:** crystals that record images and store power.
- **Sphere grid** growth.
- **Blitzball stadium tech:** water held in a sphere.
