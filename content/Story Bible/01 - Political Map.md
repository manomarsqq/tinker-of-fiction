# 01 - Political Map

Real-world borders are background. Earth Bet's power map is made of **organizations**, not territory.

## The organizing fact

**Every major power is acting on assumptions that were true in 2000 and aren't now.** The PRT thinks it has a monopoly. Cauldron thinks it has a monopoly. Vought thinks Compound V is a secret. The Yangban thinks its model scales. None of them are stupid, and all of them are wrong.

> [!note] Correction from the session
> The crisis is **epistemic, not competitive**. The PRT never had a monopoly and was never meant to: Cauldron funds the Guild, the Suits and their equivalents worldwide because a broad, varied cape ecosystem is the goal. Vought and Sky High are more of the same. What the PRT is losing is the ability to understand what's in its own files.

## The equilibrium

Three-gang stability, held up by mutual deterrence, profit, the unwritten rules, and Truce familiarity: Kaiser, Lung and Armsmaster have stood in the same mud, and that changes how willing you are to burn a man's house down.

**What breaks it:** Bakuda in May · Leviathan on the 15th · whatever Coil has been building that nobody thought to ask him about.

> [!warning] Updated 2026-09-26
> The equilibrium is **nearly gone**. On 8 April 2011 Brockton Bay is one issue away from civil war: the gangs, the neighbourhoods and City Hall are all at breaking point at once. The issue comes on **2 May**, when Winslow High falls into the Labyrinthos and every student comes out with powers. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].
