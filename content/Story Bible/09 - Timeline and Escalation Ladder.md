# 09 - Timeline and Escalation Ladder

## Deep background
- **Always there:** the occult layer (vampires, ghosts, demons, fae, Mimics, Curses, the Labyrinthos, Dante's afterlife, the Lament Configuration). Hidden, denied, never verified.
- **The sorcerer tradition:** old family lines that found awakened children and taught them. Worked for centuries.
- **All For One:** granting and taking powers for generations, long before parahumans.
- **Vought's century:** Compound V developed in the **1940s–50s**. Vought built a business on supes decades before Scion.

## The escalation ladder
✅ *User-approved.* About **one world-altering event every 6–8 weeks for thirty months.**

### 2011
| When | Event |
|---|---|
| April–May | Bakuda arrives · Coil takes Dinah · *the protagonist is active by now* |
| **05-02** | **THE POWDER KEG.** A powers-out brawl at Winslow High tears open **the first public Gate**, which drops the whole school into **the Labyrinthos**. Every student comes out with powers (shard and shardless). The entrance never closes. Brockton Bay tips into civil war. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]] *(user ruling; date proposed)* |
| 05-03 | Civil Defense Ordinance vote, in the panic *(proposed)* |
| **05-15** | **Leviathan at Brockton Bay. The [[Harbingers]] debut.** Twelve of them. The Truce holds; the doctrine doesn't |
| 06 | **First [[Lesser Endbringers\|Lesser Endbringer]] attack, and somebody kills it.** The hope bubble inflates to bursting |
| 07 | **Gates spread.** After Winslow, the second and third public Gates open elsewhere. The first is filed as a Master with constructs; by the third, the framework fails in public |
| 08 | **Vought's salvage bill passes**, covering "dimensional anomalies". Licensed Gate extraction begins |
| 09 | **[[The Labyrinthos]] is officially recognised.** Entrances worldwide make "a Shaker construct" impossible to say. Winslow was the first. The entrances don't close |
| 10 | **[[Lord Zedd]]'s first strike.** A monster grows to building height on live TV. The classification system fails irreversibly |
| 11–12 | **The [[Beasts of Entropy]] break through.** Regions fall. The cults explode, and "Blessed" enters ordinary speech |
