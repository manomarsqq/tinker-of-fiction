# 06 - Culture

## The organizing fact

**Earth Bet is a machine for making randomness mean something.** A shard picks you and picks a shape, and the shape has no meaning. Every culture on the planet exists partly to deny that. The PRT has classifications, PHO has power rankings, the cults have divine gifts, the Fallen have worship, the diaspora has fate and water, and the families have bloodline. Ultimate Talent culture has **merit**: the only honest framework, which is exactly why it's so admired.

**What's being denied:** that the worst thing that ever happened to you was a coin flip, thrown by something that doesn't know you exist.

---

## Cape professional culture
Heroes and villains together, shaped by the Truce, masks and shared funerals.
- **Proverbs:** *Masks up, names down.* · *You'll meet them in the mud.* · *Nobody retires loud.* · *A win you can't repeat is a coincidence.* · *Count the exits before the cameras.*
- **Metaphors:** weather and triage for fights; theatre for public life (billing, staging, who gets the close-up).
- **Rituals:** a nod, no names; naming someone in public is an assault. Deals are spoken aloud and both sides repeat the **limits** back. Nothing is written, ever. Challenge the plan, never the person.
- **Formality:** steep and inverted. Capes by cape name; civilian names are never spoken. Tenure outranks power.
- **Taboos:** never speak a civilian identity · **never ask where someone triggered** · never say "worth it" about an Endbringer fight.
- *"Never ask where someone triggered" is why nobody in this world notices the pattern sitting in plain sight.*

## PHO
The nervous system of cape culture: wiki, gossip, rankings, versus threads. News breaks here before the PRT confirms it. *(PHO Sunday: important characters post under telling usernames.)*
- **Proverbs:** *Pics or it didn't happen.* · *Read the Wiki first.* · *Don't feed the Void.* · *Tier lists are not arguments.*
- **Style:** capes discussed as athletes. No greeting; you're a username arriving mid-argument. The Wiki is cited like scripture.
- **The line:** doxxing is a permaban and a felony. On identity protection, an internet forum and the US government agree completely.
- **The Void joke:** [[Greg Veder]] posted the truth, with evidence, repeatedly. The joke ate the fact.

## The Kyushu diaspora / the Docks
Twelve years and two generations on. Grief that has become structure.
- **Proverbs:** *The water remembers.* · *We were fishermen.* · *Count the ones who came.* · *Ask the tide, not the almanac.*
- **Rituals:** the greeting asks where someone's family was *from* (really, *were they there*). Funerals without bodies. A date in 1999 the rest of the city doesn't notice.
- **Taboos:** don't ask what someone lost · don't say "moved on" · don't romanticize the sea.
- The generations who remember Kyushu and the ones who don't are functionally different castes.

## Cult culture
Powers **mean** something. A gift implies a giver; a giver implies intent; intent implies obligation.
- **Proverbs:** *The Blessed walk among us.* · *Devotion is the only currency that appreciates.* · *Ask what the gift is for.* · *Nothing is given without a reason.*
- **Rituals:** witnessing (recounting a cape sighting as formal testimony) · calling a cape **Blessed** to their face, which capes find deeply unsettling · scrupulous courtesy between cults.
- **Heresy:** saying a power is random.
- *They offer a world where your trigger event was FOR something. The PRT can't offer that.*

## The Flame families
Generations of hiding a real power under a fake one.
- **Proverbs:** *The flame is quiet.* · *A doctor you didn't choose is an enemy you haven't met.* · *Say cape.* · *Burn clean or don't burn.*
- **Rituals:** the ring at coming of age · the lie rehearsal, drilled from childhood · never light where a camera might be.
- **Taboos:** never light a Flame before an outsider · never see a doctor outside the family · never use the English word for it.

## Winslow / teenage Brockton Bay
- **Proverbs:** *Go Ward.* · *One more week of this and I'm gonna trigger.* · *That's capeshit.* · *Nobody's coming.*
- **Style:** territorial before social: *which way did you come in, whose blocks did you cross.* Cape knowledge is currency.
- **The sentence Winslow is built on:** kids joke about triggering. It's a joke about wanting something unbearable to happen to you, because a power is the only exit anyone can name.

## Other cultural facts
- **Cape celebrity:** merchandising, action figures, PR teams. To teenagers, heroism looks like a career path.
- **Ultimate Talents** *(Danganronpa)*: a celebrated meritocracy next to an arbitrary one. It quietly grates on every parahuman alive.
- **Comic Book Pretty:** everyone is a tier more attractive. Beauty no longer marks status, and the people it skips (the Case 53s) are conspicuous.
- **"Huh, isn't that...":** familiar faces from other fiction live ordinary, unpowered lives. *(A Tinker of Fiction would notice every one of them.)*
- **Holidays:** the Kyushu anniversary (diaspora only) · Endbringer memorial days, local to each city. The calendar is filling up.
