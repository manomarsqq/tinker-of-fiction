# 05 - Economy

## Deglobalization

Leviathan, Reemergence marine megafauna and the Kyushu subsidence have made **bulk sea freight actuarially uninsurable**, and ~90% of world trade by volume moved by sea. Nations have retreated to continental self-sufficiency. Resource wealth isn't leverage anymore; it's survival.

- **Winners:** continental states with domestic energy, ore and grain (USA, Russia, China, Brazil, Australia). Rail, trucking.
- **Losers:** island and coastal economies. Japan lost Kyushu, then lost the ability to import food.
- **Replacements:** air freight (brutal cost), Tinkertech transport (bespoke, dies with its maker), requisitioned Movers (resented).
- **Cape transport is infrastructure**, which is why the Requisition Clause exists.

## The hope bubble

The new age of heroes has restored **confidence** far faster than **capacity**. Markets price in a safety the physical economy can't deliver. Insurance is written against Endbringer frequencies a decade out of date. Cities rebuild where they shouldn't, because [[All Might]] smiled on television.

**The correction**, when it comes, is financial as well as physical: instantaneous and global. It inflates in **June 2011** (the first Lesser kill) and **pops in 2012-Q2** (the Legions).

## The Merchant Markets

The biggest business in Brockton Bay, and nobody knows. In a world where legitimate shipping doesn't work, mobile illegitimate logistics is worth more than the cargo. They out-earn [[Empire 88]] and the [[ABB]] **combined** in liquid, usable money. The PRT files them as a drug gang. See [[The Merchants]].

## The Gate rush *(from May 2011: Winslow; widespread from July)*
When Gates go public, a genuine gold rush, but only a few can go in and come back. Governments rush through **licensed corporate Gate extraction**: companies hiring capes for resource recovery. A legitimate, better-paying private sector competes directly with the Protectorate. Rogues become economically central. [[Faultline's Crew]] and [[Toybox]] move first.

> A Tinker of Fiction is both a **buyer** (of exotic materials) and **unsellable** (their tech dies with them). The Gate rush is a job market built for them. *(proposed)*
