# 08 - Geography and the Second Layer

## The organizing fact

**Earth has grown a second layer of geography** (Gate-realms and the Labyrinthos), **and the Endbringers have colonized it.** The Endbringer problem and the Gate problem are the same problem, and nobody on Earth knows that either exists in that form.

## The second layer

- **[[The Gate-Realms]]:** tears into the debris of dead worlds, stabilized into pocket realms. **Zero public Gates at anchor.** Some open onto Legion nurseries.
- **[[The Labyrinthos]]:** a dimension of shifting tunnels between worlds. Its natives hate everyone, which limits how deep the Endbringers can colonize. Dead-world debris is inert; the Labyrinthos fights back.
- **Nurseries:** hard to find, possible to destroy, and destroying one really does cut Legion output.
- **Shard Dungeons** *(modifier)*: underground dungeons maintained by a special caste of shards.

## New terrain

- **[[The Lost Island]]** (South Pacific): its concealment is failing *now*, as part of the same destabilization that's making the Gates. Megafauna and flora with strange properties.
- **Reemergence chasms** (caves and seafloor worldwide): see [[Reemergence Megafauna]].
- **Nemesis spore deposits:** don't exist yet. Once she attacks, the supply is the ruins of every city she's hit.

## The oceans
**Dying as a transit medium**, killed by Leviathan, marine megafauna and the Kyushu subsidence. This is the direct cause of dead port economies, Brockton Bay's [[The Boat Graveyard|Boat Graveyard]] included.

## The supernatural layer
Invisible to institutions: [[Curses]] (densest at schools, hospitals, graveyards, and Brockton Bay) · [[Mimics]] · [[The Supernatural|the Horror Bet bestiary]] · [[He Who Waits Behind the Wall]] · the [[The Cenobites|Lament Configuration]] · Dante's Three Kingdoms.

## First contact rule ✅
Nothing in this build has ever been verified by Earth Bet: no alien contact, no authenticated supernatural encounter, no Gate, no Labyrinthos expedition, no Curse, no Mimic, no Returnee on record. The PRT's threat model is canon-accurate and about to be catastrophically wrong.

**Exceptions:** terrestrial-human phenomena are established (Ultimate Talents, the *CAN YOU DIG IT* gangs, cults). **Dying Will Flames** are terrestrial but deliberately suppressed.
