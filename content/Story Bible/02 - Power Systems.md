# 02 - Power Systems

## The organizing fact

Earth Bet runs **at least a dozen unrelated sources** of superhuman ability, and the cape framework has absorbed every one. That absorption isn't just an intellectual mistake. For at least one of these populations, being correctly diagnosed is the difference between living and dying.

## Understood

| System | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Shards** | Trigger events, canon rules. Conflict drive present but **weak**. |
| **Tinkertech** | **Non-transferable, non-replicable, dies with its maker.** Every tinker death is permanent infrastructure loss. |
| **Cauldron vials** | Finite, drawn from a corpse, priced in obligations. |

**The Brute floor** *(Brute modifier)*: every parahuman survives low-calibre fire unharmed, and natural Brutes are stronger. No ordinary police officer can arrest any cape, ever. PRT foam and specialist munitions are the only things that work, which is the structural basis of the PRT.

**Gravity!** Capes physically gravitate toward each other, more strongly between comparable power levels. That's why Brockton Bay's cape density is absurd.

**None of these appear in Cauldron's model of the world, or in Scion's.**

### Why nobody has noticed the corona loophole
Every shardless population has **its own doctor**, for ordinary reasons (liability, discipline, secrecy), so no corona scan ever enters a PRT system. At Endbringer fights, Vought triages Vought supes with Vought staff, and has for thirty years. **The arrangement breaks on 15 May 2011**, if Vought's medical team is overwhelmed and a supe ends up in a Protectorate bay.

## Cursed energy

**Exists. Largely unknown. Lethal.** *(Age of Curses, Jujutsu Kaisen.)*

- **Source:** ambient negative human emotion. **Awakening** is almost always traumatic, which is exactly why it's mistaken for a trigger.
- **Grants:** body reinforcement, an innate cursed technique, and the ability to **see [[Curses]]**, which nothing else on Earth can do.
- **Only cursed energy harms a Curse.** Using it is a **beacon** that draws them in.
- **Needs a teacher.** There are almost none. **Median survival: months.**

**The catastrophe:** the cape era destroyed the sorcerer tradition. Before 1982, a child who saw things found [[The Old Network]]. Since 1982 that child thinks they triggered, gets filed as an unreliable Thinker, and dies. The PRT has spent thirty years intercepting and killing sorcerers, *kindly*, with a stipend and a costume. The pattern is sitting in its own database, and nobody has ever run that query.

**Brockton Bay** is the densest Curse hotspot in North America. It produces the most awakenings and kills them fastest.

## Occult practice

Fringe, real, and effective **within its own domain**: voodoo, witchcraft, kabbalah, paganism, exorcists, monster hunters, and underfunded government divisions. Works on the [[The Supernatural|Horror Bet bestiary]] and [[Mimics]]. **Useless** against capes, Endbringers and Curses.

Earth Bet has **two** separate hidden communities fighting invisible monsters with incompatible toolkits, and neither knows the other exists.

## Labyrinthos-changed *(from 2 May 2011)*

*(user ruling, 2026-09-26)* When [[The Winslow Gate|Winslow falls into the Labyrinthos]] ([[14 - The Powder Keg]]), every student comes out with powers, by **a mix** of two routes:

- **Triggers.** Three days of terror, injury and entrapment: ordinary shard triggers, hundreds of them.
- **Changes.** Exposure to the Labyrinthos itself (its air, its ore, native flesh, cursed loot) changes people. These powers are **shardless**: no corona, invisible to shard Thinkers and to Scion.

Nobody can tell which is which without a scan. The PRT registers them all as capes anyway. This is a new shardless population; the separate ruling that the [[Beasts of Entropy]] bring no shardless class still stands.
