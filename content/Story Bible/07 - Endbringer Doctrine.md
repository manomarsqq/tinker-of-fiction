# 07 - Endbringer Doctrine

## The organizing fact

**Endbringer doctrine at the anchor date is mature, correct, and has five weeks to live.** Thirty years of response have produced something genuinely good: Dragon's tracking and alert network, the Truce, an understood rotation, triage protocols, evacuation timings, and grim but accurate casualty expectations. It works as well as anything can against **three** Endbringers. Every assumption in it is about to fail at once.

## The two schools

**Primary: attrition and evacuation.** Mass capes, rotate hard, screen civilians out, accept losing the city. Legend leads because he's honest about what the job is. The metric is bodies saved, not damage dealt.

**Minority: decapitation.** [[Armsmaster]], [[Dragon]], and a scattering of others. The cores can be reached and killed, but it needs Sting-class capability nobody reliably has. They're treated as cranks with a hobby, and they're about to be the only people who were thinking about the right problem.

**Schools that don't exist yet:** Harbinger-first · Lesser-hunting.

## Killability ✅
Endbringers **can be killed**, by two routes: firepower that scours the planetary surface, or a power that ignores layered defenses entirely (Sting-class, All-or-Nothing). Untethered: [[Eidolon]] can't switch them off.

> [!workshop] The Catalog and killability *(proposed)*
> Both routes exist in fiction. **Planet-scouring firepower:** Halo, Warhammer 40k, Stellaris, The Culture, Homeworld. **Layer-ignoring weapons:** many settings have something that cuts through any defense. A Tinker of Fiction may be the first person who can *actually build* the decapitation school's weapon. That's a huge lever, and it needs a ruling on how far the power scales.

## PRT doctrine against capes
Built on the **Brute floor**. A squad evacuates, contains and foams; it doesn't fight. Every trooper knows their rifle is a prop against anything with a corona, and that grinds on an organization that keeps asking them to stand in front of things anyway.

## Conventional militaries
- **The nuclear question:** closed, and everyone pretends it's open. Behemoth eats energy; it was tried and made things worse.
- **Actual jobs:** the Ellisburg wall, the Machine Army perimeter.
- **Against Endbringers:** traffic control with tanks.

## The Yangban
An army against a profession. A hundred operatives running the same five powers: drilled, coordinated, no egos, complete willingness to spend lives. Their Trumps temporarily boost allied capes, which is the real reason nations invite them.

## The vacuum
**Unknown at anchor:** [[Harbingers]] · Legions · [[Lesser Endbringers]] · the five charged Endbringers · anything that comes through a Gate. No threat rating, no playbook, and no framework for an enemy that arrives through a hole in the world instead of walking to you.

**First contact behaviour:** the first Dungeon Break will be filed as a Master with constructs, a Shaker event, or an unregistered tinker, for at least the first three incidents. *Because it always is.*

**Lesser morale:** when Lessers appear, they **die**. It's the first clean win against anything with that silhouette. Nobody has planned for what that does to morale, or for what people will then try against the real ones.

## Debut: 15 May 2011, Brockton Bay
Leviathan, with **twelve Harbingers**. Every cape on that beach arrives expecting the fight they've trained thirty years for. The Truce holds. The doctrine doesn't. Casualties are far beyond canon.
