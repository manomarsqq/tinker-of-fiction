# 13 - Arc Outline

> [!warning] Everything on this page is *(proposed)*
> The world has a fixed escalation ladder ([[09 - Timeline and Escalation Ladder]]). The story doesn't exist yet. These arcs just cut the ladder into story-sized pieces so there's something to push against. Rename, merge or throw them away.

## Arc 1: Working Explanation (8 April → 1 May 2011)
**Span:** anchor date to the eve of the Powder Keg. **Brockton Bay.**
- Leet's first catalogs, first builds, first stunts. The PRT files him as a "versatile Tinker".
- Canon pressure: Taylor goes out on 8 April · Lung · the Undersiders · Coil and Dinah · Bakuda on the way.
- The city tightens: the Heights, Little Saigon, Southside, the roads. The Ordinance vote is set for 3 May. See [[14 - The Powder Keg#The kindling Brockton Bay on 8 April]].
- Leet starts recognising fiction in the world: an expy, a Vought supe, Void_Cowboy's posts.
- **Ends:** Monday morning, 2 May. School.

## Arc 2: The Powder Keg (2 → 14 May 2011)
**The Winslow Gate.** *(user ruling)* A powers-out brawl in the cafeteria; the first public Gate; the whole school drops into the Labyrinthos. Joshua and Robyn are inside.
- Three days underground with the entire student body. Enemies fight side by side. Everyone comes out with powers.
- The Ordinance vote in the panic. Every faction goes after the Winslow kids. **Civil war.**
- Leet's biggest points payout, in a mine of substitute materials, with his canon map now useless.
- **Ends:** Dragon's models narrow the next Endbringer attack to Brockton Bay.

## Arc 3: Harbingers (15 May 2011)
**Leviathan and twelve Harbingers**, landing on a city already at war with itself, with a permanent Labyrinthos entrance in the middle of it. The doctrine dies on the beach.
- The corona loophole may crack if a Vought supe ([[Mainstay]]?) ends up in a Protectorate triage bay.
- [[The Ferrymen]]'s knowledge of the Boat Graveyard suddenly matters.
- Endbringer and Harbinger tissue becomes available on the ground. *(A Tinker of Fiction with [[Kaiju No. 8]] or [[Pacific Rim]] in reach...)*

## Arc 4: Gate Rush (June → September 2011)
The first Lesser kill (who kills it?) · Gates spreading · Vought's salvage bill · licensed extraction · the Labyrinthos officially recognised.
- The material ladder opens: Gate salvage from dead worlds, *"some of it from settings people would recognize."*
- [[Toybox]], [[Faultline's Crew]] and Vought all want what the MC can do.

## Arc 5: Unfileable (October → December 2011)
[[Lord Zedd]] on live TV · the [[Beasts of Entropy]] · regions falling · "Blessed" · [[Saint Anne's]] turning into a cult.

## Arc 6: The Pop (2012)
[[Nemesis]] · the Rangers · **the Legions fulcrum** · Vulcan and Acedia · the Lanterns · the Returnees. All Might holds.

## Arc 7: Golden Morning (2013)
All eight Endbringers · Tithonus detonates every secret · the Variants · Drakkon · **Scion**, meeting a world that accidentally built the only weapon that works.
