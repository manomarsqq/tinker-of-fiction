# 14 - The Powder Keg

> [!warning] What it is
> **The Powder Keg is an event** *(user ruling, 2026-09-26)*. In **May 2011, before Leviathan**, the first public Gate opens beneath **[[Winslow High School]]** in the middle of a violent, powers-out brawl between students, and drops the entire school into the **[[The Labyrinthos|Labyrinthos]]**. The students fight their way out. **Every single one of them comes out with powers**, some by triggering and some changed by the Labyrinthos. Brockton Bay, already one issue away from civil war, gets its issue.
>
> Details marked *(proposed)* are Claude's; the rest is your ruling.

## The rulings

- **When:** May 2011, **before Leviathan**. *(proposed date: **Monday 2 May 2011**, the day before the Civil Defense Ordinance vote)*
- **What:** a **Gate that opens into the Labyrinthos**. It's the world's **first public Gate** and the first undeniable proof of the Labyrinthos. The school building is gone. **The entrance never closes.**
- **The powers:** **a mix.** Some students trigger (shards) from the trauma. Others are **changed by the Labyrinthos** (shardless: no corona, invisible to Thinkers and Scion). Nobody can tell which is which without a scan.
- **Who's inside:** the whole student body, including **[[Leet|Joshua]] and [[Uber|Robyn]]**, who are both Winslow students.

## The kindling: Brockton Bay on 8 April

Before the event, the city is already **one issue away from civil war**: three layers of conflict stacked up, each near breaking.

1. **The gangs.** Empire, ABB, Merchants and Coil, plus a dozen smaller crews that now have capes of their own.
2. **The neighbourhoods.** The North End, the Heights, the Docks, Little Saigon, Southside and the Eastside have each armed themselves against a specific enemy.
3. **The city itself.** [[Brockton First]] wants to legalise the anti-cape militia [[The Breakwater]]; the council votes on the **Civil Defense Ordinance** on **Tuesday 3 May 2011**.

Winslow is where all of it sits in one building. Its students are the gangs' sons and daughters, the Lamplighters' kids, Iron Parish boys the Empire is recruiting, Ninety-Nine members, Wards, a Lung-tier cape nobody believes in, and a girl in her first month as a cape.

### The fault lines

- **The Heights.** The Hollowmen burned out tenants on Fairmount Street; the [[The Heights Union|Heights Union]] armed itself. A Hollowman died in March (killed by [[Bleach]], on Empire orders, to provoke exactly this). [[Whitehand]] branded a Union organiser.
- **Little Saigon.** The ABB's conscription sweeps created [[The Lamplighters]]. The ABB burned a shop in March.
- **Southside.** [[Iron Parish]] and the Empire are fighting over the Parish's sons; the [[The Breakwater|Breakwater]] patrols the same streets.
- **The roads.** [[The Rust Saints]] are hijacking Merchant convoys.
- **The water.** [[Kappa]], [[Obol]] and [[La Marea]] are fighting over the safe channels.
- **The Empire itself.** Kaiser wants City Hall; Hookwolf and [[Brünhild]] want the Docks, now.
- **The PRT itself.** [[Bulwark]] reports to the hardliners; [[Lodestar]]'s family is in the Heights Union; Piggot is sick and surrounded.

### What was still holding

1. **The unwritten rules**: no killing, no unmasking, no families.
2. **Truce familiarity**: Kaiser, Lung and Armsmaster have stood in the same mud.
3. **Neutral ground**: the Boardwalk, [[Mercy Street]]'s clinic, [[Tollkeeper]]'s bridge, Saint Anne's.
4. **Profit.**
5. **The PRT**: stretched, split and infiltrated ([[Sable]]).

The Powder Keg breaks the first one in front of fourteen hundred witnesses, and then makes the rest irrelevant.

## The event: 2 May 2011

*(sequence proposed)*

### 1. The brawl

Lunch, the cafeteria. The city's feuds are all in the room. *(proposed)* It starts between **Declan Kilbride** (an Iron Parish boy who took the Empire's white handprint a week ago), **[[Ashfall]]** (Ninety-Nine) and **Min-jun Kang** (an ABB conscript, [[Wick]]'s son). Min-jun uses his power. Ashfall answers with cinders. Within a minute there are powers going off in the cafeteria **in front of everyone**, with no regard for the unwritten rules:

- Capes who've kept their identities secret use their powers openly. [[Sophia Hess]] goes into her shadow state in front of the student body.
- [[Greg Veder]], who has been telling everyone for months, finally proves it. Nobody can call him a liar again.
- [[Taylor Hebert]], [[Emma Barnes]], [[Madison Clements]], [[Moth]], [[Uber]] and [[Leet]] are all in the building.

### 2. The cascade

The fight spreads through the building, and the power clash cascades. *(proposed)* Winslow sits above the old storm drains, near the Labyrinthos rupture [[The Undertow]] lost four people to last year. The combined powers tear the thin spot open. **A Gate opens under Winslow**, and the whole school, building, students and all, **drops into the Labyrinthos**.

### 3. The Labyrinthos

Inside, the tunnels shift when unobserved and the natives spawn from the walls and attack everything. *(proposed: the students are inside for about **three days**.)* Enemies from the cafeteria have to fight side by side or die. The powers come here:

- **Triggers.** Terror, injury, loss and entrapment: every classic trigger condition, hundreds of times over. **Shard powers.**
- **Changes.** Eating native flesh, breathing the Labyrinthos air, touching the ore in the walls, picking up cursed loot. **Shardless powers**, invisible to Thinkers and Scion.
- **A mix** that nobody can sort without a scan, and the PRT's scanners are about to be very busy.

### 4. The way out

The survivors come out scattered: some through the crater where Winslow stood, some through [[The Storm Drains]], some through entrances nobody knew were there. **Every student who comes out has powers.** *(proposed: about **1,400** students)* The building is gone. The crater is a permanent Labyrinthos entrance in the middle of Brockton Bay.

## The aftermath

- **Fourteen hundred new parahumans** in a city of 350,000, overnight, all teenagers, many traumatised, some shardless, most of them with a grudge from the cafeteria and three days of reasons.
- **Identities are gone.** Everyone at Winslow saw who has powers. The unwritten rules broke in front of the whole school.
- **The vote.** The Civil Defense Ordinance goes to the council **the next morning**, in the panic.
- **Recruitment war.** Every faction goes after the Winslow kids at once, mostly along the same lines as the brawl: the Empire for its white kids, the ABB for its conscripts, the Lamplighters and Ninety-Nine for theirs, the PRT for anyone it can register, the Breakwater for anyone it can foam, the Chorus calling it the day the angels came.
- **That's the civil war.**
- **Thirteen days later, Leviathan.** The Harbingers land on a city already fighting itself, with a permanent Labyrinthos entrance in the middle of it.

## How the world files it

The classification trap, at its limit. The PRT files the first public Gate as **a Shaker event** and fourteen hundred new capes as **a mass trigger**. The shardless kids go through the same registration as everyone else, because "it's a cape" explains everything. The Labyrinthos is officially "a Shaker construct of unknown origin" until September, when entrances elsewhere make that impossible to say with a straight face.

## Leet and the Powder Keg

- **He's inside.** Joshua and Robyn are Winslow students. For three days, Uber and Leet are in the Labyrinthos with the entire student body, and with the rest of the Bay's cape children.
- **Points.** Conflict pays, and parahumans pay more. Three days of fighting in the Labyrinthos, surrounded by capes, is the biggest points payout Leet will ever see. He can buy catalogs mid-disaster.
- **Materials.** Labyrinthos ore and native tissue are on the [[04 - Tinker of Fiction#Materials|substitute materials]] list. He's standing in the mine.
- **Foreknowledge fails.** Canon has nothing like this. From 2 May on, his map of Worm is mostly useless.
- **Exposure.** Everyone at Winslow saw who used powers. Whether they saw Uber and Leet is your call.
