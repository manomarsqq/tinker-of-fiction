# 00 - Premise & World Rules

## Premise

**Earth Bet, April 2011**, rebuilt from **Lt Ouroumov's Worm CYOA V17** (60 selections, see [[12 - CYOA Build]]). A self-insert stuck as **[[Leet]]**, whose dead Eden shard became **[[04 - Tinker of Fiction|Tinker of Fiction]]** (a Tinker whose specialty is fiction itself), is in **[[Brockton Bay]]** five weeks before Leviathan. It's a world that's quietly full of things out of fiction, and nobody has noticed, because everyone already has a working explanation.

**Genre:** superhero / cosmic horror / kitchen-sink crossover.
**Tone:** *Bigger, not grimmer.* A world at a **higher equilibrium** than canon Worm (more heroes, more hope, more confidence) with far more loaded guns. The fall is from a greater height.
**Scale:** Earth Bet, global, with Brockton Bay as the dense node.
**Tech level:** modern (2011), degraded by deglobalization, with Tinkertech as an overlay that can't scale.

## Standing rulings
✅ 
*(Confirmed in the worldbuilding session.)*

- **Mode:** hybrid. **Anchor: 8 April 2011**, just before canon opens. Scope: Earth Bet, global.
- **No Precursors: removed.** Standard Worm cosmology restored: the Entities have run many cycles, prior-cycle [DATA] exists, shards and Cauldron vials behave as canon, and [[Scion]] follows a script. Ancient factions (Guardians of the Universe, Zordon-era lore, the Super Saiyan legend, Viltrum's fallen golden age) exist normally.
- **[[Beasts of Entropy]]** arrive **late 2011**, *after* the protagonist is already active as a cape. No shardless power class comes with them.
- **"The Blessed"** is not a power category. It's a word: what the empowered get called once "cape" stops being the dominant framing. Pairs with *Cults, Cults, and More Cults!*
- **Cosmic powers** (Viltrum, the Frieza Force, the Saiyan Army) know Earth exists and don't care. Dormant pressure, not an active threat.
- **Path to Victory is NOT degrading.** Cauldron stays competent. The new power systems are blind spots, but Contessa paths around them with knowledge, the same way she handles Eidolon.
- **The March:** a fuse only. The First Sons and Ray Field research exist. No plague, no Beast yet.
- **Addons:** only ones whose prerequisites are met count. Two taken: **He Noticed**, **Broodmothers of the Apocalypse**.
- **Expy Galore override:** expies arrive **with their native powers**. The addon rule is explicitly set aside. (Dave Lizewski still has none, and still gets killed by the Empire in four minutes.)
- **All Might is at full strength.** Panacea fixes him after every Endbringer fight.
- **The conflict drive exists but is weak**: a thumb on the scale, not a leash. Capes cooperate *more* than in canon. Truces hold, and the unwritten rules have real force.
- **The Endbringer Truce is formal and universal**, and covers Lesser Endbringers.
- **Killing a Lesser Endbringer grants no protection** against the full ones. It's just a win.
- **Tattletale is 17.**
- **The Powder Keg** *(added 2026-09-26)*: in May 2011, **before Leviathan**, a powers-out brawl at [[Winslow High School]] tears open **the first public Gate**, which drops the whole school into **the Labyrinthos**. Every student comes out with powers (**a mix**: shard triggers and shardless Labyrinthos changes). The entrance never closes. [[Leet|Joshua]] and [[Uber|Robyn]] are Winslow students and are inside. Brockton Bay, already on the edge, tips into civil war. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].
- **Ben 10 is in the world** *(added 2026-09-26)*: Alien Force era, Ben is 15. [[The Plumbers]] are hidden like the other expies, so the first-contact rule still holds. Threats: [[The Highbreed]] (active, covert), [[The Forever Knights]] (active), [[Vilgax]], [[Aggregor]], [[Malware]] (reserve).

## The five themes

1. **The classification trap.** The PRT isn't blind through incompetence. It has a working explanation for everything, and *"it's a cape"* is never falsified. A Mimic gets filed as a Changer, a Flame user as a Striker, a Curse death as an unregistered Master, a vanishing as a Stranger. Case 53 absorbs the rest.
2. **Everyone already has a working explanation.** Institutional (the PRT taxonomy), social (*"Void_Cowboy is full of shit"*), religious (*powers mean something*). Every one of them works, and every one of them is catastrophically wrong.
3. **First contact hasn't happened.** Nothing extradimensional or non-human has ever been verified. Institutional knowledge: zero. Fringe knowledge: real, unverifiable, and career-ending.
4. **A machine for making randomness mean something.** Every culture on Earth Bet exists partly to deny that powers are arbitrary. See [[06 - Culture]].
5. **The hope bubble.** Confidence has recovered far faster than capacity, and the gap is the largest unpriced risk on Earth. See [[05 - Economy]].

> **Narrate false certainty, not mystery.** Characters don't experience gaps in their knowledge. They experience a *complete* picture that happens to be wrong. See 11 - Knowledge Map.

## Where the protagonist fits

A Tinker of Fiction is built to break theme 2. Leet knows Worm canon and the fiction behind the expies, so he's the one person whose explanation is right, even while he can't work out why they're here. See [[04 - Tinker of Fiction#The thematic engine]].
