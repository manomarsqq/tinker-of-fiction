# 03 - Law and Institutions

## The organizing fact

**The law defines "parahuman" anatomically.** Every cape statute on Earth rests on one biological test: the presence of a **corona pollentia and gemma**. Anyone whose powers are shardless is **not legally a parahuman**. They aren't subject to registration, PRT jurisdiction, Three Strikes, the Birdcage, or cape-labour rules. That covers every Vought supe, every expy, every Ultimate Talent, every Flame user and every Mimic.

The regulatory state can only govern what it can define, and it defined it by scanning brains.

## The Parahuman Response Act
Funds the PRT and creates the Protectorate and the Wards. Defines parahumans by corona. Establishes **identity protection**: unmasking is a serious federal crime, for heroes *and* villains. It creates **no meaningful registration**.

**Why:** Cauldron's design is cops and robbers, structured and survivable conflict that maximizes triggers. Registration would collapse it. [[Alexandria]] is the Chief Director; she only has to keep saying no.

**The anomaly:** thirty years of cape-registration bills have a **100% failure rate**. They die in committee, sponsors lose interest, witnesses become unavailable. A statistical impossibility sitting in the public record, and nobody runs the query.

## The Ward Protection Doctrine
A Ward's identity and conduct records are **sealed against civil discovery**, on the reasoning that unmasking a minor gets them and their family killed.

**This law is correct.** It's a genuinely necessary protection. It's also exactly why [[Danny Hebert]] can't subpoena a single record about what Emma, Sophia and Madison did to [[Taylor Hebert|his daughter]], and why Winslow's administration is legally shielded for covering it up. The injustice isn't corruption. It's a defensible law working exactly as written, and that's what makes it unanswerable.

## The Requisition Clause
The PRT may **compel Mover-classified parahumans into federal service** during declared emergencies, because cape transport is infrastructure now. Constitutionally contested, never struck down, and the biggest reason teleporters loathe the agency.

Cauldron didn't block it. They **narrowed** it, from every classification in every circumstance down to Movers-only, emergencies-only. A collapsed civilization triggers fewer people.

## The Enhanced Individual carve-out
Vought's people are "enhanced individuals" under **employment law**, not parahumans under the PRA. It's four pages, filed as a **tax matter** around 2003, and nobody at the PRT read it. The most consequential legal document in the United States, sitting in a Treasury archive.

## Three Strikes and the Birdcage
Three cape-crime convictions: no trial after that, no parole, no release, no oversight. Administered entirely by [[Dragon]]. American capital sentencing has one point of failure, and it's a person the government keeps in chains.

## The Reciprocal Response Protocol
A nation that asks for [[The Yangban|Yangban]] help against an S-class threat transfers custody of a set number of **convicted** parahuman felons. Framed as ordinary extradition. Entirely legal. The convicts never come back and no signatory has ever asked.

**US:** not signed. **Allies:** three have. Every Endbringer attack makes the next refusal harder, and the US's confidence is *correct today and wrong on May 15th*.

## The unwritten rules
Not law, and **stronger than law** here, because the conflict drive is weak and cooperation genuinely holds.
- No civilians · No unmasking · No going after family · **The Endbringer Truce is absolute**

Violators are hunted by heroes and villains together, and it works. The Boardwalk stays neutral because everyone agrees it does.

## The Endbringer Truce
**Formal, universal, and it covers Lesser Endbringers.** Participants: Protectorate, Guild, Suits, Vought, Sky High, UA, the Yangban, the Elite, Gesellschaft, every independent, and every villain who wants to keep their standing.

At the anchor date it fires a few times a year. Once there are eight Endbringers plus Lessers, it becomes **the dominant social institution of the cape world**. Heroes and villains are **colleagues**: they've stood in the same mud a dozen times and been to the same funerals.

## The law that doesn't exist yet
Vought's lobbying arm has already drafted a **hazardous anomaly extraction** framework covering Endbringer-site salvage and Simurgh quarantine recovery. It passes in **August 2011**, amended to cover "dimensional anomalies". The bill that passes is the one that was already written, by the company that will hold the first licence.

## Other rules
- **Cauldron's rule:** the transaction is unprovable. Buyers get a power, a memory-altered delivery, and an obligation.
- **The League's rule:** All For One's obligations are absolute, and he can take powers back. No court required.
- **Corruption** is image management, not bribery. Every individual decision is defensible.
