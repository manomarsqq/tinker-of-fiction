## Overview

**Dungeon Gates**: tears into drifting debris of dead worlds, temporarily stabilised into pocket realms. Destabilising one causes a **Dungeon Break**; clearing all inhabitants disperses it harmlessly.

**Zero public Gates at anchor.** Every Gate so far formed below or beyond human presence (seafloor, deep voids). The Endbringers found this layer first, and an unknown fraction of Gates open onto **Legion nurseries**.

## What they're like

Each Gate is a fragment of a dead world: ruins, strange skies, alien ecology, monsters. Some are empty. Some are nurseries.

## Gate salvage

Wildly variable. *Some of it from settings people would recognise.*

## Timeline

- **2 May 2011:** the first public Gate opens under [[Winslow High School]] and drops the school into the Labyrinthos: **[[14 - The Powder Keg|the Powder Keg]]**.
- **July 2011:** Gates spread; the second and third public Gates.
- **August 2011:** licensed extraction begins.
- **Q2 2012:** a nursery empties into a city. The Legions reveal.
