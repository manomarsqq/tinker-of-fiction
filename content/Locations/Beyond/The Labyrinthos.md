## Overview

A self-contained dimension of shifting, non-Euclidean tunnels that move when unobserved. Entrances rupture through karst, deep mines and subway tunnels, and get **misfiled as cave-ins, sinkholes and missing persons**.

## What it's like

Tunnels that shift, sections that mimic human architecture, and walls that grow ore and gems.

- **Natives:** living malice. They spawn from the walls and hate *all* outsiders, Endbringer Legions included. That constant attrition is the natural limit on how deep the Endbringers can colonise.
- **Resources:** ore and gems growing in the walls (Tinker feedstock, refines into power-enhancement material); loot from tool-using natives (esoteric, often **cursed** with the bearer's dying breath).

## Timeline

- **Now:** entrances rupturing worldwide, misfiled.
- **2 May 2011:** [[Winslow High School]] falls into it: [[14 - The Powder Keg|the Powder Keg]]. The first undeniable entrance, in the middle of Brockton Bay. Filed as "a Shaker construct".
- **September 2011:** officially recognised, as entrances appear worldwide. The entrances don't close.
