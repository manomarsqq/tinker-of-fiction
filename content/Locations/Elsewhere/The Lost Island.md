## Overview

An uncharted island in the South Pacific whose concealment is failing, as part of the same destabilisation producing the Gates. Unclaimed. Everyone is about to notice at once.

## What it's like

Mesozoic and Pleistocene megafauna across volcanic highlands, primeval swamps, open plains and monsoon rainforest. **Flora** with unusual chemical and energetic properties: some toxic, some medicinal, some seemingly aware.

## For Leet

Unusual flora is exactly the kind of substitute a Tinker of Fiction needs for fictional reagents. See [[04 - Tinker of Fiction#Materials]].
