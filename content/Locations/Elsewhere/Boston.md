## Overview

The biggest city in New England, and home of [[Justice, Like Lightning]]. Close enough to Brockton Bay to answer a call. That's the hook.

## What it's like

A big, healthy city by Earth Bet standards: universities, hospitals, money, and a Protectorate branch far better funded than Brockton Bay's.

## Who's here

- **[[Justice, Like Lightning]]**: beloved heroes, disguised villains.
- **Protectorate Boston.**

## For Leet

The nearest real city. Where parts come from.
