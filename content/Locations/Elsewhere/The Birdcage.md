## Overview

The parahuman prison, built into a mountain in Canada, run by [[Dragon]]. No trial, no parole, no release. Three Strikes ends here.

American capital sentencing has one point of failure, and it's a person the government keeps in chains.

## What it's like

A sealed prison inside a mountain, with cell blocks run by their own inmates. Nothing goes in or out except through Dragon.

## Who's here

- **The worst capes in America.**
- **[[Dragon]]**, as warden.

## For Leet

A Tinker who builds power-granting tech, or anything from a hazardous catalog, is a Tinker the PRT would put here.
