## Overview

A town in New York that fell to **Nilbog** in 1993. Walled and quarantined, one of the military's real jobs. [[Emily Piggot]] was maimed here.

## What it's like

A wall. Behind it, a town Nilbog rebuilt into a kingdom of creatures he made from its people. Watchtowers, patrols, and a standing order to burn anything that comes out.

## History

Nilbog triggered and turned the town into his kingdom. The PRT sent teams in. They died. Piggot survived. The wall went up.

## For Leet

Leet knows Nilbog from canon. Fiction has a lot of ways to deal with a biokinetic king.
