## Overview

Japan's national hero academy, built around [[All Might]] in the Kyushu recovery. National reconstruction as much as a school.

## What it's like

A campus of glass towers, training grounds that simulate cities, and a student body drawn from the Quirk lineages.

## Who's here

- **[[All Might]].**
- **[[UA]]** staff and students.
