## Overview

Sunk by Leviathan in **1999**. About 13 million dead. The worst Endbringer event in history. Global confidence collapsed. **[[All Might]] rose from the wreckage.** [[UA]] was founded in the recovery. Japan lost the ability to reliably import food, which is the root of the global diaspora.

## What it's like now

Gone. The sea over where the island was. Memorials on the coast of Honshu.

## Consequences

- **The Kyushu diaspora:** millions displaced into dead port districts worldwide, including Brockton Bay's [[The Docks|Docks]].
- **All Might**, and the Peace Symbol.
- **UA.**
- **Japan's food crisis.**

## For Leet

In canon, Kyushu sank too. Here, All Might stood up in the wreckage. Leet has no idea why.
