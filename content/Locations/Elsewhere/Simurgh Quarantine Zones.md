## Overview

**Lausanne, Madrid, Canberra.** Sealed cities with everything still inside them. Legally frozen, economically enormous, morally radioactive. [[Vought International|Vought]]'s salvage bill wants the licence to go in.

## What it's like

Walls. Inside, cities left as they were on the day the Simurgh came, and the people who weren't evacuated.

## History

The Simurgh's attacks leave people seeded with triggers that turn them into time bombs. The cities are sealed so nobody can bring the seeds out.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**August 2011:** the salvage bill passes, covering "hazardous anomaly extraction". Quarantine asset recovery is on the table.

## For Leet

Everything inside is still there, including Tinkertech.
