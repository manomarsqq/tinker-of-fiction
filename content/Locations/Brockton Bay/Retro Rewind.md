## Overview

A movie and video game rental shop on [[The Boardwalk]], among the fancier places: part Blockbuster, part game rental. [[Leet]] (Joshua) owns it, inherited when his father died. It does better than you'd expect.

**The stock:** everything from the **early 2000s and older**. **The look:** straight out of the **90s**.

## What it's like

- **The front:** a neon sign, window posters for films a decade old, a bell over the door.
- **The floor:** VHS and DVD shelves by genre, a wall of games sorted by console (NES through PS2, Game Boys, a Dreamcast shelf), a new-arrivals rack where nothing is new.
- **The counter:** [[Uber]] (Robyn) behind it, a CRT playing whatever she picked, a jar of candy and a late-fees sign.
- **The back:** returns bin, the office, and the stairs down.
- **The basement:** the workshop, and the base of [[Uber and Leet]]. Locked, and in the SI's hands since Christmas 2010.

## Why it does well

*(proposed)* It's the only place in the Bay that has everything old, and on the Boardwalk that reads as retro chic rather than out of date. Boardwalk foot traffic, a loyal crowd of collectors and nostalgic regulars, and rent-a-game weekends for kids. The Concord's neutrality means nobody robs it.

## Who's here

- **[[Leet]]**: owner.
- **[[Uber]]**: works the counter.

**Hours** *(proposed)*: after school and weekends. Joshua and Robyn are both [[Winslow High School]] students, and on 2 May the school falls into the Labyrinthos with them inside ([[14 - The Powder Keg]]).
- **Regulars:** Boardwalk shoppers, collectors, kids, off-duty capes who never say they're capes.

## History

Joshua's father opened it and ran it until he died. Joshua inherited it, and with it the basement where canon Leet built his gadgets. In December 2010 a self-insert woke up upstairs.

## For Leet

A Tinker of Fiction living on top of a shop full of fiction. **Every tape, disc and cartridge on the shelves is a setting.** The store is his cover, his income, his base, and his research library.
