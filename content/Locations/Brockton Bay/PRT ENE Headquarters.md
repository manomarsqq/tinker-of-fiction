## Overview

Director [[Emily Piggot]]'s building downtown. Troopers earn ~$45,000 plus hazard pay, and every one knows their rifle is a prop against anything with a corona. The Wards' base and records are here, sealed.

## What it's like

- **A federal building** with armoured glass.
- **Containment cells** for captured capes.
- **The Wards' common area**, games console and all.
- **Armoured vans** with foam sprayers in the garage.
- **Piggot's office**, on the top floor.

## Who's here

- **[[Wards ENE]].**
- **PRT troopers and analysts.**
