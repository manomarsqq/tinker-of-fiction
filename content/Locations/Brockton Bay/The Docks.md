## Overview

Home of the Kyushu diaspora (two generations deep) and the pre-existing Asian-American community. **[[Lung]]'s real power base**: a displaced people with no economic future in a city with no port, who can't fall back on fishing because the ocean now kills fishermen. ABB territory.

## What it's like

- **Warehouses and cranes** that haven't moved in years.
- **Cheap apartments** packed with families.
- **Markets and restaurants** run by the diaspora.
- **Shrines** to the dead of Kyushu.
- **ABB members** on the corners, and everyone knows whose street it is.

## Who's here

- **[[ABB]]**: Lung, Oni Lee, and soon Bakuda.
- **[[The Ferrymen]]**: along the water.
- **[[Dockworkers' Association Hall|The DWA hall]]** and [[Danny Hebert]].

## History

The port's working heart. When it died, the diaspora arrived to find a district with no work. Lung took it. The ABB rules it now.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** Bakuda's bombs in May, then Leviathan on the 15th. The Docks take the worst of both.

## For Leet

ABB ground. Lung.
