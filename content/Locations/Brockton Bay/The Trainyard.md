## Overview

A rail yard on the edge of the city. Rail still runs, a little: freight to the interior now that the sea is closed. [[The Ninety-Nine]] hold the edge.

## What it's like

- **Freight cars** rusting in sidings, and a few that still move at night.
- **Gravel, weeds, fences** with holes in them.
- **Abandoned workshops and sheds** along the tracks.
- **White armbands** on the kids who watch the gates.

## Who's here

- **[[The Ninety-Nine]]**: diaspora youth who refused the ABB, silent and disciplined.
- **Rail workers**, the few still employed.
- **Squatters.**

## History

The Trainyard was where the port's cargo went inland. Now it's where inland cargo comes in, because the sea is closed. It matters more than it looks, and nobody powerful has claimed it, which is why the Ninety-Nine could.

## 8 April 2011 and after

The Ninety-Nine are being courted by the Fallen and three cults. **Coming:** Leviathan makes the Trainyard one of the few working supply routes into the city.

## For Leet

Quiet, abandoned, and a good place to test things away from the Boardwalk. Also a working rail line, and fiction has a lot of trains.
