## Overview

Empire 88 and Hollowmen territory. Deindustrialised, white working class, with real grievances. Kaiser keeps feeding them.

## What it's like

- **Row houses** built for factory workers, a lot of them boarded up.
- **Closed plants and mills** along the old rail spur, the jobs that left first.
- **Bars and social clubs** where the Empire recruits, and where the talk is always about who took the jobs.
- **Graffiti** marked with a white handprint: Hollowmen turf.
- **Cheap rent** and cheaper hope.

## Who's here

- **[[Empire 88]]**: its base of support, not its headquarters. Kaiser lives elsewhere.
- **[[The Hollowmen]]**: evictions, intimidation, arson.
- **Families** who don't share the ideology and can't afford to move.

## History

The North End was the city's industrial belt. The factories fed the port. When the port died, the factories followed, and the white working-class families who'd worked them for generations were left with nothing but grievance. Allfather's Empire moved in to name an enemy for them. Kaiser gave that enemy a boardroom voice.

## 8 April 2011 and after

Quiet by Empire standards. **Coming:** after Leviathan, the Empire uses the rebuilding to buy up the North End cheaply.

## For Leet

Empire ground. Fights here pay well and bring Hookwolf. Most of the people living here are just poor.
