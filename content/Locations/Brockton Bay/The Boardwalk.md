## Overview

Brockton Bay's shopping and tourist strip along the waterfront, **where all the fancier places are**: boutiques, restaurants, cafés, a few surviving hotels, and [[Retro Rewind]]. It's where the money in a dying city goes to spend itself.

It stays **neutral** because everyone has agreed it does, enforced by private security ([[The Concord]]) who couldn't stop a single cape, which is the whole point.

## What it's like

- **A boardwalk proper** along the water, with the Rig visible out in the bay.
- **Shops and restaurants** a class above the rest of the city: clothes, jewellery, galleries, seafood, Fugly Bob's burgers *(canon)*.
- **Grey-coated Concord doormen** outside every major building.
- **Sea fog** in April, and a cold wind off the water.

## Who's here

- **[[The Concord]]**: security and fixers.
- **Businesses:** [[Retro Rewind]], and dozens of others.
- **Shoppers** from every side of the city, including off-duty gang members and capes, who all behave.

## The neutrality

Every gang has people who want to shop here, so every gang agreed to leave it alone. A cape who causes trouble is **escorted out** by the Concord, and goes, because the arrangement works and nobody wants to be the one who broke it. The unwritten rules in miniature.

## 8 April 2011 and after

Busy. **Coming:** Leviathan on **15 May** hits the waterfront, and the Boardwalk with it.

## For Leet

Retro Rewind is here. It's neutral ground, and it's his home. Any fight that reaches the Boardwalk breaks the arrangement that protects his store.
