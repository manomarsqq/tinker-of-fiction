## Overview

**Population:** ~350,000. **The port died and nothing replaced it. That's the whole story of the city.** Federal money and Medhall keep the lights on.

- **Cape density** is far above what the population should support. **Gravity!** *(modifier)*: capes physically gravitate toward each other, more strongly between comparable power levels. Watchdog has noticed the correlation without understanding it. The worse the city gets, the more capes it pulls in.
- **Curses:** one of the densest Curse hotspots in North America (a dying port of stacked misery under a gang war). The deadliest place on the continent to be a sorcerer. Invisible to everyone.
- **Economy:** median household ~$38,000. Docks rent ~$600/mo for a two-bedroom in bad repair.
- **Weather, April:** cold coastal spring, 4–13 °C, frequent rain and heavy sea fog off the bay. **Leviathan arrives 15 May.**
- **Haywire's portal:** communication with Earth Aleph only. Also a dimensional stress point.

## The powder keg

> [!warning] One issue away from civil war
> On 8 April 2011 the gangs, the neighbourhoods and City Hall are all at breaking point at once. On **2 May** the issue arrives: [[Winslow High School]] falls into the Labyrinthos, and fourteen hundred students come out with powers. The Ordinance vote is the next morning; Leviathan thirteen days later. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].

## History

A port city that lived on shipping. Leviathan, Kyushu's sinking and the Reemergence megafauna made the oceans too dangerous for intercontinental trade, and the port died. The dockworkers lost their jobs, the Kyushu diaspora arrived with no jobs to take, and the gangs filled the gap.

## 8 April 2011 and after

The anchor date. Pure canon Brockton Bay: three gangs, one Protectorate, a new cape on her first night out.

**Coming:** **15 May**, Leviathan with the Harbingers. Casualties far beyond canon. The city after that is a different city.
