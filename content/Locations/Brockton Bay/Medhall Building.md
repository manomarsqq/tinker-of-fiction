## Overview

Medhall's corporate headquarters, a glass tower downtown. A real pharmaceutical company, larger on paper than the Merchants, but most of its wealth can't be moved for gang purposes without exposing [[Kaiser|Max Anders]].

## What it's like

- **Glass and steel**, the tallest private building downtown.
- **Research labs** that make real medicine.
- **Executive floors** where Max Anders takes meetings.
- **Security** that's better than it looks. Some of it wears Empire colours off-hours.

## Who's here

- **[[Kaiser]]**, as CEO.
- **Medhall employees**, most of whom have no idea.
- **Empire capes** in civilian identities, on the payroll.

## History

Medhall made the Anders family rich before the Empire made it powerful. When the port died, Medhall was one of the few employers that stayed, which gives Kaiser leverage over the whole city.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** the salvage bill (August 2011) and exotic materials from Gates. Pharmaceutical research is exactly where Gate materials would go.

## For Leet

Leet knows who the CEO is. So does nobody outside the Empire. Medhall also has labs, reagents and equipment a Tinker would kill for.
