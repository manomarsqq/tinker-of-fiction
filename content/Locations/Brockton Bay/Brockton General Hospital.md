## Overview

The city's main hospital. Where [[Panacea]] works her hours, and where a shardless person would be exposed by a single scan: the reason every shardless population keeps its own doctor.

## What it's like

Overcrowded, underfunded, busier than any hospital in a city this size should be. A cape wing with PRT liaisons. A queue outside Panacea's room.

## Who's here

- **[[Panacea]]**: most days.
- **PRT medical liaisons.**
- **Everyone the gang war injures.**

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** Leviathan. On 15 May this building is the city's triage centre.

## For Leet

A corona scan here would show Leet's corona pollentia, and maybe something wrong with it. Better to stay out.
