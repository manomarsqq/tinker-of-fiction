## Overview

Protectorate ENE's base, out in the bay: a former oil platform converted into a fortress, with a forcefield dome that goes up in emergencies. It's visible from the Boardwalk, and from Retro Rewind's front door.

## What it's like

- **Armsmaster's lab**, the most advanced Tinker workshop in the city.
- **Barracks, a mess, training rooms.**
- **A hangar and a helipad**, and boat docks.
- **Holding cells** for capes awaiting transfer.
- **The forcefield dome**: a fortress in an emergency.

## Who's here

- **[[Armsmaster]]**, most of the time.
- **[[Miss Militia]].**
- **[[Protectorate ENE]]**: Assault, Battery, Velocity, Dauntless.

## History

An old offshore platform the Protectorate bought and rebuilt. Out in the water, it's defensible, symbolic and a long way from the gangs.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** on **15 May**, Leviathan comes out of the bay. The Rig is the first thing in his path.

## For Leet

The other Tinker workshop in the city, across the water from his own.
