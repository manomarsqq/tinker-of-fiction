## Overview

Merchant territory on the surface: a sprawling street market and the neighbourhoods around it. [[The Wake]] is pushing at its edges.

## What it's like

- **Stalls and flea markets** that sell everything: food, clothes, electronics, stolen goods, drugs.
- **Pawn shops and junk dealers.**
- **Merchant lookouts** who look like addicts, because many of them are.
- **The best place in the city** to buy something without a receipt.

## Who's here

- **[[The Merchants]]**: the street face of a logistics empire.
- **[[The Wake]]**: two capes and a crew from nowhere, pushing in.
- **Everyone** looking for a bargain.

## History

A market district from the port's heyday, where sailors and dockworkers traded. When the port died it became a black market, and the Merchants took it over. The real Merchant business (the fleet) runs through the Boat Graveyard. The Market is just where it touches the street.

## 8 April 2011 and after

The Wake keeps pushing. **Coming:** a Merchant–Wake war is waiting to happen.

## For Leet

A place to buy parts nobody asks about. Also where the Wake is, and the Wake is hostile to everyone, which makes them easy points.
