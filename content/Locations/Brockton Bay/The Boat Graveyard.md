## Overview

The wrecks of the dead shipping trade, choking the harbour. [[The Ferrymen]] free-dive it and know it better than anyone alive. **On 15 May** that becomes the most valuable knowledge in the city.

## What it's like

- **Rusting hulls** and half-sunk freighters, dozens of them.
- **Oil slicks, gulls, and cold water.**
- **Paths of planks** between the wrecks.
- **[[Squealer]]'s workshop** hidden inside one of the ships.
- **The Merchant fleet** hiding in plain sight among the dead ships.

## Who's here

- **[[The Ferrymen]]**: small-boat smugglers.
- **[[The Merchants]]** and their fleet.
- **[[Squealer]].**

## History

When shipping collapsed, ships were abandoned where they sat. Over years the harbour silted with wrecks. It's the port's corpse.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** Leviathan on 15 May turns the Graveyard into a maze of new wrecks. Anyone who knew the old one knows where everything is.

## For Leet

Scrap by the ton, and a Tinker's paradise. Also the Merchants' base.
