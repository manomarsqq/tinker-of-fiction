## Overview

Functionally a holding facility. **The administration is legally shielded** by the Ward Protection Doctrine for covering up what happened to Taylor.

**Teenage Brockton Bay:** gang recruitment is real and comes with health insurance. School is triage. Everyone knows somebody who triggered. *"One more week of this and I'm gonna trigger."* Kids joke about triggering: a joke about wanting something unbearable to happen to you, because a power is the only exit anyone can name. That's the sentence Winslow is built on.

> [!warning] The Powder Keg
> In **May 2011, before Leviathan** *(proposed: Monday 2 May)*, a powers-out brawl in the cafeteria tears open the ground beneath the school. **The first public Gate drops the whole of Winslow into the [[The Labyrinthos|Labyrinthos]]**, building and all. Every student who comes out has powers. The building is gone; the crater is a permanent Labyrinthos entrance. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]] and [[The Winslow Gate]].

## What it's like

- **Metal detectors that don't work**, gang colours in the halls, overworked teachers.
- **The cafeteria**, where the city's feuds sit at separate tables: Empire kids, ABB conscripts, Lamplighter kids, Ninety-Nine armbands, Iron Parish boys.
- **The locker.**
- **About 1,400 students** *(proposed)*.

## Who's here

- **[[Taylor Hebert]]**, a cape in her first month.
- **[[Sophia Hess]], [[Emma Barnes]], [[Madison Clements]]**: Wards.
- **[[Greg Veder]]**: Lung tier, and nobody believes him.
- **[[Leet]] (Joshua) and [[Uber]] (Robyn)**: students, when they're not at [[Retro Rewind]].
- **[[Ashfall]]** (Ninety-Nine) and **[[Moth]]** (Lamplighters) *(proposed)*.
- **Declan Kilbride**, an Iron Parish boy who took the Empire's handprint, and **Min-jun Kang**, [[Wick]]'s son, now an ABB conscript *(proposed)*.

## History

A big public high school in a dying city. The school every kid without money ends up at.

## After 2 May

Gone. A crater with a hole in the bottom that goes somewhere else. The PRT cordons it; the Undertow, the Chop Shop and the Merchants all want to know what's down there.

## For Leet

Leet knows which locker. He also goes here, and on 2 May he's inside.
