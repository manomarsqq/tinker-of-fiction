## Overview

The DWA's hiring hall on the waterfront. A big, cold, old building with a board of shifts that's mostly empty. It places a third of its members in a given month.

## What it's like

- **Folding chairs and coffee.**
- **A job board**, mostly blank.
- **Union posters** from the port's good years.
- **[[Danny Hebert]]'s office** at the back.

## Who's here

- **[[Danny Hebert]].**
- **Dockworkers** waiting for shifts that don't come.

## History

Built when the DWA was one of the most powerful unions in the state. The building is bigger than the union is now.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** after Leviathan, a relief centre.

## For Leet

A hall full of skilled men with nothing to do. Automation catalogs could change that.
