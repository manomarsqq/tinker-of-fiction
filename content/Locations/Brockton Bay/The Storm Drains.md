## Overview

Flooded sublevels and old storm drains beneath the city. [[The Undertow]]'s territory. One of them isn't a drain anymore.

## What it's like

- **Brick tunnels** from the 1800s, concrete ones from the 1960s.
- **Flooded sections** that need rebreathers.
- **Echoes, rats, and lamps.**
- **Old service rooms**, some used as caches.

## Who's here

- **[[The Undertow]]**: two capes and a crew of scavengers.

## History

The drains were built for a city that expected to grow. It shrank, and the drains were left to rot. The Undertow use them to get anywhere unseen.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** on 2 May 2011 the rupture beneath [[Winslow High School]] tears wide open: [[14 - The Powder Keg|the Powder Keg]]. Some of the Winslow survivors come out through these drains.

## For Leet

Underground access to half the city.
