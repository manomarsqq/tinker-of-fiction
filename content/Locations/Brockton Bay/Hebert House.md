## Overview

Danny and Taylor's house, a modest two-storey in a neighbourhood that used to be working class and is now just tired. The Hebert family home.

## What it's like

- **Quiet.** Two people who love each other and don't talk.
- **Annette's books** still on the shelves. Nobody has moved them.
- **A kitchen** where dinner is eaten mostly in silence.
- **The basement**, where Taylor sews her spider-silk costume and plans.
- **Taylor's room**, with the window she'll leave through tonight.

## Who's here

- **[[Taylor Hebert]].**
- **[[Danny Hebert]].**

## History

The Heberts have lived here since Taylor was small. Annette died in a car accident; the house has been quieter since.

## 8 April 2011 and after

Taylor leaves from here tonight for her first night out.
