## Biography

A struck-off ER doctor with a minor healing Striker power and a diagnostic Thinker sense. She runs an unlicensed clinic on Mercy Street where every gang sends its wounded, because she doesn't ask and doesn't report. Her clinic is neutral ground by common agreement.
