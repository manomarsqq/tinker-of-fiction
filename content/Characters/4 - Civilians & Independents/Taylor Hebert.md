## Biography

Triggered in **January 2011**, in the locker. A new cape with no support, no team, and a sealed institutional record that legally can't be opened.

Three girls with enhanced legal protection are in the Wards, and one of them ([[Sophia Hess]]) is the reason she triggered. [[Emma Barnes]]'s power is the answer to *why nobody believes her*. [[Madison Clements]] got the sanitized, marketable version of Taylor's own power and a Wards slot for it.

**She goes out on 8 April**, the anchor date. *"Everything else in this city routes through what she decides in the next fortnight."*

**The money:** the only thing between Taylor and $50,000 a year in Wards trust is that the program already contains the girl who put her in the locker, and the [[03 - Law and Institutions#The Ward Protection Doctrine|Ward Protection Doctrine]] means she'll never be able to prove it.
