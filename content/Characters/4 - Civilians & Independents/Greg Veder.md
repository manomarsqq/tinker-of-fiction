## Biography

**XxVoid_CowboyxX (Verified Cape) ×4**: a true high-tier parahuman, Lung or Purity tier. One of the strongest capes in Brockton Bay, and a fifteen-year-old PHO poster with no impulse control.

**He has triggered, and has bragged about it on PHO repeatedly, with evidence.** Nobody believes him, because he's Greg.

**Why the PRT didn't investigate:** they did, early. Greg had years of false cape claims *before* he triggered. The investigation found nothing and closed. Every new claim gets matched against a closed file, and that file is a joke passed around the ENE office.

> The PRT can't see anomalies because "it's a cape" explains everything. PHO can't see Greg because "Void_Cowboy is full of shit" explains everything. Both work perfectly and are catastrophically wrong.
