## Biography

Runs the **Dockworkers' Association** hiring hall, which places about **a third of its members** in a given month. The port is dead, and the DWA is a union for a job that stopped existing. He keeps it going anyway.

His wife **Annette** died in a car accident. He and his daughter [[Taylor Hebert|Taylor]] share a house in quiet grief, and he doesn't know what's happening to her at school. His household is under the Brockton Bay median (~$38,000).

He **cannot subpoena a single record** about what Emma, Sophia and Madison did to his daughter. That isn't corruption; it's the [[03 - Law and Institutions#The Ward Protection Doctrine|Ward Protection Doctrine]] working exactly as written.
