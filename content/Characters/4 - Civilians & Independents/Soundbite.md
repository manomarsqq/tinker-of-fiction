## Biography

A political speechwriter who triggered after a breakdown on the campaign trail. Anything he writes sticks: a slogan becomes the thing people say to each other. "Our city, our streets, our safety" is his. He writes Brockton First's speeches and radio spots, and hides his power from the man he works for.
