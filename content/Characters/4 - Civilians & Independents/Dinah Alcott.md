## Biography

The mayor's niece, and a **precog**. Her power answers questions with probabilities: ask her whether something will happen, and she sees the odds. It comes with headaches that get worse the more she uses it.

**[[Coil]] takes her in May 2011** *(canon)* and keeps her drugged and captive, using her numbers to steer his split timelines.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Thinker (precog).** Numerical probabilities for yes/no questions about the future.
