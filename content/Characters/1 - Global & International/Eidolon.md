## Biography

**David.** The most powerful man alive, and declining. A Cauldron vial gave him a **Trump** power that draws on a pool of other powers, up to three at a time, chosen by what he needs. For decades that made him the strongest cape on Earth.

His powers are weakening. [[Panacea]] can't help, because the decline is shard-related, not biological. He over-commits to every fight to prove he still can.

And he carries a guilt nobody else knows about. In canon, Eidolon's wish for "worthy opponents" is what made the Endbringers. Here, **Endbringers Untethered** means it wasn't him. They came online by themselves. He still believes, in the part of himself he never examines, that they're his.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Power pool.** Up to three powers at once, selected by need. Blaster, Brute, Mover, Thinker, whatever the moment calls for.
- **Declining.** The powers take longer to arrive and come in weaker. He compensates by arriving too hard.
- **Triumvirate standing.** Alongside Alexandria and Legend, the face of the Protectorate for thirty years.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Strained gravitas. Over-explains. A man performing confidence for an audience of one.

**Wants:** To matter again.

**Wrong about:** **The big one.** That the Endbringers are his. They aren't. The guilt is hollowing him out just as fast as if it were true.

**Tendencies:** Over-commits. Arrives too hard. Needs to be needed.

**Breaking point:** Being told, credibly, that it was never him.

## Relationships

- [[Alexandria]] and [[Doctor Mother]]: know he's declining and have started planning around him.
- [[Panacea]]: tried, and couldn't help.
- [[All Might]]: the other pillar of public hope, and the one whose strength is *growing more credible* as David's fades.

## 8 April 2011

Still at every Endbringer fight, still one of the strongest capes alive. His confidence is a performance, and he's the only audience that matters to him.

**Coming:** the new Endbringers (Nemesis 2012, Vulcan and Acedia 2012, Anubis and Tithonus 2013) will look to him exactly like his guilt made flesh. **Tithonus**, who speaks buried truths, could be what finally tells him.

## What Leet knows

**Canon:** Leet knows about the "worthy opponents" line and what it meant, and how Eidolon dies in canon: to Scion.

**Here:** Leet may be the only person alive with enough information to realise **it wasn't him**, if he works out that the Endbringers here are Untethered. What he does with that is a choice.
