## Biography

**Fortuna.** The instrument of Cauldron. Her power is the **Path to Victory**: name a goal and she sees every step to reach it. She's been walking Paths since she was a child and has stopped experiencing the world in any other way.

**PtV is not degrading** *(ruling)*. Her blind spots are Scion, Eden, the Endbringers, and now a growing **shardless population**: Vought's supes, the Quirk lineages, All Might, every expy. She paths around them with knowledge, the same way she's always handled Eidolon, and she does it so smoothly she's stopped noticing how much she's routing.

She is the reason Cauldron still works. Every kidnapping, every hidden vial sale, every "coincidence" that kept the conspiracy secret for thirty years is a Path she walked.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Path to Victory.** Step-by-step certainty toward any goal she can phrase. Can't see what her power can't see.
- **Blind spots:** Scion, Eden, Endbringers, and anything shardless. She fills the gaps with information and inference.
- **Physical skill** via the Path: she can win a fight she isn't physically capable of winning, one precise step at a time.
- **Doormaker access.** Cauldron's portals go anywhere she needs.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Minimal. Short declaratives. Doesn't explain or repeat herself.

**Wants:** The Path completed.

**Wrong about:** Nothing she can see, which is the problem.

**Tendencies:** Acts without explaining. Never hesitates. Treats people as steps.

**Breaking point:** A Path that returns nothing at a moment that matters.
