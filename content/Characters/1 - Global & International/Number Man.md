## Biography

**Kurt.** Cauldron's ledger. A Thinker whose power is numbers: he sees them, calculates them, and makes them do what he wants. He runs Cauldron's finances, prices every vial, launders the money, and keeps the models that tell Doctor Mother how long they have.

His models are **silently degrading**. A growing share of Earth's powered population is shardless (Vought's supes, the Quirk lineages, the expies) and none of them are in his data. The forecasts still add up. They just don't describe the world anymore.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Mathematical Thinker.** Instant calculation, perfect aim with anything thrown or fired, a feel for probability.
- **Cauldron's money.** Accounts, fronts and investments in every major economy.
- **Actuarial models** of the cape population, the vial supply and the Endbringer toll.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Pleasant, quantitative, unnervingly calm. Offers you tea while explaining your death.

**Wants:** The numbers to keep working.

**Wrong about:** That numbers capture it. Shardless people aren't in the data.

**Tendencies:** Quantifies everything. Never emotional. Will tell the truth if asked precisely.

**Breaking point:** A forecast that fails catastrophically and publicly.

## Relationships

- [[Doctor Mother]]: reports to her. Tells her the truth when she asks the right question.
- [[The Merchants]]: exactly the kind of money he'd notice, if they were in his data. They aren't, because the PRT files them as a joke.

## 8 April 2011

Balancing books that look healthy. The vial market is steady, the funding is steady, the forecasts are steady.

**Coming:** the **hope bubble** after the first Lesser Endbringer kill (June 2011) and its collapse at the Legions reveal (Q2 2012) are exactly the kind of public forecast failure that breaks him.

**Canon:** Leet knows Number Man handles Cauldron's money, and that he's the Cauldron member most willing to simply answer a question.

**Here:** a Tinker whose store does "better than you'd expect" and whose income comes from nowhere is the kind of line item Number Man would notice, **if** Leet ever shows up in the data.
