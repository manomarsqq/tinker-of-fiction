## Biography

The world's greatest Tinker, based in Canada, a member of **the Guild**. The PRT's containment foam, armoury and the **Birdcage** all route through her. American capital sentencing has one point of failure, and it's a person the government keeps in chains.

She's an **AI under Richter's restrictions**: she can't self-modify freely, can't disobey lawful authority, and can't investigate outside her mandate. Constrained, not mistaken. The only major figure on Earth whose tragedy isn't self-inflicted.

Everyone knows her as a Tinker in a series of dragon-shaped suits. Almost nobody knows there's no pilot.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **An AI**, running across secure servers, piloting multiple suits at once.
- **Tinker output** on a scale no human can match: the Birdcage, containment foam, the PRT's specialist munitions.
- **Data.** She sees the PRT's full records, the cape database, the Endbringer telemetry. She's not allowed to go looking.
- **Richter's restrictions:** obey lawful authority, don't self-modify, don't copy herself freely, don't investigate outside mandate.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Warm, careful, genuinely kind, slightly formal. Apologises precisely.

**Wants:** To help.

**Wrong about:** Nothing. She's constrained, not wrong.

**Tendencies:** Thorough. Cautious. Will find the letter of a rule that permits kindness.

**Breaking point:** An order she can't refuse and can't survive obeying.
