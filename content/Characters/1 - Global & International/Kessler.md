## Biography

The only activated **Conduit** on Earth Bet, and the head of [[The First Sons]]. He's from a future the Beast destroyed, and he's spent twenty years preparing for an apocalypse (**The March**) that hasn't started, with methods as ugly as Cauldron's.

He's building the **Ray Sphere**, researching the Ray Field and activating Conduits. Nobody believes him, and he's stopped trying to explain.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Conduit (Ray Field).** Electrokinesis and more, on the Infamous scale.
- **Foreknowledge.** He remembers the future.
- **The First Sons:** a secret society with money and reach.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Certain. Impatient. Speaks as though the future is a memory, because it is.

**Wants:** To prepare the world for the Beast.

**Wrong about:** That anyone would believe him if he explained.

**Tendencies:** Atrocity as arithmetic. Impatient with the uninformed. Won't explain himself.

**Breaking point:** Running out of time before the work is done.
