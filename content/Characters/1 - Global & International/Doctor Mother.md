## Biography

The founder and the **will** of Cauldron. She and [[Contessa]] built the conspiracy around one goal, **killing Scion**, and she has accepted every cost it has asked for: the vials, the Case 53s, the kidnapped test subjects, the trade in powers that funds the whole thing.

She runs Cauldron from its base outside reality, a clean white facility reached only through the Doormaker's portals. Nine people, finite vials, and thirty years of decisions she doesn't revisit.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **No public power.** Whatever she can do matters far less than what she decides.
- **Cauldron's resources:** the vials, the Doormaker, [[Contessa]]'s Path, [[Number Man]]'s money, and the global network of teams Cauldron quietly funds.
- **Information.** She knows more about shards, Scion and the cycle than anyone on Earth. Most of it is correct. The part about the Endbringers isn't.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Flat, clinical, never raised. Speaks in costs and outcomes. Doesn't comfort.

**Wants:** Scion dead. Will accept any cost.

**Wrong about:** That she has time.

**Tendencies:** Decides fast. Never revisits a sunk cost emotionally. Spends people.

**Breaking point:** Contessa's death.

## Relationships

- [[Contessa]]: the only person she can't afford to lose.
- [[Alexandria]]: argues with her about tempo. Alexandria wants stability; Doctor Mother wants results.
- [[Eidolon]]: a declining asset.
- [[The First Sons]]: being watched, deliberately, to see what they develop.

Running a strategically obsolete conspiracy without knowing it. The vial supply is a corpse and it's depleting; All For One and Compound V broke the monopoly years ago; the Endbringers aren't what Cauldron thinks they are.
