## Biography

**Rebecca Costa-Brown is Alexandria is Cauldron.** She was one of Cauldron's earliest vial customers and is still one of its inner circle, and for decades she has worn two faces: the invulnerable founding member of the Triumvirate, and the Chief Director of the PRT who runs the agency she was supposed to be the cape for. Everything the PRT does is subject to a hidden veto, and she exercises it quietly, in committee, never in the field.

She doesn't have to lobby against cape registration. She only has to keep saying no. Thirty years of registration bills have a **100% failure rate**: they die in committee, sponsors lose interest, witnesses become unavailable. It's a statistical impossibility sitting in the public legislative record, and nobody runs the query because nobody has a reason to.

The PRT's model of the world is her model. She built the classification system, the doctrine, the Brute-floor logic that makes the agency the only thing that can stop a cape. It works, and it's about to be catastrophically wrong, and she's the last person on Earth who'd believe that.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Invulnerability.** She doesn't get hurt. Her body was locked in place when she took the vial.
- **Super strength and flight.** Enough to wrestle Endbringers without dying, not enough to beat them.
- **Perfect recall and a Thinker edge.** She remembers everything and processes it fast. She has never needed to take notes.
- **Institutional power.** The Chief Director's desk, the budget, and the ability to kill a threat with paperwork. This is the one she actually uses.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Precise, executive, institutional even in private. The mask never comes off because there's nothing underneath it anymore.

**Wants:** Control, and Scion dead.

**Wrong about:** That the PRT's model of the world is basically accurate, because she built it.

**Tendencies:** Kills threats in committee rather than in the field. Sacrifices individuals without visible hesitation. Treats [[Contessa]]'s Path as near-gospel.

**Breaking point:** Public exposure of either identity.

## Relationships

- [[Doctor Mother]] and [[Contessa]]: her Cauldron partners. She and Doctor Mother argue about **tempo**, never about goals.
- [[Eidolon]]: a colleague she watches declining and has quietly started planning around.
- [[Emily Piggot]]: a regional director she rates as competent and difficult. Piggot's reformer faction is a problem she manages, not one she fights.
- [[Dragon]]: the infrastructure the PRT can't live without, and a being Alexandria is very careful never to think of as a person.
- [[Vought International]]: a public rival she holds in private contempt. She has no idea Vought is a *mirror* of Cauldron.

## 8 April 2011

At the top of an institution that believes it has a monopoly on cape response. Three Endbringers, canon rotation, canon doctrine. Everything in her world is exactly as she designed it, for five more weeks.

**Coming:** Leviathan at Brockton Bay on **15 May** debuts the Harbingers, and in ninety minutes thirty years of doctrine expire.
