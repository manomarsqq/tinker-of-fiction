## Biography

Heir to [[The League of Villains]], and [[All For One]]'s chosen successor. His Quirk, **Decay**, turns anything he touches with all five fingers to dust.

All For One raised him, and overestimates how ready he is. Shigaraki is resentful, childish, and dangerous, and he hates heroes on principle.
