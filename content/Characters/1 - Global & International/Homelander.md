## Biography

The face of **Vought International** and leader of **the Seven**. A Compound V supe: flight, strength, heat vision, near-invulnerability. **Alexandria-adjacent, not above her** *(ruling)*.

He's **shardless**: no Thinker can read him. He doesn't know why and has never asked. Vought markets him as America's hero; Vought's handlers manage him as its most dangerous asset. He wants love and obedience at the same time.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Warm broadcast cadence that slips. The register drops mid-sentence when he stops performing, then comes back up.

**Tendencies:** Charming until contradicted. Escalates instantly when the mask slips. Punishes privately.
