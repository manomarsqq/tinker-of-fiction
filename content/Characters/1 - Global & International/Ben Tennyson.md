## Biography

**Alien Force era** *(ruling)*. When he was ten, Ben found the **Omnitrix** on a summer road trip with his grandfather Max and his cousin Gwen, and spent a summer as a hero nobody believed in. He took it off and tried to be normal.

At fifteen, Grandpa Max went missing, the **Highbreed** and their **DNAliens** started infiltrating Earth, and Ben put the watch back on. The Omnitrix recalibrated with a new set of aliens. Now he, Gwen and Kevin are fighting a secret alien invasion with the remains of a secret alien police force, and Earth Bet has no idea.

Like the other expies, he's here **with his native powers** and he's **shardless**.

## Powers & capabilities

- **The Omnitrix:** a Galvan device holding the DNA of thousands of species. Ben transforms into one at a time, for a limited time, from a set of unlocked forms: Swampfire, Humungousaur, Big Chill, Jetray, Echo Echo, Brainstorm, Spidermonkey, Chromastone, Goop, Alien X and more.
- **Shardless.** No corona pollentia. Invisible to Scion and shard-based Thinkers.
- **Plumber resources**: badges, ships, a network of Plumbers' kids.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Cocky, impulsive, funny, and growing up faster than he wants to.

**Wants:** To find Grandpa Max, and stop the Highbreed.

**Wrong about:** That the world will keep not noticing.

**Breaking point:** Losing Gwen or Kevin.

- **Gwen Tennyson**: his cousin. Part Anodite; manipulates mana. Smarter than him and happy to say so.
