## Biography

**Toshinori Yagi.** The Peace Symbol. Rose from the wreckage of **Kyushu (1999)**, the worst Endbringer event in history, with about 13 million dead. He told a broken world it would be all right, **and was believed**.

Earth Bet's scarcest resource is hope. The Protectorate can't manufacture it; they're a government agency managing a decline and everyone can smell it. All Might can. One For All is incidental. **Being believed is the actual power.**

[[UA]] was founded in the Kyushu recovery, built around him, as national reconstruction as much as a school.

**Full strength** *(ruling)*: not weakened, not dying, no time limit. His MHA decline was a Quirk-system constraint that Earth Bet doesn't have. [[Panacea]] rebuilds him after every Endbringer engagement, and his injuries were never neurological.

## Powers & capabilities

- **One For All (Quirk).** Stockpiled strength passed down generations. He's at the height of it.
- **Shardless.** Invisible to Scion and to shard-based Thinkers.
- **Symbolic power.** More strategically important than Alexandria, and far more fragile than anyone realises.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** **Two voices.** Public: enormous, warm, deliberately theatrical, an instrument built on purpose out of the wreckage. Private: quiet, thoughtful, and far more tired than a man in perfect health should be, because the symbol costs more than the injuries ever did.

**Wants:** To hold the line, and eventually find a successor worthy of One For All.

**Wrong about:** How much of the world's morale is load-bearing on him personally.

**Tendencies:** Arrives smiling. Overextends. Deflects concern with volume.

**Breaking point:** A failure large enough that being unafraid stops being credible.
