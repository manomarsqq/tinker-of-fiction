## Biography

Head of the council behind [[The League of Villains]], the shadow counterpart to the PRT. He's been operating in the shadows for generations, **granting and taking powers as patronage** long before anyone had heard of a parahuman.

His Quirk lets him steal powers and give them away. A villain who accepts his gift is bound to him in a way money never manages. **Cauldron's monopoly on manufactured powers was broken before Cauldron existed**, and they've never noticed, because everything he gives is **shardless**: no corona pollentia, no shard telemetry, invisible to Cauldron's Thinkers.

He's physically diminished. He's grooming [[Tomura Shigaraki]] as his heir, and he overestimates how ready the boy is.

## Powers & capabilities

- **All For One (Quirk).** Takes powers from others and gives them away. Holds a vast stockpile.
- **Shardless.** Invisible to shard-based Thinkers.
- **The League:** a council of the worst villains alive, all of them in his debt. Seats for [[Gesellschaft]], [[The Elite]] and the Merchant Markets.
- **Patience** measured in decades.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Courteous, unhurried, paternal. The most dangerous politeness on Earth.

**Wants:** A web of obligation covering the world, and a successor worthy of it.

**Wrong about:** Shigaraki's readiness.

**Tendencies:** Patient across decades. Prefers a gift to a threat. Escalates only against rival power-brokers.

**Breaking point:** Another entity that can grant powers.
