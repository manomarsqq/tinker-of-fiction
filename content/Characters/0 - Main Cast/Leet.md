## Biography

Someone from our world who woke up **stuck as Leet**, the video-game-themed Tinker half of [[Uber and Leet]]. It happened in **December 2010, just before Christmas**, a little over three months before the anchor date.

**Who Leet is.** **Joshua**, 17, white. He owns [[Retro Rewind]], a movie and video game rental shop on [[The Boardwalk]] (think Blockbuster plus game rentals) that he inherited when his father died. It stocks everything from the early 2000s and older, it looks straight out of the 90s, and it does better than you'd expect. [[Uber]] (Robyn) works there. The workshop and the duo's base are in its **basement**.

**School.** Joshua is still a student at **[[Winslow High School]]**, and so is Robyn *(user ruling)*. The store opens after school and on weekends *(proposed)*.

**Passing as Leet.** The SI has **all of Leet's memories**, so playing the part isn't hard. The habits, the in-jokes with Robyn, the store's regulars, the codes to the basement: all there.

**The power.** Leet's shard is an **Eden shard**. With Eden dead, the shard was orphaned. The SI's arrival **tampered with it**, forcing it into something else, and what came out is **[[04 - Tinker of Fiction|Tinker of Fiction]]**: a Tinker whose specialty is fiction itself. **Canon Leet's limit is gone**: repeating a design no longer makes it worse.

**Standing.** Technically [[Uber and Leet]] are **Rogues**. Nobody treats them that way. They're dicks about it, so the Bay, PHO and the PRT all file them as **villains**.

**Fixed by the world build:** he's active as a cape **before the [[Beasts of Entropy]] arrive in late 2011**.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Tinker of Fiction.** Builds technology from any setting he's bought on the [[Catalog/index|The Catalog]].
- **The System.** Shows **only his balance and the Catalog**. No stats, no quests, no notifications.
- **Points from conflict.** Anything that could escalate into an incident pays. Bigger pays more, and parahumans pay more. Every incident pays **Catalog Points**; only big ones also pay **Tech Tree Points**.
- **Powers for sale.** A setting's magic or native power (Chakra, the Force, Aura) can be bought separately.
- **Combining settings** works, with varying degrees of success, if any.
- **Automation** might break "Tinkertech doesn't scale". He doesn't care, and he isn't advertising it.
- **Canon knowledge.** All of Worm, and the fiction behind the expies.
- **The workshop** in Retro Rewind's basement, and a store full of fiction upstairs.

On **2 May 2011** *(proposed date)* a powers-out brawl in Winslow's cafeteria tears open the first public Gate, and the whole school drops into the Labyrinthos. **Joshua and Robyn are inside.** Three days of fighting surrounded by capes is the biggest points payout Leet will ever see, and the Labyrinthos walls are full of the substitute materials his catalogs need. Every student comes out with powers. See [[14 - The Powder Keg#Leet and the Powder Keg]].
