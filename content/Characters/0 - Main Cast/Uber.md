## Biography

The other half of [[Uber and Leet]]. A Thinker who can pick up **mastery of any skill** she focuses on. Her partner [[Leet]] is now a self-insert.

**In this story Uber is a woman:** Robyn, 17, a dark-skinned tomboy with a curvy build (big chest, big hips). She also **works at [[Retro Rewind]]**, Leet's store on [[The Boardwalk]], and goes to **[[Winslow High School]]** with him *(user ruling)*.

Technically a **Rogue**, like Leet. Treated as a villain for the same reason: they're dicks about it.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Skill mastery (Thinker).** Focus on a skill and she has it at a professional level: driving, shooting, fencing, parkour, cooking, whatever the stunt needs. She can only hold so many at once.
- **The stunts.** She's the one who does the physical part of Uber and Leet's video-game-themed jobs.
- **The counter.** She runs Retro Rewind's front of house.
