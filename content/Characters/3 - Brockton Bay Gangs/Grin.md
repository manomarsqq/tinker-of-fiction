## Biography

Grin arrived in Brockton Bay eighteen months ago with a dozen hard men, two capes and no history anyone can find. He took a corner of the Market by making the people standing on it too frightened to stay. He doesn't negotiate, doesn't honour the unwritten rules beyond the minimum, and has picked fights with every major faction at once without losing. Nobody knows what he wants the Market *for*.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Master 4:** an aura of dread that grows with proximity; people freeze, flee or break.
- **Pain tolerance** that suggests a Brute rating he hasn't shown.
- A crew of out-of-town professionals.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Amused, soft-spoken, always smiling. Talks like everything is a joke he's in on.

**Wants:** The Market, and whatever comes after it.

**Wrong about:** That fear holds territory as well as loyalty does.

**Breaking point:** Someone who isn't afraid of him.
