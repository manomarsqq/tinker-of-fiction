## Biography

A runaway the Chorus took in. On 2 April 2011 he was stabbed in a "trial" staged by the Chorus, and triggered on the floor: wings and beams of burning white light. The congregation fell to its knees. He's been treated as an angel for six days, and he's starting to believe it. The PRT filed the stabbing as a random assault.
