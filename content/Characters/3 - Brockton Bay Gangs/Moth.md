## Biography

A Filipino-American teenager whose parents run a bakery two doors from Wick's. She triggered during the ABB fire in March, flying out of a burning shop toward the streetlights. She flies on grey moth wings and is drawn toward light, which makes her fast between lanterns and very easy to track.
