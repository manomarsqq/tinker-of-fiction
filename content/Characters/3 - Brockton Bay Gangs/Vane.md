## Biography

A thin, silent cape who rides air currents between rooftops, high above the Market. She scouts for Grin, carries messages, and drops things on people from a great height. She came with Grin from wherever he came from, and doesn't seem to like him.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Mover 3:** rides and shapes wind currents; flight in gusts.
- **Blaster 1:** focused gusts that knock people down.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Hardly speaks. When she does, it's quiet and precise.

**Wants:** To be somewhere else.

**Wrong about:** That leaving Grin is possible.

**Breaking point:** Being ordered to kill a child.

## Relationships

- [[Grin]]: her leader, feared.
- [[Marrow]]: she keeps her distance.

## The powder keg

She sees every movement in the Market from above. If the Wake moves, it's on her information. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].
