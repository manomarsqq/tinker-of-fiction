## Biography

A Captain's Hill girl who triggered at a party where a friend overdosed. Now a bubble of euphoria spreads around her: fear drains away, pain dulls, people laugh. The Set uses her to make gang members too happy to fight back. Crowds under her effect do stupid, dangerous things, cheerfully.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Shaker 3 / Master 1:** an area of euphoria that suppresses fear and pain.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Giddy, warm, a little hollow.
