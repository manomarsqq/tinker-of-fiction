## Biography

The Ninety-Nine's leader. Her armband says 31: her whole extended family, lost with Kyushu. She triggered at the ABB's funeral for a boy she'd grown up with, a boy Lung had sent to die. She feels every member's grief and position and moves them as one silent body. The Fallen have been talking to her for weeks.
