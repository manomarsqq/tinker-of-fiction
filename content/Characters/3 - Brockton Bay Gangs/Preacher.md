## Biography

The Rust Saints' president since the 90s. He triggered in a New Hampshire county jail in 2009 and came out preaching. The men around him grow more devoted every month, until they'd die for him. In January 2011 he brought the club south to Brockton Bay because, he says, the dying city called to him.
