## Biography

Marrow looks like an ordinary person in a grey hoodie, until they fight. Then their body reshapes: longer arms, harder skin, too many joints. The PRT filed them as a Brute/Changer from a single sighting. Nobody has ever put Marrow in a scanner, and nobody has a reason to. Marrow eats a great deal, alone.
