## Biography

A welder's daughter from the Eastside yards. She triggered when a smuggling boat capsized in the harbour and she was the only one who came up. She can raise and lower the local tide and feel what moves in the water. She took over the Drowned in 2009 and turned it from a street crew into a smuggling operation.
