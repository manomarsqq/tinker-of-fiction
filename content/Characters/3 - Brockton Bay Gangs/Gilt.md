## Biography

The Pryce family's daughter and Escrow's niece. She triggered at a debutante ball when a man she trusted turned on her, and woke up wearing his face. She can wear any face she's seen. She gathers blackmail on council members and judges for Tidewater, and enjoys it far too much.
