## Biography

The only cape of [[Redline]], the city's fast-courier crew. Low-rated, and worth more than her rating by an order of magnitude.

Her power marks two points and **shortcuts anything moving fast between them**. A courier on a bike becomes a courier who arrives before she left. Redline owns the last mile in Brockton Bay because of her.

She's mixed, like much of Redline, and the Empire made that a problem.
