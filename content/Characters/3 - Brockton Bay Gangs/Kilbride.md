## Biography

A Southside boxer from an old Parish family. He triggered in the ring when an Empire-backed opponent tried to kill him. Every punch he lands makes the next one harder. The families don't know he's a cape; Father Keane does. He fights for the Parish at night in a balaclava, and has broken three Hollowmen's jaws.
