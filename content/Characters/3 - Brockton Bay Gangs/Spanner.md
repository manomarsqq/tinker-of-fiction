## Biography

A car thief and mechanic who triggered when a stolen car turned out to belong to a Tinker and tried to kill her. Her specialty is other people's work: she can jury-rig any Tinkertech so a non-Tinker can use it, for days or weeks before it fails. It turned a car-theft ring into the only market in the city for stolen Tinker gear.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Tinker 3 (jury-rig):** makes other Tinkers' devices usable by anyone, temporarily.
- Strips, sorts and rebuilds Tinkertech fast.
