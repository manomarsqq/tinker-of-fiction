## Biography

A Boston PD tactical officer who retired to Brockton Bay and lost his daughter in the Hollowmen's Southside arson. He triggered that night. He has told nobody, and he founded the Breakwater two months later to hunt the thing he became. He leads from the front, which is easy when bullets don't hurt.
