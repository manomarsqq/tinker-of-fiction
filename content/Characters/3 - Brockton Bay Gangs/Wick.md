## Biography

A Korean grocer in Little Saigon whose son, Min-jun, was taken by [[Yūrei]] in the February sweeps. He hung a paper lantern in the window and asked his neighbours to do the same. A week later, alone in the shop at night holding the lantern, he triggered. His hands burn with lantern light, and the light burns.
