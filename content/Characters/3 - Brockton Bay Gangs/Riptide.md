## Biography

A former city sewer engineer who triggered when a storm surge flooded the tunnel he was working in. He can push and pull water in currents strong enough to knock a Brute down. He built the Undertow out of other people the city had forgotten, and knows the drains better than the maps do. Eight months ago he lost four people to a "collapse". He's stopped believing that's what it was.
