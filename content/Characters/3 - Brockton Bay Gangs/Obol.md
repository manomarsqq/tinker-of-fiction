## Biography

A Ferryman's son who drowned free-diving the Boat Graveyard, and woke up on the bottom, breathing water. The Ferrymen put a coin in the mouths of their dead; they put one in Tommy's hand instead, and called him Obol. To them he's a sign that the water remembers, and that the Bay still owes them.
