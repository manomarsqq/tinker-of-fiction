## Biography

A quiet photography nerd who triggered in the car crash that made Varsity. She remembers everything she sees in perfect, frame-by-frame detail, and replays it at will. She edits the Set's videos, and has hours of footage of every fight they've been in: faces, costumes, licence plates.
