## Biography

The son of Councilman Robert Harrington. He wrapped his father's Porsche around a tree in October 2010, walked away, and found out why. Since then he's led the Arcadia Set on "hunts" through gang territory: beating up street dealers and Hollowmen, posting the videos, and going home to a gated house. He's never been hurt, and he's started to believe he can't be.
