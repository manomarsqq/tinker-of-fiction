## Biography

A Saint Anne's volunteer who triggered in a 2009 tenement fire, singing to the children trapped with her. Everyone in the room felt the same calm she did, and walked out through the smoke together. She took it as a sign. She left Saint Anne's in 2010 when they wouldn't follow her further, and took thirty people with her. She believes suffering calls angels down, and that the Chorus's job is to help them come.
