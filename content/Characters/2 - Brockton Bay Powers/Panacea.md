## Biography

**Amy Dallon.** The most strategically important seventeen-year-old on Earth. A member of [[New Wave]], adopted into the Dallon family, and the healer at Brockton General.

She rebuilds biology by touch. Cancer, organ loss, spinal damage, burns: trivial. Self-imposed absolute refusal: **brains**.

**She's the reason [[All Might]] is at full strength.** His MHA decline was a Quirk-system limit; Earth Bet has Panacea, and they share the Endbringer triage tent. They've met many times over the years. His injuries were never neurological. She thinks of it as a normal Tuesday.

She can't fix [[Eidolon]] (his decline is shard-related). She can't fix [[Emily Piggot|Piggot]], who refuses parahuman healing on principle.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Biokinesis by touch.** Full control of living biology she's touching, except her own.
- **The brain rule.** She won't touch brains.
- **Hospital hours** that never end.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Tired, flat, faintly irritable. Deflects thanks. Clinical about bodies, vague about everything else.

**Wants:** To not be looked at.

**Wrong about:** That the brain rule is about ethics rather than about what she's afraid she would do.

**Tendencies:** Says yes when she should say no. Withdraws rather than refuses. Resents the gratitude.

**Breaking point:** Being asked for something the brain rule forbids, by someone she can't refuse.

## Relationships

- [[New Wave]]: her adoptive family. Victoria (Glory Girl) is her sister.
- [[All Might]]: owes her his career.
- [[Brockton General Hospital]]: where she spends her life.
