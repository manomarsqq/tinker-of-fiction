## Biography

A Gesellschaft-raised cape who takes the shape of a massive eight-legged horse with a rider's harness. The Empire uses him to move people and packages across the city in minutes. He likes the Rust Saints, and they like him; he's the reason the Empire and the Saints are friendly.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Changer 2 / Mover 4:** eight-legged horse form; sprints at highway speed; carries two riders.

## Personality
