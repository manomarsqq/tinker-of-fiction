## Biography

**Hannah.** Second-in-command of Protectorate ENE, and the steady one. Her power manifests a weapon of green energy that takes the form of any gun or blade she needs, from a knife to a rocket launcher. She doesn't sleep, and hasn't since she triggered.

She's the adult in the room at the Rig. When Armsmaster retreats into his lab and Piggot retreats into policy, Miss Militia is the one who talks to the Wards, calms the troopers and handles the press. She absorbs the cost of it silently.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Weapon manifestation.** Any conventional weapon, from pistols to launchers, formed of green energy. She knows how to use all of them.
- **No sleep.** Endurance nobody else in the Protectorate can match.
- **Experience.** One of the longest-serving capes in the region.
