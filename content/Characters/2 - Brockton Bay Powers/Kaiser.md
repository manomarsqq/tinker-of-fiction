## Biography

**Max Anders.** CEO of [[Medhall Building|Medhall]], the pharmaceutical company that keeps Brockton Bay's lights on, and leader of [[Empire 88]], the city's largest cape roster. He inherited the Empire from his father, Allfather, and rebuilt it into something that wears a suit.

His base is the **deindustrialised white working class** of the [[North End]], with real grievances: the port died and nothing replaced it. That's what makes Kaiser dangerous rather than merely repellent. He delivers the ideology in boardroom register, and it lands.

The Empire is a dynasty. His lieutenants are more violent than he is, and he spends much of his energy keeping them leashed.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Shaker: blades.** Grows metal blades and spikes from any surface near him: walls, floors, the air around him.
- **Roster:** Hookwolf, Stormtiger, Cricket, Krieg, Fenja and Menja, Night and Fog, Victor, Othala, Rune, Alabaster. The largest in the city. **Purity recently split off.**
- **Medhall's money**, lawyers and political connections.
- **Client gang:** [[The Hollowmen]], who do what his capes can't be photographed doing.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Corporate, urbane, never crude. The ideology in boardroom register, which is what makes it frightening.

**Wants:** Legitimacy and dynasty.

**Wrong about:** That he controls what he's stoking.

**Tendencies:** Patient. Delegates atrocity. Never crude in person.

**Breaking point:** Losing Medhall, or losing Hookwolf's leash.
