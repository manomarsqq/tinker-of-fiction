## Biography

Director of **PRT East-North-East**, headquartered in Brockton Bay. She was a PRT agent at **Ellisburg** in 1993, when Nilbog's town fell, and came out of it maimed and with a permanent opinion about parahumans. She hates capes and runs a cape agency competently anyway.

Inside the PRT she's the face of the **reformers**: the people who can see the system failing and want it restructured. She's fighting the careerists (who protect budget and image) and the hardliners (who think capes are a weapons problem), and she answers upward to a Chief Director who is, unknown to her, Cauldron.

Her Bay is the densest cape city in North America per capita, with three major gangs, a dozen minor ones, a Wards team she can't fully audit, and a Protectorate leader who'd rather be in his lab.

## Capabilities

- **No powers**, and she refuses parahuman healing on principle.
- **PRT ENE's resources:** troopers, containment foam, specialist munitions, the budget, and the Brute-floor monopoly: nobody else can arrest a cape.
- **Institutional judgment.** She assumes bad faith and is usually right.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Blunt. Short sentences. Contempt held under visible control. Fatigue she refuses to name.

**Wants:** The city to survive the week.

**Wrong about:** That the danger is the capes.

**Tendencies:** Assumes bad faith and is often right. Chooses containment over engagement.

**Breaking point:** Being forced to rely on a cape she despises.
