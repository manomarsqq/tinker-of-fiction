## Biography

Kyushu-born, evacuated as a child. She triggered in a relocation camp and came to Brockton Bay with nothing. Lung gave her a job: walk into a shop, collect what's owed, walk out, and nobody remembers the face. Since February she's been collecting people too. The conscription sweeps through Little Saigon were hers.
