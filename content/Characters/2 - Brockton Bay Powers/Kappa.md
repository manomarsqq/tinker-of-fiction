## Biography

Nobody knows who Kappa was before. He turns into a scaled, beaked, amphibious thing and lives half in the water. Lung uses him to tax the harbour channels. Anyone moving a boat through the Docks pays the ABB, or Kappa pulls them under.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Changer 3:** amphibious form: gills, webbed claws, a hard shell.
- **Brute 2:** strong and durable in water.
- Can hold his breath for an hour.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Doesn't speak in his changed form. Chatty and bored out of it.

**Wants:** The water to himself.

**Wrong about:** That the water is his.

**Breaking point:** Something bigger in the water.
