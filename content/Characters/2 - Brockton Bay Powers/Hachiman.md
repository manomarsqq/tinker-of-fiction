## Biography

He served in the Japan Self-Defense Forces during the Kyushu evacuation and lost his whole unit when Leviathan came back. He triggered on the last helicopter out. In Brockton Bay he found Lung, and in Lung a commander who wouldn't leave his people behind. He drills the conscripts in a warehouse on the waterfront, and he's making them an army.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Brute 4:** armour-hard skin that thickens under fire.
- **Blaster 1:** short, concussive bursts from the palms.
- Military training, and an eye for it in others.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Clipped, formal Japanese-accented English. Treats everyone as a recruit.

**Wants:** Never to lose a unit again.

**Wrong about:** That an army of conscripts is protection rather than a target.

**Breaking point:** Losing a squad to a fight he didn't choose.
