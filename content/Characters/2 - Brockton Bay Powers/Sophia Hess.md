## Biography

**Shadow Stalker**, a Ward. Canon shadow state, canon trigger. One of the [[Wards ENE|Parahuman Three]].

Before she was a Ward she was a vigilante who hurt people, and she was put in the Wards on probation. She believes in predators and prey, and she's decided which one she is. At Winslow she's Emma's best friend and the muscle behind the campaign against [[Taylor Hebert]], including the locker.

The Wards program is legally indefensible, and now that Sky High and Vought exist, the only kids left in it are the ones with no other option: exactly Sophia. She's been banking **$50,000 a year in trust** while using the position to torment a classmate. It vests when she turns eighteen.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Breaker 3.** Shifts into a shadow state: lighter, faster, able to pass through solid matter.
- **Crossbow** with tranquiliser and bladed bolts.
- **Legal protection** under the Ward Protection Doctrine. Her records are sealed.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Terse, contemptuous, restless. Talks about the strong and the weak.

**Wants:** To be the predator. To be proven right about how the world works.

**Wrong about:** That she earned any of it.

**Breaking point:** Being made prey, in public, by someone she wrote off.
