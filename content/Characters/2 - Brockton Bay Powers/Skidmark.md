## Biography

**Adam Mustain.** Leader of [[The Merchants]], filed by the PRT as a mouthy junkie running the city's worst drug gang. In fact he's running **the premier parahuman black market on the East Coast**.

Legitimate shipping is dead worldwide. Squealer built the Merchants a fleet in 2009–2010 (flying warehouses, armoured convoys, stealth ships, submarines) and Skidmark turned it into the only reliable unaffiliated freight network on the seaboard. The Merchants out-earn Empire 88 and the ABB combined.

Same mechanism as [[Greg Veder]]: the information is public, the delivery discredits it, and the working explanation is never falsified.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Shaker.** Creates fields that accelerate or repel anything crossing them.
- **The Merchant Markets:** logistics, fencing, smuggling, and a seat on the [[The League of Villains|League]]'s council.
- **Genuinely sharp operational judgment** that nobody hears.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Relentlessly profane. The obscenity wraps sophisticated routing, timing and risk decisions nobody hears, because everyone stops listening at the third word.
