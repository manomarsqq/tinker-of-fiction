## Biography

**Sherrel.** Tinker, vehicles specialist, [[Skidmark]]'s partner, and the engine behind the Merchants' rise. She built the Merchant fleet in 2009–2010: flying warehouses, armoured convoys, stealth ships and submarines. Her specialty turned out to be the single most valuable capability in a deglobalised world, by accident.

She works out of a workshop at [[The Boat Graveyard]], high most of the time, lucid in bursts about engines.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Tinker (vehicles).** Anything that moves: trucks, boats, subs, aircraft. Big, armoured and ugly.
- **The fleet.** The Merchants' entire logistics network runs on her builds.
- **Can't stop improving a finished thing.**

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Technical, drug-addled, abruptly lucid in bursts about vehicles. Loses the thread on anything else.
