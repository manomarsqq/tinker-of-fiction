## Biography

A Ward, and the most effective PR asset the PRT ENE has. Cape name *(TBD)*.

**Power:** not mind control. People's *social* read of her bends toward the flattering: she's whoever the room needs, and **memories of her adjust after the fact**. Every teacher sincerely remembers Emma as lovely.

Her crime against Taylor has always been rewriting the social record. The power makes it literal, and answers the question canon never quite does: **why does nobody believe Taylor?**

**Trigger:** the alley attack (canon event), rescued by [[Sophia Hess]]. That rescue remade her. She was Taylor's best friend, and then she wasn't. **PRT view:** loves her. Excellent on camera.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Master 3 / Stranger 2.** Social perception bends in her favour. People remember her more kindly than they experienced her.
- **Not combat-useful.** She's a PR asset, not a fighter.
- **Legal protection** under the Ward Protection Doctrine.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Bright, confident, practised. Cruel only where it counts.

**Wants:** To be strong, after the alley taught her she wasn't.

**Wrong about:** That her power is who she is. It's who everyone else thinks she is.

**Breaking point:** Being seen clearly, by someone her power doesn't reach.

## Relationships
