## Biography

A forensic accountant who triggered during an audit that ended his career. He reads paperwork the way Tattletale reads people: any document shows him the debts, secrets and pressure points behind it. Coil uses him to buy the city: Tidewater's debts, council members' mortgages, the Breakwater's funding.
