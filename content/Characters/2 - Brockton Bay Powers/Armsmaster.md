## Biography

**Colin Wallis**, leader of **Protectorate ENE**. A Tinker whose specialty is **efficiency**: compressing, miniaturising and optimising technology until it does more with less. His halberd is the most famous piece of Tinkertech in the Bay.

He's one of the **decapitation school** (with [[Dragon]]), who believe the Endbringers have cores that can be reached and killed. He's treated as a crank with a hobby. He's about to be one of the only people who was thinking about the right problem.

He wants recognition badly, takes credit clumsily, and works alone because working with people is inefficient.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Tinker (efficiency).** Everything he builds is smaller, lighter and more integrated than it should be.
- **The halberd:** multi-tool weapon with a containment-foam dispenser, grappling line, electrical and blade modes.
- **Armour** with combat-prediction software built from recorded fights.
- **A theory nobody funds** about Endbringer cores.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Clipped, efficiency-obsessed, socially graceless, precise. Interrupts to correct.

**Wants:** To matter.

**Wrong about:** His priorities, constantly. RIGHT about the cores, which nobody will hear.

**Tendencies:** Optimises the wrong variable. Takes credit clumsily. Works alone.

**Breaking point:** Being proven right in a way that costs someone else everything.
