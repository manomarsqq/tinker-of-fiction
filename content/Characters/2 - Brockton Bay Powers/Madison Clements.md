## Biography

A Ward, marketed as **harmless, cute, marketable**. Cape name *(TBD)*.

**Power:** fine control over small objects. Things go wrong at precisely the wrong moment: a flute won't stay in its case, juice tips, a locker latch sticks.

A petty power, because her cruelty was always petty. And a deliberate mirror: **Madison got the sanitized, marketable version of Taylor's power** (control over small things) and a Wards slot for it.

**Trigger** *(proposed)*: the bullying itself, from fear of what she was becoming. Same event stream as Taylor's. One of them got a costume and a stipend; the other got a locker and a monster.
