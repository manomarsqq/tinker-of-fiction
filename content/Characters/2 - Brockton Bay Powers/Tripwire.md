## Biography

A Tinker who can only build things that wait: traps, sensors, mines, alarms. Coil found him in a cheap motel after his last employer died, and gave him an underground base to fill. Every corridor of Coil's base is his.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Tinker 4 (traps):** sensors, mines, snares, lockdown systems, all hard to detect.
- Paranoia, professionally.
