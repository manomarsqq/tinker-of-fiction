## Biography

A port shipping clerk who lost her job when the port died, and triggered the night she realised nobody would ever hire her again. She sees every shipment she knows about as a live, moving map: routes, timings, risks. She plans the Merchant fleet's movements. Skidmark takes the credit, and she lets him, because being invisible is safe.
