## Biography

A merchant seaman who triggered when his ship went down in the Graveyard. He controls buoyancy: he can sink a boat gently to the bottom and raise it again undamaged. The Merchants hide cargo, and sometimes whole boats, on the harbour floor until they're needed.

---

## Powers & capabilities

- **Mover 3 / Shaker 2:** controls the buoyancy of objects and vessels in water.
