## Biography

**Thomas Calvert.** Ex-military, now a PRT consultant, and secretly the warlord building a private army under Brockton Bay. He funds the [[The Undersiders|Undersiders]] (and owns [[Tattletale]] outright), runs mercenaries, and plans to take the city.

His power splits the world. He makes a choice, lives both branches at once, and keeps the one he prefers. Every interaction with him has been **tested first**. He's patient, never in the room, and faintly amused by everything.

**He takes [[Dinah Alcott]] in May** *(canon)*. With a precog feeding his split timelines, he believes the city is already his.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Timeline split.** Two simultaneous realities; he keeps one and discards the other. Only two at once.
- **The PRT consultancy.** Inside access to the agency hunting him.
- **A private army** of mercenaries and a base under the city.
- **The Undersiders**, who don't know how owned they are.
