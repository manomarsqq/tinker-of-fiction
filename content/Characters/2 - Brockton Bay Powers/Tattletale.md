## Biography

**Lisa Wilbourn.** Thinker, member of [[The Undersiders]], and [[Coil]]'s property. Her power tells her things she shouldn't know, from tiny clues, as if her mind skipped the reasoning. More precisely: it tells her when **an explanation doesn't fit the evidence**.

In a world whose central failure is that **everyone has a working explanation that is wrong**, she's the single most dangerous person alive, and she doesn't know it. She's spending it on card games and blackmail, because she's seventeen and owned by a man with a private army.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Thinker.** Intuitive leaps from minimal evidence. Names, secrets, motives, lies.
- **Migraines** when she pushes too hard.
- **Information network** she's quietly building for herself.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Fast, mocking, deflective. Hides real insight inside jokes so she's never held to it.

**Wants:** Out from under Coil.

**Wrong about:** What she's for.

**Tendencies:** Deflects with jokes. Overshares to destabilise. Can't resist showing off.

**Breaking point:** Being genuinely outsmarted, or Coil turning on the team.
