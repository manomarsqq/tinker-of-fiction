## Biography

**Kenta.** Warlord of the [[ABB]] and the strongest cape in Brockton Bay that anyone knows about. He's fought Endbringers and walked away, and he runs the Docks the way a feudal lord runs a province: protection, tribute, and absolute punishment for disrespect.

His real power base is the **Kyushu diaspora**. Kyushu sank in 1999 and millions were displaced into dead port districts worldwide. Brockton Bay's Docks are full of them: a displaced people with no economic future in a city with no port, who can't even fall back on fishing because the ocean now kills fishermen. The ABB recruits from a specific, dated, traceable catastrophe, and what Lung offers isn't money. It's dignity and protection from a man stronger than anything the city can send.

He honours the **Endbringer Truce** absolutely. He doesn't forget anything.

## Powers & capabilities

- **Escalation.** The longer he fights, the stronger he gets: bigger, tougher, hotter, faster. Given time he becomes a dragon.
- **Pyrokinesis** that scales with the escalation.
- **Regeneration.** Very hard to put down, and nearly impossible to keep down.
- **Weakness:** ramp-up time. Hit him hard and early or not at all.
- **Roster:** Oni Lee, and from May, **Bakuda**, who gives him the ability to level blocks.

## Personality

**Temperament / voice:** Few words. Heavy accent. Absolute. His speech escalates the way his power does.

**Wants:** Respect, territory, and the diaspora protected on his terms.

**Wrong about:** That the city will always be enough.

**Tendencies:** Escalates rather than negotiates. Honours the Truce absolutely. Doesn't forget.

**Breaking point:** Public humiliation by someone he considers beneath him.

## Relationships

- [[ABB]]: his organisation. Oni Lee and, soon, Bakuda.
