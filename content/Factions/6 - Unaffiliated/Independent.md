## Overview

Catch-all for characters with no organisation: [[Taylor Hebert]], [[Greg Veder]] and [[Dinah Alcott]].

In Brockton Bay, independence is dangerous. The Brute floor means any cape can break the law and walk away from police, and every gang wants every new cape. Independents are either strong enough to refuse, invisible, or about to be recruited.

## Who's here

- **[[Taylor Hebert]]**: a new cape on her first night out.
- **[[Greg Veder]]**: a Lung-tier cape nobody believes in.
- **[[Dinah Alcott]]**: a precog, until Coil takes her.

## Uber and Leet and the independents

Uber and Leet have a faction of their own, but they're independents in every way that matters: no patron, no backup.
