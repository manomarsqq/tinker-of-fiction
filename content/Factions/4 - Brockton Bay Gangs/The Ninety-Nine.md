## Overview

**Mostly unpowered**, now with a few capes of its own (see *The powder keg* below). Kyushu-diaspora youth who refused the ABB.

- **Look:** white armbands with a number: how many of that member's family died.
- **Style:** silent, disciplined, coordinated swarms. They keep a count of everything.

## Dogma

Grief turned into discipline. They keep a count of everything, and they won't let anyone profit from their dead, which is exactly what they think Lung does.
