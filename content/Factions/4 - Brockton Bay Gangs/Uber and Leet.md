## Overview

[[Uber]] and [[Leet]], the protagonist's duo. Their stunts are themed on video games.

**Standing:** technically **Rogues**. The Bay, PHO and the PRT treat them as **villains** anyway, because they're dicks about it.

## Members

- **[[Leet]]** (Joshua, 17): Tinker of Fiction.
- **[[Uber]]** (Robyn, 17): skill-mastery Thinker.

## Base

The basement of **[[Retro Rewind]]**, Joshua's rental store on [[The Boardwalk]]. Robyn works the counter upstairs.

## How they operate

- **Stunts themed on video games**, done in costume.
- **Being dicks about it**, which is why nobody believes the Rogue label.
- **Points:** every conflict that could escalate into an incident pays Leet's System. Bigger pays more; parahumans pay more.
