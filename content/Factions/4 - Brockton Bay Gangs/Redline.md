## Overview

Last-mile couriers. **1 cape** ([[Checkpoint]]). Fast, small, deniable deliveries. Reflective striping, helmets never off, even indoors. The Merchants own bulk; Redline owns speed.

## History

A bike-courier crew that got a cape. Checkpoint's power turned it from a hustle into a business, and made it worth Coil's money.

## Roster

- **[[Checkpoint]]**: the cape.
- **Couriers**: bikes, motorbikes, cars. A mixed crew.

## Territory & operations

- **Territory:** downtown and the highways.
- **Business:** fast, deniable, last-mile deliveries. Anything small that has to be somewhere else now.
