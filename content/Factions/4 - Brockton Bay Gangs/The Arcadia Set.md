## Overview

**Rich-kid vigilantes** from Arcadia High (the good school where the Wards go) and Captain's Hill. Three capes and a dozen hangers-on who film everything. They hunt gang members for fun, post the videos, and go home to houses with gates.

They're the same age as Uber and Leet, with better lawyers and worse judgment.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

Formed in **autumn 2010** when [[Varsity]] triggered in a car crash and his friends decided it was the best thing that ever happened. Their videos have hundreds of thousands of views. Their parents' lawyers handle the rest.

## Capes

- **[[Varsity]]** (Brute 4): Leader of the Arcadia Set. A rich-kid Brute from Captain's Hill who thinks the city is a game, and films himself winning it.
- **[[Afterparty]]** (Shaker 3 / Master 1): The euphoria. Fills a room with euphoria. Fights turn into parties, and parties turn into riots.
- **[[Snapshot]]** (Thinker 3): The camera. Perfect visual recall, frame by frame. She films everything, and remembers it better than the tape.

## Territory

None. Captain's Hill is home; the rest of the city is a playground.

## The powder keg

They're the likeliest people in the city to kill someone important by accident, on camera. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].

## Uber and Leet and The Arcadia Set

**Rivals.** Uber and Leet are also 17-year-olds doing stunts for an audience. The Arcadia Set has money, lawyers and no villain label. They'd love to take Uber and Leet down on camera.
