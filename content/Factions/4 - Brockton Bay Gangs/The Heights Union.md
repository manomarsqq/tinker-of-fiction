## Overview

A **tenants' union** in the Heights, Brockton Bay's historically Black neighbourhood, that became an **armed community defence** after the Hollowmen started evicting families for the Empire's property partners. They run patrols, a legal clinic, a food co-op, and a network of watchers on every block.

They're not a gang. The PRT files them as one anyway.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

In **February 2011** the Hollowmen burned out a building on Fairmount Street after its tenants refused to leave. The tenants' union armed itself the next week. In **March**, a Hollowman died in a fight on Fairmount; the Union says it was self-defence. [[Whitehand]] branded a Union organiser in reply.

## Capes

- **[[Keystone]]** (Shaker 4): Holds the block. Anchors a forcefield to the building he's in. Nothing gets in while he's standing.
- **[[Bellwether]]** (Thinker 3): The watcher. Feels a crowd's mood, and knows when it's about to turn.

## Territory

**The Heights**, a hill neighbourhood between downtown and the North End. Front line with the Empire.

## Business

- A food co-op, a legal clinic, dues.
- Armed patrols.
- Voter drives against the Civil Defense Ordinance.

## The powder keg

The Heights is where the Empire and the city's Black community meet, and one death has already happened. The next one decides whether it's a feud or a war. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].
