## Overview

**Mostly unpowered**, now with a few capes of its own (see *The powder keg* below). Ex-dockworkers, fishermen's sons, second-generation diaspora and old Bay families.
- **Business:** small-boat smuggling through channels nobody else can navigate.
- **Look:** oilskins, gaff hooks, faces painted with a coin over the mouth.
- **Dogma:** the water remembers, and the Bay is owed. A coin in the mouth of their dead.
- **Capability:** free-dive the Graveyard. Peak human from years of hauling.

**Hook:** they know the Boat Graveyard better than anyone alive. On **15 May** that's the most valuable knowledge in the city.

## Uber and Leet and the Ferrymen

Small-boat smugglers who free-dive the Graveyard would pay for good diving gear, and [[Subnautica]] and [[Barotrauma]] are on the Catalog. After 15 May, the Ferrymen are the only people who know where everything sank.
