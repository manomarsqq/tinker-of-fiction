## Overview

**The Drowned.** A Latino crew from the **Eastside shipyards**, where the families of welders and fitters stayed after the yards closed. They run small-boat smuggling and street dealing, and fight the Empire on the Eastside's edge.

They named themselves after the men who drowned when the last ship launched from the yard sank in the harbour, which is also the kind of joke they make.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

The shipyards closed in the late 90s. The crew formed in the 2000s as neighbourhood protection, then moved into smuggling. They compete with the Ferrymen for the few safe channels and with the Merchants for the street trade.

## Capes

- **[[La Marea]]** (Shaker 3 / Thinker 2): Leader of the Drowned. Moves the tide in the harbour, and feels everything that moves in the water.
- **[[Anzuelo]]** (Striker 3 / Mover 1): The hook. Anything he touches, he can yank back to himself later. Wallets, guns, people.

## Territory

**The Eastside shipyards** and the streets around them.
