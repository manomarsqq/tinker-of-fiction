## Overview

An **anti-parahuman civilian militia**: ex-cops, ex-soldiers, dockworkers and the families of people killed in cape fights. The police can't touch a cape (the Brute floor), and the PRT is stretched thin, so the Breakwater decided to do it themselves. They carry **black-market PRT containment foam** and Tinkertech bought, with some irony, from [[The Merchants]].

They call their raids "citizen's arrests". Capes call them lynchings.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

Formed in **late 2010** after a Hollowmen arson killed a family in Southside and nobody was charged. In **March 2011** they made their first "arrest": a fourteen-year-old ABB conscript, caught shoplifting with a minor Brute power, foamed and left for the PRT. He suffocated in the foam before the van arrived. Half the city called it murder. The other half sent donations.

## Capes

- **[[Seawall]]** (Brute 4 / Shaker 2): Commander (secretly a cape). The Breakwater's commander, ex-Boston PD. Secretly a cape, and he hates it.

## Territory

**Southside**: working-class neighbourhoods south of downtown, mixed and tired. They patrol in pickup trucks with foam rigs in the bed.

## Business

- Donations, and they're growing.
- Black-market foam, sprayers and Tinkertech, bought through cut-outs from the Merchants.
- Training nights in a closed gym.

## The powder keg

The Breakwater is how a gang war becomes a **civil** war: ordinary people with weapons that work on capes. If the **Civil Defense Ordinance** passes on **3 May**, they become legal. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].

## Uber and Leet and The Breakwater

Uber and Leet are capes filed as villains. The Breakwater would foam them on sight, and foam is exactly what Leet can't Tinker his way out of without planning.
