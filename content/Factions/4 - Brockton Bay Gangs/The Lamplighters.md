## Overview

A **shopkeepers' defence association** of Korean, Filipino and Vietnamese families on the edge of the Docks. The ABB treats every Asian family in the city as a recruiting pool. The Lamplighters refuse. A paper lantern in a shop window means *this family is under our protection; we will not be conscripted.*

They're the reason the ABB has to fight for every block it used to take for granted.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

In **late February 2011** the ABB ran conscription sweeps through Little Saigon ahead of Bakuda's arrival. A Korean grocer's son was taken. His father hung a lantern in the window and asked his neighbours to do the same. Within a month there were two hundred lanterns. In **March** the ABB burned one of the shops. The lanterns stayed up.

## Capes

- **[[Wick]]** (Blaster 3): The grocer. The grocer whose son was taken. He hung the first lantern, and his hands burn like one.
- **[[Moth]]** (Mover 3): The messenger. Flies on dusty wings toward any light. Carries messages between the lantern shops.

## Territory

**Little Saigon** and the streets on the edge of the Docks.

## The powder keg

Bakuda arrives in May needing recruits. The Lamplighters are the obvious target, and burning out two hundred lanterns would be a massacre. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].

## Uber and Leet and The Lamplighters

The Lamplighters are ordinary families standing up to the most dangerous cape in the Bay. They would be very grateful for help, and Lung would be very angry.
