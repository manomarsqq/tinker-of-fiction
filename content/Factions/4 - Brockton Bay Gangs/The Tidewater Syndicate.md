## Overview

**Old-money organised crime** in the wealthy neighbourhoods of Captain's Hill and the Lord's Port marina. The Tidewater families were shipping money before the port died. Now they do loans, real estate, construction contracts, and council votes. They never touch a gun themselves.

They're buying the city block by block, cheaply, from people who are scared. A war would make it cheaper.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

The Tidewater families (the Whitmores, the Castellanos and the Pryces) ran the port's insurance and shipping finance. When the port died they moved into property. Over the last two years **Coil** quietly bought their debts, and now owns them without their knowing who he is.

## Capes

- **[[Escrow]]** (Trump 3 / Thinker 2): Head of the Whitmore family. Anyone who breaks a deal made in his presence suffers for it. Tidewater's contracts enforce themselves.
- **[[Gilt]]** (Changer 3 / Stranger 2): Blackmailer. Wears any face she's seen. Tidewater's blackmailer.

## Territory

**Captain's Hill** and **Lord's Port**: the rich neighbourhoods. Their real territory is the city's land registry.

## Business

- Loan-sharking to businesses the banks won't touch.
- Buying property in contested districts.
- Construction and demolition contracts.
- Council votes.
