## Overview

A **salvage and fencing** operation that deals in the one commodity nobody else can: **stolen Tinkertech**. After every cape fight in the city, the Chop Shop's scavengers are there, stripping armour, weapons and gadgets from the losers. They sell the parts to anyone.

Tinkertech doesn't work for long without its maker, but even broken Tinkertech has parts nobody else can make.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

Started as a car-theft ring in the Market. When [[Spanner]] triggered and found she could make other Tinkers' tech work, for a while, the business changed overnight.

## Capes

- **[[Spanner]]** (Tinker 3 (jury-rig)): The mechanic. Makes other Tinkers' tech work for anyone, for a while. The reason stolen Tinkertech is worth stealing.
- **[[Magpie]]** (Thinker 3): The finder. Senses Tinkertech at a distance, like a magpie spotting something shiny.

## Territory

A garage in **the Market** and a hulk in **the Boat Graveyard**.

## Business

- Stripping Tinkertech after fights.
- Selling parts, and short-term repairs.
- Stealing from Tinkers directly.

## The powder keg

Every fight in the city feeds them, and every part they sell arms the next fight. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].

## Uber and Leet and The Chop Shop

**Direct threat.** Anything Leet builds and loses ends up here, and Spanner can make it work for someone else. Leet's canon limit is gone, so his tech is more valuable than any Tinker's in the city.
