## Overview

An **outlaw biker club** from upstate that rode into Brockton Bay in **January 2011** and hasn't left. Meth, guns, and highway robbery. They've been hijacking Merchant convoys on the interstate, which nobody else has ever dared to do.

They don't care about the unwritten rules because they aren't from here.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

The Saints were a New Hampshire club until their president, **[[Preacher]]**, triggered and started hearing what he calls the Word. He brought the club south to "the dying city" because it called to him. They've taken a closed truck stop on the industrial belt as their chapter house.

## Capes

- **[[Preacher]]** (Master 4): President of the Rust Saints. The club president. He hears 'the Word', and his men come to hear it through him.
- **[[Axle]]** (Changer 3 / Mover 3): Road captain. Fused with his motorcycle in a crash. Half man, half machine, all road.
- **[[Gasoline]]** (Blaster 4): Arsonist. Ignites fuel and fumes at a distance. Convoys explode when she's around.

## Territory

**The industrial belt** along the old rail spur, and the **I-93 corridor** into the city.

## Business
