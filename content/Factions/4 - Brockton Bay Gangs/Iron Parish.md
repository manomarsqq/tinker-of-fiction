## Overview

The remains of the **old Irish waterfront mob**: families who ran the docks' unions, rackets and pubs for a century. They're Catholic, loyal to the [[Dockworkers' Association]], and furious that the Empire keeps recruiting their sons.

They're white working class, like the Empire's base, and they despise the Empire. That makes Southside a front line.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

Iron Parish ran waterfront protection for decades until the port died and the capes arrived. Now it's a few families, a parish church (**Saint Brendan's**), three pubs, and the pride of people who used to matter. In **February 2011** the Empire recruited two Parish boys; one came home with a white-handprint tattoo. The families haven't forgiven it.

## Capes

- **[[Kilbride]]** (Brute 3 / Striker 2): The Parish's fist. A boxer whose punches build power as a fight goes on. The Parish's secret cape.
- **[[Mourne]]** (Shaker 3): The fog. Calls sea fog thick enough to lose a street in. Southside disappears when she's angry.

## Territory

**Southside**, around Saint Brendan's. They share it uneasily with the Breakwater.

## The powder keg

If the Empire recruits one more Parish son, or one comes home dead, Southside splits white working-class Brockton Bay down the middle. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].
