## Overview

A **trigger cult** in the Towers, the apartment blocks north of downtown. The Chorus split from [[Saint Anne's]] and went all the way: powers are angels descending into the chosen, and **suffering calls them down**. So the Chorus helps. It stages ordeals for its young members.

On **2 April 2011** it worked. A sixteen-year-old member was stabbed in a staged "trial" and triggered. The PRT filed it as a random assault.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

A small group of Saint Anne's volunteers who took the word *Blessed* literally. Their leader, **[[Hymn]]**, triggered after a fire in 2009, and has been preaching since. They took over three floors of a Tower block in 2010.

## Capes

- **[[Hymn]]** (Master 4): Leader of the Chorus. When she sings, her congregation becomes one mind. She believes she's an angel's vessel.
- **[[Seraph]]** (Blaster 5 / Mover 2): The first angel. Triggered in a staged ordeal on 2 April 2011. Wings and beams of burning light.

## The powder keg

A cult manufacturing capes in the middle of a gang war is adding fuel faster than anyone can see. When the city learns the Chorus *makes* capes, everyone will want them, or want them gone. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].

## Uber and Leet and The Chorus

A cult that makes capes by hurting children. Leet knows triggers from canon, and knows this works.
