## Overview

**Unpowered.** Bookmakers. Dog fights, cape fights, Endbringer pools, which gang holds which block by Friday. Deliberately forgettable. They never fight; they hire. Neutral by policy.

## Operations

- **Betting** on everything in the city.
- **Data.** They hold the best raw data in Brockton Bay.
- **Hiring.** When they need muscle, they buy it.

## Relations

Neutral by policy, to everyone.

## Uber and Leet and the Tally

Uber and Leet's stunts are exactly the kind of thing the Tally takes bets on. Leet knows canon outcomes. That's a very profitable combination, and a very noticeable one.
