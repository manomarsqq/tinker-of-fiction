## Overview

A union with no docks. [[Danny Hebert]]'s hiring hall places maybe **a third of its members** in a given month, at ~$18/hr when there's work. That's exactly why his daughter's school doesn't care what happens to her.

## History

Brockton Bay was a port city. Then intercontinental shipping collapsed (Leviathan, Kyushu, the Reemergence megafauna) and the port died with it. The DWA was a powerful union in a powerful port. Now it's a hiring hall, a food line and a political voice for a waterfront nobody invests in.

## Membership

- **[[Danny Hebert]]**: runs the hiring hall.
- **Hundreds of dockworkers**, most out of work most of the time.
- **Families** who've been on the docks for generations. Some of their sons are [[The Ferrymen]].

## Assets

- **[[Dockworkers' Association Hall]]**, the hiring hall.
- **Political weight** in city hall, fading.
- **Relationships** with every business on the waterfront, including the ones that aren't legitimate.

## 8 April 2011 and after

Barely surviving. After **15 May**, Leviathan wrecks the waterfront, and the DWA becomes a relief organisation overnight.

## Uber and Leet and the DWA

A Tinker with automation and fabrication catalogs could put the Docks back to work. That's also the kind of thing that makes the Merchants, the ABB and the PRT all pay attention at once.
