## Overview

**Unpowered.** Not a gang, a guild. Doormen, security and fixers enforcing **the Boardwalk's neutrality**. Matching grey coats, no visible weapons.

They can't stop a cape and everyone knows it, so they don't fight capes. They **escort them out**, and the capes go, because the arrangement works and nobody wants to be the one who broke it.

## History

The Boardwalk survived the port's death because it's where the money goes to spend itself. The shop owners hired security. Security organised. Then the gangs agreed to leave the Boardwalk alone, because every gang had people who wanted to shop there. The Concord became the thing that held the agreement.

## Membership

- **Doormen, bouncers, security staff, fixers**: hundreds of them.
- **Grey coats.** No weapons shown.
- **The businesses** of the Boardwalk, who pay dues.

## Operations

- **Escort.** A cape who causes trouble on the Boardwalk is walked off it.
- **Fixing.** Deals between enemies, brokered on neutral ground.
- **Security** for the shops, including [[Retro Rewind]].
