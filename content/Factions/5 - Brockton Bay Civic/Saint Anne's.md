## Overview

**Unpowered.** A church-basement mutual aid group formed after the port died: a protection network, food bank, and a parallel justice system that works better than the city's. No colours at all, which is itself the statement.

## History

When the port died, the parish of Saint Anne's in the [[Deep Docks]] started feeding people. Then protecting them. Then settling disputes, because the police wouldn't come and the gangs wouldn't be fair. Over a decade it became an institution.

## Membership

- **Parishioners, volunteers, retired dockworkers**.
- **Leadership:** a small council around the church. *(names open)*
- **No capes.** They'd shelter one.

## Operations

- A **food bank** and shelter.
- A **protection network**: neighbours watching neighbours.
- A **parallel justice system**: disputes settled in the basement. People abide by it.

They refuse protection money, and survive because breaking them would cost more than it's worth and because they're genuinely and dangerously beloved.
