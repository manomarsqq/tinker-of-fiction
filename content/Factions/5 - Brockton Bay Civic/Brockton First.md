## Overview

A **political movement** built around Councilman **Dale Kerrigan**, a talk-radio host and a slogan: *"Brockton First: our city, our streets, our safety."* It wants the city to arm and deputise civilian militias, to petition the state to bring in PRT hardliners, and (quietly) for the US to sign the Yangban's **Reciprocal Response Protocol**.

It's the political wing of the civil war before there's a civil war.

*(Proposed: added 2026-09-26 for the powder keg.)*

## History

Kerrigan's campaign grew out of the Breakwater's first "arrest" in March 2011. On **28 March** he filed the **Civil Defense Ordinance**: deputised neighbourhood militias with access to containment equipment. The council vote is set for **Tuesday 3 May 2011**. It's currently one vote short.

## Capes

## The powder keg

The **3 May vote** was supposed to be the spark. Instead it happens **the morning after [[The Winslow Gate|Winslow falls into the Labyrinthos]]**, with fourteen hundred newly powered teenagers loose in the city and every parent terrified. If it passes, the Breakwater gets legal cover, the Heights and the Docks arm to resist it, and the Empire gets a militia to do its work. If it fails by one vote, the Breakwater decides the system has failed them. See [[14 - The Powder Keg]].

## Uber and Leet and Brockton First

A movement that wants capes controlled is a movement that wants Uber and Leet in a cell. Also a very easy target for a stunt, and a very bad idea.
