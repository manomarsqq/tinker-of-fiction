## Overview

Nine people. Goal unchanged from canon: **manufacture enough power to kill [[Scion]]**, fund it by selling vials, accept any atrocity.

**Why it is obsolete, and doesn't know**
- The vial supply is a **corpse**, and it is depleting. [[Number Man]] knows the exact date it runs out.
- Its monopoly is broken twice over, by [[All For One]] and by Compound V ([[Vought International]]). Neither is finite.
- A growing share of Earth's powered population is **shardless**, so Cauldron can't see it: [[All Might]], [[Homelander]], every Vought supe, every expy.
- **Endbringers Untethered** means Cauldron's theory of the Endbringers is wrong. [[Eidolon]] still *fears* they're his; they came online by themselves.

**Pricing:** seven figures minimum, but they prefer **obligations**. Cash is spent once; a favour compounds. Prices have risen quietly for a decade.

**The law of Cauldron:** the transaction is unprovable. Nobody who took a vial has ever identified the supplier. [[Contessa]] ensures it.

**Path to Victory is NOT degrading** *(ruling)*. The new power systems are blind spots, but Contessa paths around them with knowledge, the same way she handles Eidolon.

## Structure

- **[[Doctor Mother]]**: the will. Founder.
- **[[Contessa]]**: the instrument. Path to Victory.
- **[[Alexandria]]**: Chief Director of the PRT, Triumvirate.
- **[[Eidolon]]**: Triumvirate, declining.
- **[[Number Man]]**: the ledger.
- **The Doormaker and the Clairvoyant**: the paired capes whose powers give Cauldron its portals and its sight.
- **Legend**: Triumvirate, kept away from the worst of it.

**The base** sits outside reality, reached only through the Doormaker's portals. White corridors, labs, and the Case 53 holding areas.

## The trade

- **Vials** sold to the rich and desperate, each a sample from Eden's corpse.
- **Case 53s**, the failures, memory-wiped and dumped.
- **Funding** for hero teams worldwide, so the cape ecosystem stays broad.

## In Brockton Bay

No presence anyone can see. The Bay's Case 53s ([[Faultline's Crew]]) are its failures. The Bay's Chief Director is its member.

## Where it's heading

- **15 May 2011:** Harbingers. The first sign Cauldron's Endbringer model is wrong.
- **2012:** Nemesis, the Legions reveal, the Lantern Corps. More blind spots than sight.
- **2013:** Tithonus, who speaks buried truths. Cauldron is the most buried truth there is.
- **November 2013:** Golden Morning, on schedule. The answer turns out to be shardless, and on the shelf at a toy store.

## Uber and Leet and Cauldron

Leet's shard is **Eden's**, and it was tampered with by something that isn't a shard. To Cauldron that's either a threat to contain or a resource to harvest. Leet knows everything they are. They know nothing about him, for now.
