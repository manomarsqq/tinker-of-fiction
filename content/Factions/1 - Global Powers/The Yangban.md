## Overview

Deploys globally against Endbringers and S-class threats, on par with the Protectorate. **Payment is parahuman prisoners**, handed over by whichever nation asked for help (the [[03 - Law and Institutions#The Reciprocal Response Protocol|Reciprocal Response Protocol]]).

**Doctrine:** powers distributed among numbered operatives by a Trump. Identity erased. Capes interchangeable and expendable. A hundred operatives running the same five powers: drilled, coordinated, no egos. Their Trumps temporarily boost allied capes, which is the real reason nations invite them.

**Why it matters:** at S-class response it **outperforms the Protectorate**, because it's an *army* and the Protectorate is a *profession*. Governments worldwide are drifting toward cape-control legislation.

**Went active:** 2008. Three nations have signed the Protocol since. The US hasn't, and every Endbringer attack makes the next refusal harder.

**The question nobody asks:** what happens to the prisoners.

## Where it's heading

Every Endbringer attack makes the Reciprocal Response Protocol harder to refuse. After Leviathan's Harbingers in May, the pressure on the US to sign spikes.

## Uber and Leet and the Yangban

The Yangban's payment is parahuman prisoners. A villain-by-reputation Tinker with a very strange power is exactly the kind of prisoner a government might hand over.
