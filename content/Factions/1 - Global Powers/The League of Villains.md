## Overview

The shadow counterpart to the PRT that the CYOA modifier calls for: *a council of parahumans, the worst of the worst, each having connections or power or both.*

**Services:** resources, protection, influence, black-market tinkertech, trafficking, arms, political manipulation.
**Actual product:** **powers.** [[All For One]] grants them as patronage, and a villain who accepts his gift is bound to him in a way money never manages. Enforcement: he can take them back. No court required.

**Council seats:** All For One (chair) · [[Tomura Shigaraki|Shigaraki]] (heir) · [[Gesellschaft]] · [[The Elite]] · [[The Merchants]] (logistics arm).

Not a monolith: a coordination body for people who despise each other. Every member knows AFO's generosity is farming, and takes the gift anyway.

## In Brockton Bay

[[The Merchants]] hold the League's logistics seat. [[Kaiser]] answers to [[Gesellschaft]], which holds another. The Bay's villains are closer to All For One than they know.

## Where it's heading

The League needs the PRT to survive as a stable adversary. When the classification system collapses in late 2011, the League's managed equilibrium goes with it.

## Uber and Leet and the League

**Power-granting tech is allowed.** A Tinker who can build Compound V, the Ray Sphere or esper development is a **rival power-broker**, the one thing All For One escalates against.
