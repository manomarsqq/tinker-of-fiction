## Overview

US federal agency (the **PRT**) plus its cape wing (the **Protectorate**), with the **Wards** as the youth program.

**What holds it together:** the **Brute floor**. Every parahuman shrugs off small-arms fire, so only PRT foam and specialist munitions work on capes. A city without PRT coverage has no rule of law. Add federal money and the fiction that someone is in charge.

**Five pressures pulling it apart**

| Pressure | Effect |
|---|---|
| Recruitment | Vought pays more and offers fame. Sky High offers independence. Gate extraction will offer money. *(Competition, not crisis: Cauldron funds non-PRT teams worldwide by design.)* |
| Classification | Its database is full of Flame users, Mimics and expies filed as capes. Errors it structurally cannot see |
| Endbringers | Untethered means no off-switch. Harbingers make attacks deadlier. Lessers make 40% of attacks *winnable*, which is the trap |
| [[The Yangban]] | A rival model that demonstrably works |
| Its own head | Chief Director [[Alexandria\|Costa-Brown]] is Alexandria is [[Cauldron]] |

**Internal factions**
- **The Costa-Brown line:** Cauldron-aligned, invisible. Stabilize the institution, accept losses.
- **The hardliners:** Tagg-type. Capes are a weapons problem. Quietly sympathetic to the Yangban and would deny it.
- **The reformers:** [[Emily Piggot|Piggot]]-adjacent. The system is failing and needs restructuring.
- **The careerists:** the majority. Protect budget and image. These are the people who protect [[Emma Barnes|Emma]] and [[Madison Clements|Madison]].

**Simmering tension:** the Wards program is legally indefensible: child soldiers with a PR department. Now that Sky High and Vought exist, the kids still in the Wards are the ones who had *no other option*. That is precisely [[Sophia Hess|Sophia]].

**Corruption** is image management, not bribery. Every individual decision is defensible. [[Coil]] being a PRT consultant with a private army is the disease in one man.

## Structure

- **The PRT**: the federal agency. Directors per region, troopers, analysts, lawyers.
- **The Protectorate**: the cape wing, one team per region.
- **The Wards**: the youth program, now competing with Sky High and Vought for every kid.
- **Chief Director:** [[Alexandria|Rebecca Costa-Brown]].
