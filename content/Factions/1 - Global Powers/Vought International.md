## Overview

**The pitch:** the Protectorate is a failing government program; private supes are the mature answer.

**Product:** supes made with **Compound V**, marketed as entertainment, sold as a defense product. Media rights, merchandising, film, endorsements, defense contracting. One of the largest companies on Earth. Compound V is buried in R&D as a cost centre.

**Its century:** Compound V was developed in the **1940s–50s**. In 1982 [[Scion]] flooded the market with people who got powers *for free*. Everything Vought has done since (the entertainment pivot, the lobbying, the tax filing) is the strategy of a disrupted incumbent that never said so out loud. Vought and [[Cauldron]] are mirror images, and neither knows the other exists.

**The Enhanced Individual carve-out** (~2003): four pages, filed as a **tax matter**, defining Vought's people as "enhanced individuals" under employment law rather than parahumans. Nobody at the PRT read it. It is the most consequential legal document in the US.

**The bill in the drawer:** a *hazardous anomaly extraction* framework, drafted for Endbringer attack-site salvage and Simurgh quarantine asset recovery. Passes in **August 2011**, amended to cover "dimensional anomalies".

**Hidden edge:** shardless supes are immune to shard-based Thinkers and Masters. Vought doesn't fully understand why.

**Brockton Bay:** no branch. Scouting, plus one marketed hero on station, [[Mainstay]].

## Roster

## Where it's heading

- **15 May 2011:** if Vought's medical team is overwhelmed at Leviathan and a supe ends up in a Protectorate triage bay, the corona loophole is exposed.
- **August 2011:** the salvage bill passes. Vought is the incumbent contractor for Gate extraction.
- **2012:** Nemesis makes the bill look like prophecy.

## Uber and Leet and Vought

Leet has read [[The Boys]]. He knows what Compound V is, and it's on the Catalog. He's one of maybe a dozen people on Earth who could expose Vought, and he could **build** Compound V himself.
