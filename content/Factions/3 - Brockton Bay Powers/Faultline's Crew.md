## Overview

Mercenaries for hire, led by **Faultline**, based out of the Palanquin nightclub. Most of the crew are **Case 53s**: Cauldron's failures, monstrous and memory-wiped. Faultline takes the jobs and is quietly looking for the truth about where her people came from.

When licensed Gate extraction passes in **August 2011** they go legitimate overnight: first movers alongside [[Toybox]].

## Roster

- **Faultline**: Shaker. Destroys the structural integrity of anything she touches.
- **Labyrinth**: Shaker. Reshapes the environment into a maze, at the cost of her grip on reality.
- **Newter**: Case 53. Hallucinogenic skin. Wall-crawler.
- **Gregor the Snail**: Case 53. Shell-bodied, sprays acidic and adhesive fluids.
- **Spitfire**: Case 53. Breathes fire.

## Assets

- **The Palanquin**, a nightclub and base.
- **Contracts** with every side. Professional reputation.

## Operations

Jobs for money, done professionally, no questions about politics. They take work from anyone who pays, including villains, and they don't cross lines that bring the Protectorate down on them.
