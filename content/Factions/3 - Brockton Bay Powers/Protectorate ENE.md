## Overview

The Protectorate's East-North-East branch, based on [[The Rig]] in the bay, with PRT ENE's headquarters downtown. It's the only thing in Brockton Bay that can legally arrest a cape, and one of very few things that physically can.

Kaiser, Lung and Armsmaster have stood in the same mud at Endbringer fights more often than they've fought each other. That's why the unwritten rules hold.
