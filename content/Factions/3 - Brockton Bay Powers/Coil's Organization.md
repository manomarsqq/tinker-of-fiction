## Overview

[[Coil]] (Thomas Calvert): PRT consultant, mercenary contracts, a private army. Owns [[The Undersiders]]. Funds [[Redline]] for deniable courier work. **Takes [[Dinah Alcott]] in May.**

It's the most dangerous organisation in Brockton Bay, and it doesn't officially exist. The PRT has no file on it, because the man who'd write the file is Coil.

## History

Calvert was a soldier, then a PRT consultant, then a man who realised that the Bay's gang equilibrium was a market with no dominant player. He's spent years buying mercenaries, property, officials and capes. The Undersiders were a purchase. Tattletale was acquired.

## Roster & assets

- **[[Coil]]**: the boss, never in the room.
- **Mercenaries:** a private army of ex-military contractors with Tinkertech weapons.
- **[[The Undersiders]]**: Grue, [[Tattletale]], Regent, Bitch. Funded; unaware how owned.
- **[[Redline]]** and [[Checkpoint]]: on the payroll for deniable courier work.
- **The base:** an underground facility beneath the city, reached through fronts.
- **The PRT consultancy:** inside knowledge of every operation against the gangs.
- **Property** across the city, bought quietly.

## Operations

Buying everything. Coil doesn't fight the Empire or the ABB; he waits for them to weaken each other and positions himself to inherit. Every major move is **tested in a discarded timeline first**.
