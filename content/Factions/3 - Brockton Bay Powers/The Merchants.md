## Overview

**The Merchant Markets** *(modifier)*: the premier parahuman black market on the East Coast.

Legitimate shipping is dead worldwide, so **mobile illegitimate logistics is worth more than the cargo**. [[Squealer]]'s flying warehouses, armoured convoys, stealth container ships and submarines are the only reliable freight network on the East Coast that answers to nobody.

- **Product:** not drugs. The ability to move anything, anywhere, unobserved.
- **Revenue:** transit fees, brokerage, escrow on other people's business.
- **Clients:** gangs, corporations, and almost certainly a government or two through cut-outs.
- **Scale:** out-earns Empire 88 and the ABB **combined** in liquid, deployable money.
- **PRT view:** a drug gang. Classification trap, one more time.

[[Skidmark]]'s profanity is the camouflage. The routing decisions are sophisticated; nobody hears them past the third word.

**League seat:** the logistics arm of [[The League of Villains]]. **Subcontractors:** [[The Ferrymen]] (short-range water) · [[The Undertow]] (fed quietly).

## Where it's heading

**15 May 2011:** Leviathan hits the waterfront. The Boat Graveyard is Merchant ground. **August 2011:** the salvage bill, and the Merchants are the only ones who can move salvage.

## Uber and Leet and the Merchants

The one organisation in the Bay that can **move anything anywhere**, including Leet's builds and the exotic materials he needs. Best customer or biggest risk.
