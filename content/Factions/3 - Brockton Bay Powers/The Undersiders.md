## Overview

Grue, [[Tattletale]], Regent, Bitch. On [[Coil]]'s payroll. Their targeted robberies are really information operations.

## Roster

- **Grue** (Brian Laborn): leader. Darkness that swallows light and sound.
- **[[Tattletale]]** (Lisa Wilbourn): Thinker.
- **Regent** (Alec): nervous-system control. Heartbreaker's son.
- **Bitch** (Rachel Lindt): grows dogs into monsters.

## Operations

Robberies, heists, jobs. Coil picks the targets and pays well. The team doesn't know he owns them.
