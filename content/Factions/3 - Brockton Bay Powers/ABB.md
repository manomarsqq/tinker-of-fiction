## Overview

The **Azn Bad Boys**: [[Lung]] and Oni Lee, a tiny roster with enormous individual power. **Bakuda arrives in May** and gives Lung the ability to level city blocks.

**What the ABB offers the diaspora** isn't money. It's dignity and protection for people the city has written off, from a man unambiguously stronger than anything the city can send. Pretending that's nothing is why the PRT keeps losing recruits to him.

## History

Kyushu sank in **1999**, and millions of displaced people spread into dead port districts worldwide. Brockton Bay's Docks already had an Asian-American community; the diaspora doubled it. No port, no jobs, and no fishing, because the ocean now kills fishermen.

Lung took the Docks by force and held them by keeping his word. The ABB grew out of that: a protection racket that the people inside it think of as protection.

## Roster

- **[[Lung]]**: the warlord. Escalating Brute/Blaster/Changer, a dragon given time.
- **Oni Lee**: teleporting self-duplicator who leaves exploding copies. Hollow, obedient, terrifying.
- **Bakuda** *(from May)*: bomb Tinker, recruited by force, unstable. Her arrival turns the ABB from a gang into a threat to the whole city.
- **Hundreds of unpowered members**, at or above peak human like every Earth Bet gang.

## Territory & money

- **Territory:** [[The Docks]], the waterfront, parts of downtown.
- **Money:** protection, trafficking, gambling, drugs. Lung takes a cut of everything moving through the Docks.
- **Recruiting:** Kyushu-diaspora youth, and the pre-existing community. Sometimes willingly.

## 8 April 2011 and after

Holding the Docks. In canon, Lung loses a fight to Taylor tonight, and the ABB's slide begins: Bakuda's bombing campaign, the Undersiders' raid, then Leviathan.

**Here:** Leviathan's Harbingers hit the waterfront hardest on **15 May**. The Docks are ABB territory. The diaspora is about to be displaced a second time.

## Uber and Leet and the ABB

Any conflict with the ABB is **parahuman-heavy**: big points, big risk. Bakuda is a Tinker too, and a very unstable one. A Tinker of Fiction with bomb-disposal tech from the Catalog would be very popular in May.
