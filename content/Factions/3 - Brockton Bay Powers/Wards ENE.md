## Overview

The youth wing of the Protectorate in Brockton Bay. Child soldiers with a PR department.

**The Parahuman Three** *(modifier, confirmed)*: [[Sophia Hess|Sophia]], [[Emma Barnes|Emma]] and [[Madison Clements|Madison]] are all Wards with **enhanced legal protections** and roughly equal, deliberately low ratings. PR assets, not combat assets. The protection shields the PRT's image, not the city.

Their records are sealed under the **[[03 - Law and Institutions#The Ward Protection Doctrine|Ward Protection Doctrine]]**, which is why [[Danny Hebert]] can't subpoena anything about what happened to [[Taylor Hebert|his daughter]].

## Pay

A Ward accrues **$50,000 a year in trust** plus minimum wage; the trust doubles on full membership and vests at eighteen. Sophia has been banking that while tormenting a classmate.

## Why it's rotten

Now that Sky High and Vought exist, the only kids left in the Wards are the ones who had **no other option**. That's precisely Sophia. And the Doctrine seals the program even from [[Emily Piggot|Piggot]].
