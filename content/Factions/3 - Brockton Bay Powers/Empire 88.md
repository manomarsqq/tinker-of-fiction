## Overview

The largest cape roster in Brockton Bay. [[Kaiser]] (Max Anders, CEO of **Medhall**) runs it as a dynasty with a corporate face. **Purity recently split off.**

It draws on real **deindustrialisation grievance** in the white working class, which makes Kaiser dangerous rather than merely repellent. Financially it's the most legitimate gang in the city: Medhall, protection, drugs, labour racketeering.

## History

Founded by Allfather, Kaiser's father, as the American arm of [[Gesellschaft]]. When Allfather died, Max Anders took over and remade it: fewer rallies, more boardrooms. The Empire's capes are old families, recruited converts and Gesellschaft transfers.

## Roster

- **[[Kaiser]]**: leader. Metal blades from any surface.
- **Hookwolf**: a Changer who becomes a mass of spinning blades. The most violent lieutenant.
- **Stormtiger**, **Cricket**: Hookwolf's crew. Air-blasts; sonar and close-quarters.
- **Krieg**: kinetic manipulation, second-in-command.
- **Fenja and Menja**: giantess twins, Gesellschaft transfers.
- **Night and Fog**: Gesellschaft-trained, a silent killing pair.
- **Victor** (skill thief) and **Othala** (power granting, temporary).
- **Rune**: telekinesis of large objects.
- **Alabaster**: resets to an earlier state every few seconds.
- **Purity** *(split off)*: a light Blaster, now independent with Crusader and others.

## Territory & money

- **Territory:** [[North End]], and the working-class neighbourhoods around it.
- **Money:** [[Medhall Building|Medhall]], protection, drugs, labour racketeering.
- **Client gang:** [[The Hollowmen]], doing the work Kaiser's capes can't be photographed doing.

## 8 April 2011 and after

The strongest single faction in the Bay. **Coming:** Leviathan on 15 May, then the Gates. The salvage bill (August 2011) is a legal economy Medhall would want.

## Uber and Leet and the Empire

The Empire's capes shop on the **Boardwalk** too, under the Concord's neutrality. Any fight with them is parahuman-heavy and pays well. It also invites Hookwolf.
