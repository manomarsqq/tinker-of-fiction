## Overview

Independent heroes, **unmasked**. They believe capes should be public and accountable, and they've paid for it. Includes [[Panacea]], who keeps [[All Might]] at full strength after every Endbringer fight.

## History

New Wave grew out of an older team, Brandish and Lady Photon's generation. They unmasked to show capes could be accountable. Then a villain murdered one of their own (Fleur), and the experiment became a cautionary tale they refused to abandon.
