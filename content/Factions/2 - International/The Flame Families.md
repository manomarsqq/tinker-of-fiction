## Overview

Mafia bloodlines (*Katekyo Hitman Reborn*) that have hidden **Dying Will Flames** for generations. Anyone caught using one is coached to claim a parahuman power: *"Say cape."* The PRT database contains a category of misfiled Blasters and Strikers.

**The tell is anatomical:** Flame users have no corona pollentia. One thorough scan exposes them, so the families control their people's medical access absolutely. *"A doctor you didn't choose is an enemy you haven't met."* One hospital visit is a security incident.

**Culture:** the ring given at coming of age · children drilled on the lie from childhood · never light where a camera might be · never use the English word for it.

**Legal status:** invisible. If exposed they claim cape status, which *surrenders* that invisibility. The families accept the known evil over the investigation an unknown category would invite.

## Who they are

Dying Will Flames come in seven Sky attributes (Sky, Storm, Rain, Sun, Lightning, Cloud, Mist), channelled through rings and boxes. The families (*Katekyo Hitman Reborn* expies) keep them hidden inside mafia structures worldwide.

## Where it's heading

Any serious scan exposes them. The Legions reveal and Tithonus's truths in 2012–2013 put every hidden population at risk.

## Uber and Leet and the Flame Families

Leet knows KHR. He knows what "say cape" hides. That's knowledge the families would kill to keep.
