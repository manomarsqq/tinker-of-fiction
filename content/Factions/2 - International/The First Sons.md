## Overview

A small private group researching how parahumans came to be and how to make more. Has **Ray Field** research and exactly **one** activated Conduit: its leader, [[Kessler]].

Kessler is from a future the Beast destroyed (*The March*, Infamous). He is building the **Ray Sphere**.

**Cauldron knows** the First Sons exist and is letting them cook to see what they develop. Two secret organizations preparing for two different apocalypses. One is watching the other. Neither knows the other's apocalypse is real.

> [!workshop] Catalog overlap
> *Infamous* is on the [[Catalog/index|The Catalog]]. The MC's power could know the Ray Sphere better than Kessler does. See [[Infamous]].

## Where it's heading

The March is a fuse. No plague, no Beast yet. Kessler is still building.

## Uber and Leet and the First Sons

[[Infamous]] is on the Catalog. Leet could know the Ray Sphere better than Kessler does, and knows how the Infamous story ends. Building a Ray Sphere is power-granting tech, with the worst possible cost.
