## Overview

Japan's national hero academy. Founded after **Kyushu sank in 1999**, built around [[All Might]], as much national reconstruction as a school. Foreign, with its own doctrine, and strange to American eyes.

It draws from the small, isolated **Quirk lineages** (hereditary powers form family lines, like the Flame families; there is no Quirk society), plus Sky High-style hereditary supes. That's why it's small.

**Expy Galore:** MHA characters exist on Earth Bet **with their native powers** *(user override)*. Shardless, so outside [[Cauldron]]'s and [[Scion]]'s sight.
