## Overview

An **intergalactic police force** with a secret Earth branch. For decades the Plumbers have handled aliens on Earth: refugees, criminals, smugglers, invaders. They dress like plumbers, work out of hidden bases, and have never once been verified by Earth Bet. *(ruling: hidden, like the other expies)*

This keeps the world's **first-contact rule** intact. There are aliens on Earth Bet, and a police force for them, and institutional knowledge is still zero.

## History

The Plumbers go back a long way on Earth. Max Tennyson was one of their best. By 2011 the Earth branch is thin: its veterans are ageing or missing, and the Highbreed's DNAliens are infiltrating it. **Plumbers' kids** (half-alien children of Plumbers) are the next generation, and most of them don't know it yet.
