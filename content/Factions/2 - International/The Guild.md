## Overview

International hero team built around [[Dragon]]. In this world its importance is infrastructural: the PRT's containment foam, armoury and the [[The Birdcage|Birdcage]] all route through Dragon. **There is no redundancy.**

Funded in part by [[Cauldron]], which wants a broad, heterogeneous cape ecosystem, not a PRT monopoly.
