## Overview

European cape fascism. A German-rooted organisation that grew out of post-war nationalist movements and became a transnational network of capes. Kaiser's [[Empire 88]] answers upward to them, in canon and here. Holds a seat on the [[The League of Villains|League]] council.

## History

Gesellschaft has existed in some form since before the cape era, and it thrived after it. It recruits capes, and when they won't come, it takes them: kidnapping, conditioning, Master effects. It sends trained capes abroad to support its daughter organisations, including Empire 88.

## Assets

- **Capes**, trained and conditioned.
- **Money** from legitimate fronts across Europe.
- **A League seat**, and through it, All For One's patronage.
