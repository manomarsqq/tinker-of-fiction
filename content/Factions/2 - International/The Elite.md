## Overview

Professional cape crime on the American West Coast. The Elite run their criminal operations like a business: contracts, reputation, customer service. Expanding. Holds a seat on the [[The League of Villains|League]] council.

## History

The Elite grew out of cape villains who wanted money more than territory. They offer services (protection, heists, enforcement) to anyone who pays, and they've been expanding east for years.

## Assets

- **Capes**, many of them.
- **Money and legal fronts** across the West.
- **A League seat.**
