## Overview

Thousands of them. Typical cult: 7–200 members; super-cults in the thousands exist. Unusually devoted, and disproportionately likely to develop **techniques that actually work**. Mostly benign. Far too many not. Larger cults splinter.

## Doctrine

Powers **mean** something. A gift implies a giver, a giver implies intent, intent implies obligation. **Heresy:** saying a power is random.

This is one of Earth Bet's working explanations. It works for millions of people, and like the PRT's taxonomy, it's never falsified.

## "The Blessed"

Not a power category *(ruling)*. It's what the empowered get called once the [[Beasts of Entropy]] arrive and "cape" stops being the dominant word. In Brockton Bay it starts at [[Saint Anne's]].

## Examples

- **[[Saint Anne's]]**: mutual aid drifting toward certainty.
- **[[The Fallen]]**: Endbringer worship.
- Three separate cults courting [[The Ninety-Nine]].
- Countless small groups with meditation, rituals or diets that sometimes work.

## 8 April 2011 and after

**Coming:** the Beasts of Entropy (late 2011) make the cults explode.

## Uber and Leet and the cults

A Tinker who builds things out of stories is a cult's dream. A catalog of magic-adjacent powers would look like a religion.
