## Overview

A few family lines and a handful of survivors with a phone tree. Not an organisation anymore, a rumour. What's left of the **sorcerer tradition**.

## History

**Before 1982**, a child who started seeing things would eventually find them and be taught to survive **Curses**. **After 1982** that child thinks they **triggered**, goes to the PRT, gets filed as an unreliable Thinker with perceptual side effects, and is dead within months.

The network knows, and it can't compete with a federal agency that has a television show. See [[Curses]] and [[02 - Power Systems#Cursed energy|Cursed energy]].

## Assets

- **A phone tree.**
