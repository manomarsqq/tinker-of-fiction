## Overview

A rogue Tinker collective. They sell Tinkertech, repair it, and trade in the parts. When Gate extraction is licensed (August 2011), rogues become economically central, and Toybox is a first mover.

## Operations

- **Selling Tinkertech** to anyone.
- **Repairs**, for Tinkers who can't maintain their own.
- **Parts trading.**
