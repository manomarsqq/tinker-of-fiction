## Overview

Endbringer worshippers. Rural American clans (the canon Mathers, Valefor and Vasil lines) who treat the Endbringers as divine and their attacks as sacraments. **Thriving**: there are about to be eight of them to worship.

## History

The Fallen formed around the Endbringers' first attacks, and grew into a network of extended families with capes. They kidnap, recruit and breed capes, and they show up after Endbringer attacks to spread their message.

## Assets

- **Capes**, bred and recruited.
- **Rural compounds.**
- **Doctrine** that grows stronger every time an Endbringer attacks.
