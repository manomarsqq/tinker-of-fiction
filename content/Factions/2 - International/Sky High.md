## Overview

The private alternative to the Wards: an American superhero school on a floating campus, for parents who won't hand a child to a government agency. It draws on hereditary supes (Expy Galore). Its powers are **shardless**, so its students aren't legally parahuman and don't show up in shard data.

## History

Founded by hereditary superhero families (the Sky High lineage) long before the cape era, as a school for their own children. After 1982 it opened its doors wider and became the prestige choice for families with means.

## Assets

- **Legal status:** its students aren't parahumans under the law.
