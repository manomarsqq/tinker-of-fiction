# Lord Zedd

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_lord-zedd.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: INVASION
> **Status:** Hidden
> **Debut:** 2011-10
> **Rating:** S
> **Source:** Power Rangers

## What it is

Emperor of Evil. His castle is on [[Zedd's Moon Castle|the moon]]. Earth is connected to the **Morphing Grid**; nobody has tapped it. Zordon knows.

## What people think it is

Nothing, until October.

## Counter

Power Rangers, once Zordon makes them (**2012-Q1/Q2**): shardless teenagers with no corona and no registration.

## When it surfaces

**October 2011: Zedd's first strike.** The classification system fails irreversibly.
