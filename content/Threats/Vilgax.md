# Vilgax

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_vilgax.png|cover hmed]]

## What it is

**Vilgax**, a Chimera Sui Generis warlord who has conquered worlds and wants the **Omnitrix** above everything, because with it he could command an army of any species. He's fought [[Ben Tennyson]] before and lost, and he's patient.

- **Strength and durability** far past a Brute's, plus alien tech and bioids (drone soldiers).
- **A ship and an army.**
- **Shardless.**

## What people think it is

Nothing. Nobody on Earth Bet knows he exists, except the Plumbers and the Tennysons.

## Counter

Ben and the Omnitrix. Plumber tech. Anything on the [[Ben 10]] catalog.

## When it surfaces

**Held in reserve** *(unplaced)*. When Vilgax comes to Earth in force, it's an alien invasion on live television, and the first-contact rule dies.

> [!workshop] Catalog overlap
> **[[Ben 10]] is on the Catalog.** Leet could build Plumber-grade weapons against him, or a rival Omnitrix, which would make Leet Vilgax's target too.
