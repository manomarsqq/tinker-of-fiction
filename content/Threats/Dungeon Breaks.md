# Dungeon Breaks

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_dungeon-breaks.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: DIMENSIONAL
> **Status:** Hidden
> **Debut:** 2011-05-02
> **Rating:** Varies
> **Source:** Worm CYOA

## What it is

When a Gate destabilizes, its contents spill out. See [[The Gate-Realms]]. **Shard Dungeons** *(modifier)* also exist: massive underground dungeons maintained by a caste of shards, full of monsters whose remains are Tinkertech-grade and power-enhancing.

## What people think it is

A Master with constructs. A Shaker event. An unregistered tinker.

## Counter

Clear every inhabitant and the Gate disperses harmlessly.

## When it surfaces

**2 May 2011:** the first public Gate opens under [[Winslow High School]] ([[14 - The Powder Keg|the Powder Keg]]). **July 2011:** a second, then a third. By the third, the framework fails in public. **August:** licensed extraction.
