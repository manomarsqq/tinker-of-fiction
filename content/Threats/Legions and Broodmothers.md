# Legions and Broodmothers

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_legions-and-broodmothers.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: ENDBRINGER SPAWN
> **Status:** Hidden
> **Debut:** 2012-Q2
> **Rating:** S (collective)
> **Source:** Worm CYOA

## What it is

Legions breed in **nurseries** inside Gate-realms and the deep Labyrinthos, garrisoned by Harbingers. An Endbringer descends, seeds a nursery, and returns to wandering. They emerge through Gates: anywhere, no cycle, no warning.

**Broodmothers of the Apocalypse** *(addon)*: Legions act in the Endbringers' stead, like eusocial insects. Nests of alien design, defended with the Endbringer at the centre. Nukes aren't enough to clear them. They tried.

Nurseries can be found (hard) and destroyed, which genuinely reduces Legion output and doesn't trip the adaptation rule.

## What people think it is

The Gate problem and the Endbringer problem are two separate problems.

## Counter

Find and destroy nurseries.

## When it surfaces

**2012-Q2: THE FULCRUM.** A nursery empties into a city. **The hope bubble pops.** Markets crash.
