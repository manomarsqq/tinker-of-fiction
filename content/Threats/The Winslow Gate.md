# The Winslow Gate

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_the-winslow-gate.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: DIMENSIONAL
> **Status:** Imminent
> **Debut:** 2011-05-02 *(proposed date)*
> **Rating:** S
> **Source:** Original *(user ruling)*

## What it is

**The Powder Keg.** A brawl in [[Winslow High School]]'s cafeteria turns into open, powers-out fighting between students, with no regard for the unwritten rules. The clash tears open the thin spot beneath the school, and **the world's first public Gate** drops the entire school into **[[The Labyrinthos]]**. The students fight their way out. **Every one of them comes out with powers**: some triggered (shard), some changed by the Labyrinthos (shardless). The building is gone. The entrance never closes.

## What people think it is

A **Shaker event** and a **mass trigger**. The PRT files the Labyrinthos as "a Shaker construct of unknown origin" and registers the survivors as ordinary capes, shardless ones included.

## Counter

None. It's not something you fight; it's something you survive, and then live next to. The crater is a permanent Labyrinthos entrance in the middle of Brockton Bay.

## When it surfaces

**May 2011, before Leviathan** *(proposed: Monday 2 May, the day before the Civil Defense Ordinance vote)*. It turns a city one issue away from civil war into a city at war. Leviathan arrives thirteen days later.
