# Leviathan

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_leviathan.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: ENDBRINGER
> **Status:** Active
> **Debut:** 1996
> **Rating:** S
> **Source:** Worm

## What it is

Sank Kyushu (1999) and Newfoundland (2005). Lair: ocean depths. One reason the oceans are dying as a transit medium.

## What people think it is

One of three Endbringers, attacking on a rotation.

## Counter

Canon doctrine, which has five weeks to live at the anchor date.

## When it surfaces

**15 May 2011, Brockton Bay. The Harbingers debut.** Every cape on that beach arrives expecting the fight they've trained thirty years for.
