# The Forever Knights

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_forever-knights.png|cover hmed]]

## What it is

A secret society founded in medieval Europe that has spent centuries hunting aliens and **hoarding their technology**. They wear armour, use energy lances, and have castles and money on several continents. They're street-level by Ben 10 standards and well funded by anyone else's.

- **Alien tech arsenals.**
- **Knights in powered armour.**
- **Old money.**

## What people think it is

A rich eccentrics' club, if anyone thinks of them at all. Their incidents get filed as Tinker crime.

## Counter

[[The Plumbers]]. Anyone they try to rob.

## When it surfaces

**Active now.** In Brockton Bay terms they're a buyer, seller and thief of exactly what Leet builds.

> [!workshop] Relevance to Leet
> The Forever Knights collect alien tech. A Tinker who can build it from a catalog is either a supplier or a target. **Kevin 11** was a villain once too; in the Alien Force era he's reformed and on Ben's team.
