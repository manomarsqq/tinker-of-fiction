# Harbingers

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_harbingers.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: ENDBRINGER SPAWN
> **Status:** Unknown
> **Debut:** 2011-05-15
> **Rating:** A+
> **Source:** Worm CYOA

## What it is

Entities a quarter to half the size of their Endbringer, each with Typhon and scaled-down versions of its powers. With **Broodmothers of the Apocalypse**, they're the elite units of the Legions and the guards of the nests: 3–12 per attack, far more around the Endbringer itself.

## What people think it is

Nobody has ever seen one.

## Counter

No threat rating, no playbook.

## When it surfaces

**15 May 2011.** In ninety minutes the world learns thirty years of practice just expired.
