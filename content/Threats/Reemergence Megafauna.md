## What it is

*The Reemergence*: Paleozoic-to-Pliocene fauna from chasms worldwide: Megalodon, Livyatan and Dunkleosteus at sea; T. rex, Smilodon and dire wolves on land; Quetzalcoatlus in the air. Ambivalent to hostile, and often unlike their hypothetical originals. **One of the three reasons the oceans are dying.**
