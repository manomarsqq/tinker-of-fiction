# The Ten Blasphemies

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_the-ten-blasphemies.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: S-CLASS
> **Status:** Active
> **Debut:** 1994
> **Rating:** S
> **Source:** Worm CYOA

## What it is

*Mockery of the Divine*: European tinkers finished all ten before heroes could stop them. They withstand Eidolon and reform if even one survives. Europe has quietly stopped pretending it polices where they walk.

## What people think it is

Unkillable murder machines.

## Counter

Containment and evacuation only. Pattern analysis of **what they take rather than what they destroy** would reveal the plan.

## When it surfaces

Unknown, and near. The only tension with a deadline nobody can see.
