# The Supernatural

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_the-supernatural.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: HIDDEN
> **Status:** Active
> **Debut:** Always
> **Rating:** 1–5 (rarely 6–7)
> **Source:** Worm CYOA

## What it is

**Horror Bet:** vampires in Transylvania, a shapeshifter in Antarctica, a blob in Pennsylvania, vengeful ghosts, rare zombie outbreaks, mad scientists, the occasional slasher. All have weaknesses.
**Supernatural Files:** ghosts, poltergeists, demons, werewolves, vampires, wendigos, tulpas, golems, yokai, banshees, fae, changelings, skinwalkers, ghouls. Anomalous and cursed items. Legendary artefacts: Excalibur, Gram, the Eye of Ra, the Holy Grail, the Ark, the Shroud.
**Dante the Supreme Traveler:** the Three Kingdoms of Dante's afterlife exist.

## What people think it is

Unverified. Career-ending to report.

## Counter

Voodoo, witchcraft, kabbalah, paganism, exorcists, monster hunters, and underfunded specialist government divisions. **Useless against capes, Endbringers and Curses.**

## When it surfaces

Now, at the fringe. First contact has not happened.
