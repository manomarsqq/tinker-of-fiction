## What it is

Non-human creatures that perfectly mimic humans. Alien morality, hostile, have fed on people. *Mimics Rise.*

## What people think it is

A Changer or Breaker cape, or a Case 53. Small anti-Mimic groups exist and are read as conspiracy theorists.

## Counter

Occult practitioners can identify and kill them.

## When it surfaces

Now. Legally, they don't exist.
