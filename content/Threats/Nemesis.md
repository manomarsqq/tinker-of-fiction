# Nemesis

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_nemesis.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: ENDBRINGER
> **Status:** Charged
> **Debut:** 2012-Q1
> **Rating:** S
> **Source:** Worm CYOA

## What it is

**Lair:** The Caucasus mountains.

**Directive:** Kill parahumans, seed anti-parahuman materials, provoke arms races. **The Powerkiller**.

Her **spore deposits** (radiation that forces Breakers into true form, humming metals that cause Thinker headaches, liquids Shaker effects can't cross) are the only material that lets an ordinary government reliably hurt a parahuman. They grow and regenerate if not cleared. She arrives precisely when the world has decided capes are the answer to everything.

## What people think it is

Doesn't exist. Nobody has seen it.

## Counter

Unknown. No doctrine exists.

## When it surfaces

Surfaces **2012-Q1**.
