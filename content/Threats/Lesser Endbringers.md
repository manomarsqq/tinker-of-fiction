# Lesser Endbringers

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_lesser-endbringers.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: ENDBRINGER SPAWN
> **Status:** Unknown
> **Debut:** 2011-06
> **Rating:** A
> **Source:** Worm CYOA

## What it is

At least one per active Endbringer. Wander, no core, no lair, an order of magnitude weaker. **Genuinely killable.** A new one appears when one dies. Once they exist, 40% of attacks are a Lesser instead of the real thing.

## What people think it is

Don't exist yet.

## Counter

**Killing one grants NO protection against the full Endbringers** *(ruling)*. It's simply a win: clean, real, unpoisoned. The formal Truce covers them too.

## When it surfaces

**June 2011, and somebody kills it.** Three weeks after the worst day in Endbringer history. The hope bubble inflates to bursting.
