## What it is

The **Highbreed** believe they're the only pure species in the universe, and they're dying of their own inbreeding. Their plan is to exterminate every other species. On Earth they work through **DNAliens**: humans converted by a Xenocyte parasite into alien servants, disguised as people.

- **DNAliens:** converted humans, disguised, spreading.
- **Xenocytes:** the parasites that do the converting.
- **Highbreed warriors:** tall, strong, and contemptuous.
- **The fleet:** waiting for Earth to be ready.

## What people think it is

Nothing. The DNAliens are disguised. Their incidents get filed as unregistered Masters and missing persons.

## Counter

[[Ben Tennyson]], Gwen, Kevin and [[The Plumbers]]. In canon, Ben ends the war with Azmuth's help and the Omnitrix's genetic repair.
