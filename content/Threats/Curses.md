# Curses

> [!infobox]
> ![[t_curses.png|cover hmed]]

> [!warning]+ Threat file: HIDDEN
> **Status:** Active
> **Debut:** Always
> **Rating:** 1–special
> **Source:** Jujutsu Kaisen

## What it is

Invisible entities born of concentrated negative human emotion (*Jujutsu Kaisen*). Densest at schools, hospitals and graveyards. **Brockton Bay is one of the densest hotspots in North America.** Using cursed energy is a beacon that draws them in.

## What people think it is

An unregistered Master (for any Curse-related death).

## Counter

**Cursed energy is the only thing that harms them.** Exorcised, they don't come back; otherwise they return stronger.

## When it surfaces

Now, invisibly. See [[The Old Network]].
