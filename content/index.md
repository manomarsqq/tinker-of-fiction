**Earth Bet, April 2011.** A self-insert wakes up as **[[Leet]]**, the joke Tinker of Uber and Leet, and finds his dead Eden shard has become a **[[04 - Tinker of Fiction|Tinker of Fiction]]**: he can build technology from any fictional setting, paid for in points he earns from conflict. He runs **[[Retro Rewind]]**, a 90s-style rental store on the Boardwalk, with his partner **[[Uber|Robyn]]**.

Brockton Bay is one issue away from civil war. On 2 May, **[[14 - The Powder Keg|the Powder Keg]]** gives it one.
